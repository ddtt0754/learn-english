/副词的比较级&最高级规则/不规则变化

规则变化
1.直接+er/est fast faster fastest
2. e 结尾，+r/ st late later latest
3.辅音字母+y 结尾，改y 为i 加er/est busy busier busiest
4.重读闭音节，且词尾只有一个辅音字母，双写尾
字母，再加er/est		 big bigger biggest
5.多音节和部分双音节，形容词前加more/less careful more careful most careful
不规则变化
两好 good/ well better best 一远 far farther farthest
两坏 bad/ badly worse worst further furthest
两多 many/mush more most 一老 old older oldest
一少 little less least elder eldest
