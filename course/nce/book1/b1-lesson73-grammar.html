🔍 The way to King Street 去国王街的路

新概念英语第一册 Lesson 73 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 1 · Lesson 73（课本印刷页 145-146）· 13 个生词和短语 · 课文 15 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一篇 过去时小故事，同时练习 问路：Can you tell me the way to…? 以及 speak 与 say 的区别。

¶ 1 · 过去时叙述（句 1、3、7、11-12）

句子语法点
Last week Mrs. Mills went to London.不规则 go → went；last week 是过去时标志
Suddenly, she saw a man near a bus stop.suddenly 表突然；see → saw
The man smiled pleasantly.规则动词加 -ed；pleasantly 是副词，修饰 smiled
Then he put his hand into his pocket…put 的过去式与原形同形
…and took out a phrasebook.take → took；take out 拿出来
He opened the book and found a phrase.find → found；open 加 -ed

¶ 2 · 问路句型（句 4-6）

句子语法点
“I can ask him the way,” she said to herself.ask sb. the way = 向某人问路
“Can you tell me the way to King Street, please?”Can you tell me the way to…? 是最常用的问路句
“Excuse me,” she said.Excuse me 是搭话前的礼貌用语

¶ 3 · 理解与语言（句 8-10、15）

句子语法点
He did not understand English!did not + 动词原形；understand → understood
He spoke German.不规则 speak → spoke；讲某种语言用 speak
He was a tourist.tourist = 旅游者
“I do not speak English.”do not + 动词原形；speak 后直接接语言名

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
go to London去伦敦。go → went。
lose one's way迷路。she lost her way。
a bus stop公共汽车站。
Excuse me.对不起（搭话前用）。
the way to King Street去国王街的路。the way to + 地点。
speak English / speak German说英语 / 说德语。
read the phrase slowly慢慢地读那句短语。

三语法核心总结

核心语法
一般过去时叙述
本课生词
13 个
课文句数
15 句
重点内容
问路 + speak / say
  • 过去式不规则要记：go → went，see → saw，take → took，speak → spoke，find → found。
  • speak 后接语言：speak English；say 后接说的内容：say “Excuse me”。
  • the way to + 地点：去某地的路，介词用 to。
