课文对话 · Sensational news! 耸人听闻的新闻！（课本 Lesson 133，印刷页 265-266）
1Have you just made a new film, Miss Marsh?
你刚拍完一部新片子吗，马什小姐？
2Yes, I have.
是的。
3Are you going to make another?
你打算再拍一部吗？
4No, I'm not.
不，不拍了。
5I'm going to retire.
我打算退休。
6I feel very tired.
我觉得很累。
7I don't want to make another film for a long time.
很长时间内我不想再拍片子了。
8Let's buy a newspaper, Liz.
我们买份报纸吧，莉兹。
9Listen to this!
听听这个！
10'Karen Marsh: Sensational /sen'seɪʃənəl/ adj. 爆炸性的，耸人听闻的 News!'
“卡伦·马什：耸人听闻的新闻！”
11By our reporter /rɪ'pɔːtə/ n. 记者, Alan Jones.
本报记者艾伦·琼斯报道。
12Karen Marsh arrived at London Airport today.
卡伦·马什今天抵达伦敦机场。
13She was wearing a blue dress and a mink coat /ˌmɪŋk-'kəʊt/ 貂皮大衣.
她穿着蓝色连衣裙和貂皮大衣。
14She told me she had just made a new film.
她告诉我她刚拍完一部新片子。
15She said she was not going to make another.
她说她不打算再拍了。
16She said she was going to retire.
她说她打算退休。
17She told reporters she felt very tired and didn't want to make another film for a long time.
她告诉记者她很累，很长时间内不想再拍片子。
18I wonder why!
我想知道为什么！
