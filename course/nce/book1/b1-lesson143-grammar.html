一全文逐句语法解读

本课围绕 环境保护，重点是 被动语态（is surrounded / have been asked / have been placed / was covered / will be prosecuted）与定语从句。

¶ 1 · 被动语态（句 1、4、5、9）

句子语法点
which is surrounded by beautiful woods.被动 be + 过去分词，表“被……”
Visitors have been asked to keep the woods clean.现在完成时的被动语态：have been + 过去分词
Litter baskets have been placed under the trees.同上，主语是物，用被动
the ground was covered with pieces of paper.一般过去时的被动语态：was + 过去分词

¶ 2 · 定语从句（句 1、10）

句子语法点
a very old town which is surrounded by…which 引导定语从句，修饰 town
a sign which said…which 指物，在从句中作主语
Anyone who leaves litter in these woods…who 引导定语从句，修饰 anyone

¶ 3 · 一般将来时的被动与数量表达（句 3、8、10）

句子语法点
will be prosecuted!一般将来时的被动语态：will be + 过去分词
hundreds of people come from the cityhundreds of 表“成百上千的”
I counted seven old cars and three old refrigerators.count 表“数、点数”

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句

三语法核心总结

核心语法

  • 被动语态 = be + 过去分词；不同时态下 be 相应变化：is surrounded / have been asked / was covered / will be prosecuted。
  • be covered with 表“被……覆盖”；be surrounded by 表“被……环绕”。
