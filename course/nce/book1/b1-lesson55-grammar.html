🔍 The Sawyer family 索耶一家人

新概念英语第一册 Lesson 55 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 1 · Lesson 55（课本印刷页 109-110）· 11 个生词和短语 · 课文 15 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课用 一般现在时 讲索耶一家一天的固定作息：主语是第三人称单数时动词加 -s / -es，时间状语 in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening / at night 串起全天。

¶ 1 · 一般现在时与第三人称单数（句 1-5）

句子语法点
The Sawyers live at 87 King Street.一般现在时 表经常性动作；主语 The Sawyers 是复数，动词用原形 live
Mr. Sawyer goes to work…主语是第三人称单数 → 动词加 -es：go → goes
Mrs. Sawyer stays at home every day.stay 的 y 前是元音字母 a，直接加 -s；every day 是一般现在时的标志词
Their father takes them to school every day.take → takes；take sb. to… = 送某人去……
She does the housework.do 的第三人称单数是 does；do the housework = 做家务

¶ 2 · 时间状语与频率副词（句 6-13）

句子语法点
She always eats her lunch at noon.at noon 在正午；三餐可用 eat / have + 名词
In the afternoon, she usually sees her friends.in the afternoon 泛指下午；频率副词 usually / often / always 放在实义动词之前
They often drink tea together.together 一起，位置常在句末
In the evening, the children come home from school.in the evening 在晚上；但「在夜里」要说 at night
They arrive home early.arrive + 地点；home 是副词，前面不加 to
He arrives home late.early 与 late 成对，修饰动词放句末

¶ 3 · 并列与收束（句 15）

句子语法点
Mr. Sawyer usually reads his newspaper, but sometimes he and his wife watch television.but 连接两个并列分句；he and his wife 是复数主语 → watch 用原形
…watch television.watch television 看电视；口语中也可说 watch TV

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
live at 87 King Street住在国王街 87 号。The Sawyers live at 87 King Street.
go to work / go to school去上班 / 去上学。注意 go to school 不加 the。
stay at home呆在家里。Mrs. Sawyer stays at home every day.
do the housework做家务。She does the housework.
at noon / in the afternoon / in the evening / at night正午 / 下午 / 晚上 / 夜里，一天的四个时段。
arrive home early / late回来得早 / 晚。They arrive home early.
watch television看电视。sometimes he and his wife watch television.

三语法核心总结

核心时态
一般现在时（第三人称单数加 -s/-es）
本课生词
11 个
课文句数
15 句
重点内容
一天的作息与时间状语
  • 第三人称单数别漏 -s：He go to work. 错，应为 He goes to work.
  • 时间状语：用 in the morning / afternoon / evening，但用 at noon、at night。
  • home 是副词：come home / arrive home，不说 come to home。
  • 本课生词 11 个全部嵌入课文句子，无遗漏。
