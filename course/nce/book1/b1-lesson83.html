课文对话 · Going on holiday 度假（课本 Lesson 83，印刷页 165-166）
1Hello, Sam. Come in.
你好，萨姆。请进。
2Hi, Sam. We're having lunch.
你好，萨姆。我们正在吃午饭。
3Do you want to have lunch with us?
和我们一起吃午饭好吗？
4No, thank you, Tom. I've already /ɔːl'redi/ adv. 已经 had lunch.
不了，谢谢你，汤姆。我已经吃过午饭了。
5I had lunch at half past twelve.
我十二点半吃的午饭。
6Have a cup of coffee then.
那么来杯咖啡吧。
7I've just had a cup, thank you.
我刚喝了一杯，谢谢。
8I had one after my lunch.
我是在午饭后喝的。
9Let's go into the living room, Carol.
我们到客厅去吧，卡罗尔。
10We can have our coffee there.
我们可以在那儿喝咖啡。
11Excuse the mess /mes/ n. 杂乱，凌乱, Sam.
请原谅这里乱七八糟，萨姆。
12This room's very untidy.
这房间太不整洁了。
13We're pack /pæk/ v. 包装，打包，装箱ing our suitcase /'suːtkeɪs/ n. 手提箱s.
我们在收拾行李箱。
14We're going to leave /liːv/ v. 离开 tomorrow.
我们打算明天动身。
15Tom and I are going to have a holiday.
汤姆和我要去度假。
16Aren't you lucky!
你们真幸运！
17When are you going to have a holiday, Sam?
你打算什么时候度假，萨姆？
18I don't know. I've already had my holiday this year.
我不知道。今年我已经休过假了。
19Where did you go?
你去哪儿了？
20I stayed at home!
我呆在家里了！
