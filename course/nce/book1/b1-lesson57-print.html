Lesson 57  An unusual day 不平常的一天
8 点钟了。

旁白1. It is eight o'clock /ə'klɒk/ adv. 点钟.

旁白2. The children go to school by car every day.

旁白3. But today they are going to school on foot.

旁白4. It is ten o'clock.

旁白5. Mrs. Sawyer usually stays at home in the morning.

旁白6. But this morning she is going to the shops /ʃɒp/ n. 商店.

旁白7. It is four o'clock.

旁白8. In the afternoon, Mrs. Sawyer usually drinks tea in the living room.

旁白9. But this afternoon she is drinking tea in the garden.

旁白10. It is six o'clock.

旁白11. In the evening, the children usually do their homework.

旁白12. But this evening they are not doing their homework.

旁白13. At the moment /'məʊmənt/ n. 片刻，瞬间, they are playing in the garden.

旁白14. It is nine o'clock.

旁白15. Mr. Sawyer usually reads his newspaper at night.

旁白16. But he's not reading his newspaper tonight.

旁白17. At the moment, he's reading an interesting book.

旁白1. 8 点钟了。

旁白2. 孩子们每天坐小汽车上学。

旁白3. 但今天他们步行上学。

旁白4. 10 点钟了。

旁白5. 索耶太太上午通常呆在家里。

旁白6. 但今天上午她要去商店。

旁白7. 4 点钟了。

旁白8. 下午，索耶太太通常在起居室喝茶。

旁白9. 但今天下午她正在花园里喝茶。

旁白10. 6 点钟了。

旁白11. 晚上，孩子们通常做作业。

旁白12. 但今天晚上他们没在做作业。

旁白13. 此刻，他们正在花园里玩。

旁白14. 9 点钟了。

旁白15. 索耶先生夜里通常看报纸。

旁白16. 但今晚他没在看报纸。

旁白17. 此刻，他正在看一本有趣的书。
