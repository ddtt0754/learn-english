一全文逐句语法解读

本课继续围绕 间接引语，重点是 情态动词在时态倒移中的变化（may → might、can → could、will → would）。

¶ 1 · 情态动词的时态倒移（句 14、15、16、17）

句子语法点
she might retiremight may → might
she couldn't make up her mindcan → could（此处表“不能”）
she would have to ask her future husbandwill → would
her future husband would not let her…won't → would not

¶ 2 · be going to 的倒移（句 10、18）

句子语法点
We're going to get married next week.直接引语中的 be going to
they would get married next week间接引语中 going to → would
she introduced us to Carlosintroduce sb. to sb. = 把某人介绍给某人

¶ 3 · 时间与地点的变化（句 3、13、14）

句子语法点
I can't make up my mind.make up one's mind = 拿不定主意 / 打定主意
'Karen Marsh: The Latest.'the latest 在此意为“最新消息”
At her London Hotel today直接引语中的 today 在间接引语中可保留或改为 that day

短语用法与例句
