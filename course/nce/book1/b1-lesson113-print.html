Lesson 113  Small change 零钱
请买票！

售票员1. Fares /feə/ n. 车费，车票, please!

乘客2. Trafalgar Square, please.

售票员3. I'm sorry, sir. I can't change /tʃeɪndʒ/ v. 兑换（钱） a ten-pound note /nəʊt/ n. 纸币.

售票员4. Haven't you got any small change?

乘客5. I've got no small change. I'm afraid.

售票员6. I'll ask some of the passengers /'pæsɪndʒə/ n. 乘客.

售票员7. Have you any small change, sir?

1st 乘客8. I'm sorry. I've got none /nʌn/ pron. 没有任何东西.

2nd 乘客9. I haven't got any either.

售票员10. Can you change this ten-pound note, madam?

3rd 乘客11. I'm afraid I can't.

4th 乘客12. Neither /'naɪðə/ adv. 也不 can I.

售票员13. I'm very sorry, sir. You must get off /get 'ɒf/ 下车 the bus.

售票员14. None of our passengers can change this note.

售票员15. They're all millionaires!

流浪汉16. Except /ɪk'sept/ prep. 除……外 us.

1st 流浪汉17. I've got some small change.

2nd 流浪汉18. So have I.
