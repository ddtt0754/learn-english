🔍 A clear conscience 问心无愧

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 45 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 45（课本印刷页 202-205）· 4 个生词和短语 · 课文 8 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一个「小偷还钱」的幽默小品。语言点集中在 被动语态的各种时态（had been lost / must have been found / had been wrapped / was sent / was paid back）、以及 while + 现在分词、in time、together with 等结构。

¶ 1 · 丢钱（句 1-2）

句子语法点
The whole village soon learnt that a large sum of money had been lost.宾语从句 that 引导 · 过去完成时的被动 had been lost：在过去某时之前「已被丢失」· a large sum of money 一大笔钱
Sam Benton, the local butcher, had lost his wallet while taking his savings to the post office.the local butcher 是 Sam Benton 的同位语 · while taking … ＝ while he was taking …，连词后直接跟现在分词，主语与主句一致时可省略 · 本句主语是 Sam，故用 had lost

¶ 2 · 有人捡到（句 3）

句子语法点
Sam was sure that the wallet must have been found by one of the villagers, but it was not returned to him.情态动词的被动 must have been + 过去分词：对过去情况的推测，「一定已被……」· 被动语态的 by 引出动作执行者 · return sth. to sb. 把某物还给某人

¶ 3 · 钱包出现（句 4-5）

句子语法点
Three months passed, and then one morning, Sam found his wallet outside his front door.three months passed 三个月过去了 · one morning 一天早晨 · outside his front door 在他的大门外
It had been wrapped up in newspaper and it contained half the money he had lost, together with a note which said: 'A thief, yes, but only 50 per cent a thief!'过去完成时的被动 had been wrapped up in ＝ 被用……包起来 · together with 连同，和 · which said 是定语从句，修饰 a note · only 50 per cent a thief ＝ 只算半个小偷，幽默地按还钱比例自称

¶ 4 · 慢慢还清（句 6-8）

句子语法点
Two months later, some more money was sent to Sam with another note: 'Only 25 per cent a thief now!'一般过去时的被动 was sent · some more money 又一些钱 · some / any / no 等可与 more 连用
In time, all Sam's money was paid back in this way.in time 最后，终于（不是「及时」，及时是 in time for）· pay back 归还 · in this way 用这种方式
The last note said: 'I am 100 per cent honest now!'100 per cent honest 百分之百诚实 —— 与前两张字条的 50%、25% 形成层层递进的幽默

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
a large sum of money一大笔钱：a large sum of money had been lost。
while + 现在分词＝ while + 从句，主语一致时可省略：while taking his savings to the post office。
together with连同，和……一起：half the money, together with a note。
per cent百分之……：50 per cent / 100 per cent（也可写作 percent）。
in time最后，终于（注意：不是「及时」，及时是 in time for sth.）。
pay back归还，偿还：all Sam's money was paid back。
a clear conscience问心无愧，良心清白（本课标题）：a clear conscience ＝ 没有亏心。

三语法核心总结

被动语态各时态
had been lost / was sent / was paid back
情态动词的被动
must have been found 一定已被找到
while + 现在分词
连词后省略主语与 be 动词
in time
最后，终于（≠ 及时）
  • 被动语态的时态变化：本课密集出现四种——过去完成时 had been lost（丢钱在前）、情态动词 must have been found（推测）、过去完成时 had been wrapped（包裹在前）、一般过去时 was sent / was paid back（后来的事）。被动语态的关键是 be 动词要随时态变，过去分词不变。
  • while + 现在分词：while taking his savings to the post office 相当于 while he was taking …。用分词结构前提是 while 从句的主语与主句主语相同（这里都是 Sam）。
  • in time 的两种意思：本课 In time 意为「终于、最后」；而 in time 也可解作「及时」，这时通常后面接 for：get there in time for the train（及时赶上火车）。要靠上下文与搭配来分辨。
