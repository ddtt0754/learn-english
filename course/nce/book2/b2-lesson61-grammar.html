🔍 Trouble with the Hubble 哈勃望远镜的困境

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 61 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 61（课本印刷页 280-283）· 17 个生词和短语 · 课文 9 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一篇关于哈勃望远镜的科普报道。语言点集中在 被动语态（was launched / has been put right / will be sent）、将来进行时 will be doing、将来完成时 will have sent，以及 at a cost of、right from the start 等短语。

¶ 1 · 升空与故障（句 1-3）

句子语法点
The Hubble telescope was launched into space by NASA on April 20, 1990 at a cost of over a billion dollars.被动语态 was launched into space：被发射进入太空 · 具体日期前用 on · at a cost of 以……的代价（这里指耗资）· a billion dollars 十亿美元
Right from the start there was trouble with the Hubble.right from the start 从一开始（right 加强语气）· there was trouble with … ……出了问题
The pictures it sent us were very disappointing because its main mirror was faulty!定语从句 it sent us 修饰 the pictures（关系代词被省略）· disappointing 令人失望的 · 原因状语从句 because 引导 · faulty 有误差的（fault + -y）

¶ 2 · 太空维修（句 4-6）

句子语法点
NASA is now going to put the telescope right, so it will soon be sending up four astronauts to repair it.be going to do sth. 打算做某事 · put sth. right 把某物修好 · 将来进行时 will be sending up（按计划即将执行）· send up 送上天
The shuttle Endeavour will be taking the astronauts to the Hubble.shuttle 航天飞机 · Endeavour “奋进”号（专有名词）· 继续用将来进行时，写出「计划中的安排」这一语气
A robot-arm from the Endeavour will grab the telescope and hold it while the astronauts make the necessary repairs.时间状语从句 while 引导（在……期间）· robot-arm 机器手 · grab 抓住 · make the necessary repairs 进行必要的维修

¶ 3 · 视野更清晰（句 7-9）

句子语法点
Of course, the Hubble is above the earth's atmosphere, so it will soon be sending us the clearest pictures of the stars and distant galaxies that we have ever seen.above the earth's atmosphere 在地球大气层之上 · 最高级 + 定语从句 the clearest pictures … that we have ever seen（从句用现在完成时）· distant 遥远的 · galaxy 星系
The Hubble will tell us a great deal about the age and size of the universe.tell sb. about sth. 告诉某人关于某事 · a great deal 大量（此处作代词）· the age and size of the universe 宇宙的年龄和大小
By the time you read this, the Hubble's eagle eye will have sent us thousands and thousands of wonderful pictures.时间状语从句 by the time 引导（到……的时候）· 将来完成时 will have sent：到那时将已经发送了 · eagle eye 鹰眼（喻指锐利的镜头）· thousands and thousands of 成千上万的

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
at a cost of以……的代价（这里指耗资）：at a cost of over a billion dollars。
right from the start从一开始：Right from the start there was trouble with the Hubble。
be going to do sth.打算做某事：NASA is now going to put the telescope right。
put sth. right把某物修好：put the telescope right。
send up送上天，发射：it will soon be sending up four astronauts。
a great deal大量（作代词用）：The Hubble will tell us a great deal。
by the time …到……的时候：By the time you read this…。

三语法核心总结

被动语态
was launched / had been put out / will be sent
将来进行时
will be sending up / will be taking（按计划即将执行）
将来完成时
will have sent（到那时将已完成）
最高级 + 定语从句
the clearest pictures … that we have ever seen
  • 将来进行时 will be doing：it will soon be sending up four astronauts 用将来进行时，表示「按计划将要发生」的事，语气比 will send 更从容，像是在报告一项已定的安排。本课连用 will be sending / will be taking，正写出航天任务早已排定的从容感。
  • 将来完成时 will have sent：By the time you read this, the Hubble's eagle eye will have sent us thousands and thousands of wonderful pictures. by the time 引导的从句用一般现在时表将来，主句用将来完成时，表示「到那时候将已经完成」。
  • the clearest pictures … that we have ever seen：这是「最高级 + 定语从句」的固定搭配，从句必须用现在完成时，与「我们见过的最……」这一最高级结构配套。
