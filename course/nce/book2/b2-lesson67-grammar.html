🔍 Volcanoes 火山

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 67 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 67（课本印刷页 304-307）· 12 个生词和短语 · 课文 9 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则关于火山学家的报道。语言点集中在 spend time doing sth.、manage to do sth.、be able to do、so that 引导的目的状语从句，以及 any man alive 这类形容词后置的用法。

¶ 1 · 终身研究火山（句 1）

句子语法点
Haroun Tazieff, the Polish scientist, has spent his lifetime studying active volcanoes and deep caves in all parts of the world.spend time doing sth. has spent his lifetime studying：花费毕生精力做某事 · 同位语 the Polish scientist · in all parts of the world 在世界各地

¶ 2 · 基图罗火山（句 2-3）

句子语法点
In 1948, he went to Lake Kivu in the Congo to observe a new volcano which he later named Kituro.定语从句 which 引导，修饰 a new volcano · name sth. Kituro 把某物命名为基图罗 · to observe 目的状语
Tazieff was able to set up his camp very close to the volcano while it was erupting violently.时间状语从句 while 引导 · set up 搭建 · close to 靠近 · be able to do sth. 能够做某事（强调「成功做到」）· erupt violently 猛烈喷发

¶ 3 · 岩浆逼近（句 4-6）

句子语法点
Though he managed to take a number of brilliant photographs, he could not stay near the volcano for very long.让步状语从句 though 引导 · manage to do sth. 设法做成某事 · a number of 若干，许多 · brilliant 精彩的
He noticed that a river of liquid rock was coming towards him.宾语从句 that 引导 · a river of 一条……的河流（比喻用法）· liquid rock 液态岩石（岩浆）· come towards 朝……涌来
It threatened to surround him completely, but Tazieff managed to escape just in time.threaten to do sth. 有……的危险，扬言要…… · surround 包围 · escape 逃脱 · just in time 及时

¶ 4 · 往返测量（句 7-9）

句子语法点
He waited until the volcano became quiet and he was able to return two days later.时间状语从句 until 引导 · become quiet 平静下来 · two days later 两天后
This time, he managed to climb into the mouth of Kituro so that he could take photographs and measure temperatures.目的状语从句 so that 引导 · the mouth of a volcano 火山口 · measure temperatures 测量温度
Tazieff has often risked his life in this way. He has been able to tell us more about active volcanoes than any man alive.risk one's life 冒生命危险 · in this way 以这种方式 · 比较结构 more … than any man alive · any man alive 任何在世的人（alive 作后置定语，不能前置）

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
spend time doing sth.花时间做某事：has spent his lifetime studying active volcanoes。
be able to do sth.能够做某事（强调成功做到）：Tazieff was able to set up his camp。
manage to do sth.设法做成某事：he managed to take a number of brilliant photographs。
a number of若干，许多（＋可数名词复数）：a number of brilliant photographs。
just in time及时：Tazieff managed to escape just in time。
so that以便，为了（引导目的状语从句）：so that he could take photographs。
risk one's life冒生命危险：Tazieff has often risked his life in this way。

三语法核心总结

spend time doing sth.
spent his lifetime studying
不定式作目的状语
to observe a new volcano / to take photographs
so that 引导目的从句
so that he could take photographs and measure temperatures
形容词后置
any man alive（alive 只能后置）
  • spend time doing sth.：本课 has spent his lifetime studying active volcanoes 中，spend 后面的动词要用动名词形式，不能写成 to study。同类还有 spend money on sth. （在某物上花钱）、spend time in doing sth.（花时间做某事）。
  • manage to do sth. 与 try to do sth.：manage to do 强调「设法做成了」，try to do 只表示「试图做」，成败未定。本课 he managed to take … / managed to escape 都表示「成功地做到了」，语气比 try 更肯定。
  • any man alive 的语序：alive 是表语形容词，作定语时必须后置，故说 any man alive（任何在世的人），不能说 any alive man。同类还有 the people present（在场的人）。
