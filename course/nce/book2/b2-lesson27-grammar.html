🔍 A wet night 雨夜

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 27 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 27（课本印刷页 130-133）· 15 个生词和短语 · 课文 12 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是「露营遇雨」的记叙文。语言点集中在 过去时态的交替使用（一般过去时、过去进行时、过去完成时）、as soon as 引导的时间状语从句、put up / put out 这类 put 短语动词，以及 wind / wound 这种同形不同音的不规则动词。

¶ 1 · 搭帐篷、做饭（句 1-4）

句子语法点
Late in the afternoon, the boys put up their tent in the middle of a field.put up 搭起（帐篷）· in the middle of 在……中央
As soon as this was done, they cooked a meal over an open fire.as soon as 一……就……，引导时间状语从句 · over an open fire 在明火上
They were all hungry and the food smelt good.smell 是感官系动词，后接形容词 · smelt 是 smell 的过去式（也写作 smelled）
After a wonderful meal, they told stories and sang songs by the campfire.by the campfire 在营火旁 · sang 是 sing 的过去式

¶ 2 · 下雨、进帐篷（句 5-7）

句子语法点
But some time later it began to rain.begin to do sth. 开始做某事 · some time later 过了一会儿
The boys felt tired so they put out the fire and crept into their tent.put out 扑灭（火）· crept 是 creep 的过去式，表示「轻手轻脚地走」
Their sleeping bags were warm and comfortable, so they all slept soundly.复合名词 sleeping bag 的复数加在 bag 上 · sleep soundly 睡得很香

¶ 3 · 半夜的意外（句 8-12）

句子语法点
In the middle of the night, two boys woke up and began shouting.wake up 醒来 · began shouting ＝ began to shout，开始大叫
The tent was full of water!be full of 装满、充满
They all leapt out of their sleeping bags and hurried outside.leap out of 从……中跳出 · leapt 是 leap 的过去式（也写作 leaped）
It was raining heavily and they found that a stream had formed in the field.过去进行时描写背景，had formed 用过去完成时表示在 found 之前就已形成
The stream wound its way across the field and then flowed right under their tent!wind one's way 蜿蜒而行；wound 是 wind 的过去式 · right under 正好在……下面

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
put up搭起（帐篷）、举起、张贴。
put out扑灭（火、灯）。
in the middle of在……中间／中央。
sleep soundly睡得很香、睡得很沉。
wind one's way蜿蜒而行：The stream wound its way across the field.

三语法核心总结

过去进行时
was/were + doing：描写背景动作（It was raining heavily）
过去完成时
had + 过去分词：先完成的动作（a stream had formed）
put 短语
put up 搭起 · put out 扑灭 · put on 穿上
不规则动词
creep-crept · leap-leapt · wind-wound · sing-sang
  • put 的短语动词要整体记：put up 搭起、put out 扑灭、put on 穿上、put off 推迟，后面跟的介词不同，意思完全不同。
  • smell 是感官系动词：the food smelt good 中 good 是表语形容词，不能换成 well。同类还有 taste、sound、feel、look。
  • wound 在这里不是「伤口」：本课 wound 是 wind（蜿蜒）的过去式，读 /waʊnd/；而表示「伤口」的 wound 读 /wuːnd/。
