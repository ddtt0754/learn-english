新概念英语第二册 Lesson 35 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 35（课本印刷页 162-165）· 10 个生词和短语 · 课文 11 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是「公交车司机勇撞贼车」的纪实小故事。语言点集中在 used to do 与现在完成时的对比、as / while 引导的时间状语从句、see sb. do sth. 与 see sb. doing sth. 的区别，以及 such … that … 结果状语从句。

¶ 1 · 从的哥到公交司机（句 1-3）

句子语法点
Roy Trenton used to drive a taxi.used to do sth. 表示「过去常常做（现在不做了）」
A short while ago, however, he became a bus driver and he has not regretted it.a short while ago 不久前 · become 的过去式是 became · 现在完成时 has not regretted 强调到现在为止从不后悔
He is finding his new work far more exciting.far 修饰比较级 ＝ ……得多 · find sth. + 形容词 觉得某事怎么样

¶ 2 · 撞向贼车（句 4-7）

句子语法点
When he was driving along Catford Street recently, he saw two thieves rush out of a shop and run towards a waiting car.see sb. do sth. 看见某人做了某事（强调动作的全过程）· 从句用过去进行时描写背景
One of them was carrying a bag full of money.a bag full of money ＝ 一只装满钱的包；full of … 作后置定语
Roy acted quickly and drove the bus straight at the thieves.act 在这里是「采取行动」· straight at 直冲着……
The one with the money got such a fright that he dropped the bag.such + a + 名词 + that 从句 如此……以致于 · get a fright 吓一跳

¶ 3 · 捉住小偷（句 8-11）

句子语法点
As the thieves were trying to get away in their car, Roy drove his bus into the back of it.as 引导时间状语从句，强调「正在……时」· get away 逃跑、逃脱
While the battered car was moving away, Roy stopped his bus and telephoned the police.while 后接持续性动作（过去进行时）· battered 由 batter（连续重击）+ -ed 构成
The thieves' car was badly damaged and easy to recognize.be easy to do sth. 容易做某事 · 名词所有格复数 thieves'
Shortly afterwards, the police stopped the car and both men were arrested.shortly afterwards 不久之后 · both men were arrested 是被动语态

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
a short while ago不久前；while 作名词表示「一段时间」。
be full of装满、充满：a bag full of money。
such … that …如此……以致于：got such a fright that he dropped the bag。
get away逃跑、离开。
shortly afterwards不久之后，常与一般过去时连用。

三语法核心总结

used to do
过去常常做（现在不了）
see sb. do / doing
see sb. do（全过程）· see sb. doing（正在进行）
as 与 while
两者都引导时间状语从句，as 强调「同时」，while 强调「在……期间」
such … that …
such + a + adj. + n. + that 从句
  • see sb. do sth. 与 see sb. doing sth.：本课是 saw two thieves rush out（看见他们冲出来，强调全过程）；若说 saw them rushing out 则是「看见他们正冲出来」。
  • such a fright that …：fright 是可数名词，所以用 such a，不能写成 so a fright。若用 so，则要说 so frightened that …。
  • while 后面要用持续性动词：While the car was moving away …（✓）；不能说 While he stopped the bus（stop 是瞬间动词）。
