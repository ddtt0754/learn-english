🔍 A pretty carpet 漂亮的地毯

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 52 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 52（课本印刷页 244-247）· 4 个生词和短语 · 课文 9 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则「书当地毯」的生活小品。核心语言点是 现在完成进行时 have been doing（强调动作从过去持续到现在，并有现成结果），以及 to make matters worse、get sth. in order、at the moment、in one's spare time 等固定短语。

¶ 1 · 搬家收拾（句 1-3）

句子语法点
We have just moved into a new house and I have been working hard all morning.现在完成进行时 have been + 现在分词：强调动作从过去某时一直持续到现在，而且有现成结果（屋子还没收好）· move into 搬进 · all morning 整个上午
I have been trying to get my new room in order.have been trying 与上句同属现在完成进行时 · get sth. in order 把某物整理好 / 收拾整齐
This has not been easy because I own over a thousand books.原因状语从句 because 引导 · over ＝ more than，超过 · a thousand books 一千本书（a thousand 前有数词时不加 -s）

¶ 2 · 书铺满地（句 4-5）

句子语法点
To make matters worse, the room is rather small, so I have temporarily put my books on the floor.to make matters worse 更糟糕的是（不定式短语作插入语）· rather 相当，颇为 · temporarily 暂时地（temporary + -ly）· so 引出结果
At the moment, they cover every inch of floor space and I actually have to walk on them to get in or out of the room.at the moment 此刻，目前 · every inch of floor space 地板的每一点空隙 · actually 实际上（actual + -ly）· walk on them 踩在书上 · get in or out of 进出（房间）

¶ 3 · 抬书橱（句 6-7）

句子语法点
A short while ago, my sister helped me to carry one of my old bookcases up the stairs.a short while ago 不久以前 · help sb. (to) do sth. 帮某人做某事 · carry sth. up the stairs 把某物搬上楼
She went into my room and got a big surprise when she saw all those books on the floor.get a big surprise 大吃一惊 · 时间状语从句 when 引导 · all those books 所有那些书

¶ 4 · 漂亮的地毯（句 8-9）

句子语法点
'This is the prettiest carpet I have ever seen,' she said.定语从句 I have ever seen 修饰 the prettiest carpet，关系代词 that 被省略 · 从句用现在完成时，与「我见过的最……」这一最高级结构配套
She gazed at it for some time then added, 'You don't need bookcases at all. You can sit here in your spare time and read the carpet!'gaze at 凝视，盯着看 · for some time 好一会儿 · not … at all 根本不 · in one's spare time 在某人的空闲时间 · read the carpet 把地毯当书读，全文的笑点

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
move into搬进：We have just moved into a new house。
get sth. in order把某物整理好：get my new room in order。
to make matters worse更糟糕的是（作插入语）：To make matters worse, the room is rather small。
at the moment此刻，目前：At the moment, they cover every inch of floor space。
every inch of每一寸……：every inch of floor space。
help sb. (to) do sth.帮某人做某事：my sister helped me to carry one of my old bookcases。
in one's spare time在某人的空闲时间：You can sit here in your spare time。

三语法核心总结

现在完成进行时
have been doing：从过去持续到现在并有结果
最高级 + 定语从句
the prettiest carpet I have ever seen
让步/插入短语
to make matters worse / at the moment
not … at all
根本不，一点也不
  • 现在完成进行时 vs 现在完成时：I have been working hard all morning 强调「一上午一直在干」，而且现在还能看到结果（屋子乱着）；若说 I have worked hard all morning，只陈述「干完了」这个事实。本课通篇用进行式，正为了写出「忙到现在还没忙完」的状态。
  • the prettiest carpet I have ever seen：这是「最高级 + 定语从句」的固定搭配，从句必须用现在完成时 have ever seen，不能写成 I ever saw。同类结构还有 the best film I have ever seen。
  • to make matters worse 是插入语：它是一个独立的不定式短语，用逗号与主句隔开，作用是层层递进地说明处境有多糟。类似插入语还有 to tell the truth、to be honest。
