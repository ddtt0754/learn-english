🔍 Escape 脱逃

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 81 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 81（课本印刷页 374-377）· 14 个生词和短语 · 课文 9 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则战俘逃跑的故事。语言点集中在 过去完成时（had killed / had escaped）、过去分词短语作状语（dressed in …）、as 引导的时间状语从句，以及 stand to attention、knock sb. to the ground 等短语。

¶ 1 · 杀死卫兵（句 1-2）

句子语法点
When he had killed the guard, the prisoner of war quickly dragged him into the bushes.时间状语从句 when 引导 · 过去完成时 had killed（先杀死卫兵，再拖走尸体）· the prisoner of war 战俘（常缩写为 POW）· drag sb. into 把某人拖进
Working rapidly in the darkness, he soon changed into the dead man's clothes.现在分词短语作状语 Working rapidly in the darkness · in the darkness 在黑暗中 · change into 换上（衣服）· the dead man 死者

¶ 2 · 冒名顶替（句 3-4）

句子语法点
Now, dressed in a blue uniform and with a rifle over his shoulder, the prisoner marched boldly up and down in front of the camp.过去分词短语作状语 dressed in a blue uniform · with a rifle over his shoulder 肩上挎着来福枪（with 复合结构）· march up and down 来回踱步 · in front of the camp 在营房前面
He could hear shouting in the camp itself. Lights were blazing and men were running here and there: they had just discovered that a prisoner had escaped.hear shouting 听见喊叫声 · lights were blazing 灯火通明 · here and there 到处 · 过去完成时 had escaped（在「发现」之前已逃走）· the camp itself 营房本身（itself 加强语气）

¶ 3 · 军官通过（句 5-7）

句子语法点
At that moment, a large black car with four officers inside it, stopped at the camp gates.at that moment 就在那时 · with four officers inside it 里面坐着四名军官（with 复合结构）· the camp gates 营房大门
The officers got out and the prisoner stood to attention and saluted as they passed.get out 下车 · stand to attention 立正 · salute 行礼 · 时间状语从句 as 引导（当……的时候）
When they had gone, the driver of the car came towards him. The man obviously wanted to talk.时间状语从句 when 引导 · 过去完成时 had gone · come towards 走向 · obviously 显然

¶ 4 · 夺车而逃（句 8-9）

句子语法点
He was rather elderly with grey hair and clear blue eyes.rather 相当 · elderly 上了年纪的 · with grey hair and clear blue eyes 长着灰白头发和一对明亮的蓝眼睛（with 表特征）
The prisoner felt sorry for him, but there was nothing else he could do. As the man came near, the prisoner knocked him to the ground with a sharp blow. Then, jumping into the car, he drove off as quickly as he could.feel sorry for sb. 为某人感到难过 · 时间状语从句 as 引导 · knock sb. to the ground 把某人打倒在地 · a sharp blow 猛烈的一击 · 现在分词短语作状语 jumping into the car · drive off 把车开走 · as quickly as he could 尽可能快地（＝ as quickly as possible）

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
prisoner of war战俘（缩写 POW）：the prisoner of war quickly dragged him into the bushes。
change into换上（衣服）：he soon changed into the dead man's clothes。
dressed in穿着：dressed in a blue uniform。
march up and down来回踱步：the prisoner marched boldly up and down。
here and there到处：men were running here and there。
stand to attention立正：the prisoner stood to attention and saluted。
feel sorry for sb.为某人感到难过：The prisoner felt sorry for him。
as quickly as one could尽可能快地：he drove off as quickly as he could。

三语法核心总结

过去完成时
had killed / had escaped / had gone
过去分词短语作状语
dressed in a blue uniform
现在分词短语作状语
Working rapidly in the darkness / jumping into the car
as 引导时间状语从句
as they passed / As the man came near
  • 过去完成时点出先后：When he had killed the guard, the prisoner of war quickly dragged him into the bushes. 用 had killed 说明「杀死卫兵」发生在「拖动尸体」之前；they had just discovered that a prisoner had escaped 也用过去完成时，把「发现」与「逃跑」的先后交代清楚。
  • 两个分词短语的分工：Working rapidly in the darkness（现在分词，主动，表伴随）与 dressed in a blue uniform（过去分词，被动，表状态）分别用于描述动作与衣着。判断用现在分词还是过去分词，关键看逻辑主语与该动词是主动关系还是被动关系。
  • with 复合结构：with a rifle over his shoulder 与 with four officers inside it 都是「with + 名词 + 介词短语」，用作定语或状语，表示伴随的情况。这种结构在描写中极为常见，能使句子简洁而有画面感。
