🔍 Can I help you, madam? 您要买什么，夫人？

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 57 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 57（课本印刷页 264-267）· 7 个生词和短语 · 课文 11 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则「以貌取人」的幽默小故事。语言点集中在 though 引导的让步状语从句、分词短语作状语（Glancing… / Not realizing…）、the way + 定语从句，以及 make sb. do sth. 这一使役结构。

¶ 1 · 橱窗前的妇女（句 1-2）

句子语法点
A woman in jeans stood at the window of an expensive shop.a woman in jeans 一个穿牛仔裤的妇女（in + 衣物表示穿着）· at the window of 在……的橱窗前 · an expensive shop 高档商店
Though she hesitated for a moment, she finally went in and asked to see a dress that was in the window.让步状语从句 though 引导 · hesitate for a moment 犹豫片刻 · ask to see 要求看 · 定语从句 that 引导，修饰 a dress

¶ 2 · 售货员的无礼（句 3-5）

句子语法点
The assistant who served her did not like the way she was dressed.the assistant who served her 接待她的售货员（定语从句）· the way she was dressed 她穿着的样子（the way 后省略了 that / in which）· be dressed 穿着
Glancing at her scornfully, he told her that the dress was sold.Glancing at her scornfully 现在分词短语作状语（表伴随）· scornfully 轻蔑地 · 宾语从句 that 引导，作 told 的直接宾语
The woman walked out of the shop angrily and decided to punish the assistant next day.walk out of 走出 · angrily 愤怒地 · decide to do sth. 决定做某事 · punish 惩罚（这里指「教训」）

¶ 3 · 次日反攻（句 6-8）

句子语法点
She returned to the shop the following morning dressed in a fur coat, with a handbag in one hand and a long umbrella in the other.the following morning 第二天早晨 · dressed in a fur coat 穿着裘皮大衣（过去分词短语作状语）· with a handbag in one hand and a long umbrella in the other 是 with 复合结构，描述随身携带的物品
After seeking out the rude assistant, she asked for the same dress.seek out 找出，找到 · the rude assistant 那个无礼的售货员 · ask for 要求得到 · the same dress 同一件衣服
Not realizing who she was, the assistant was eager to serve her this time.Not realizing who she was 现在分词短语作状语（表原因）· 宾语从句 who she was · be eager to do sth. 急于做某事 · this time 这一次

¶ 4 · 耐心的报复（句 9-11）

句子语法点
With great difficulty, he climbed into the shop window to get the dress.with great difficulty 费了好大劲儿（介词短语作状语）· climb into 爬进 · to get the dress 目的状语
As soon as she saw it, the woman said she did not like it.时间状语从句 as soon as 引导（一……就……）· 宾语从句 she did not like it，作 said 的宾语
She enjoyed herself making the assistant bring almost everything in the window before finally buying the dress she had first asked for.enjoy oneself 玩得开心，自得其乐 · make sb. do sth. 让某人做某事（作宾补的不定式省 to）· before finally buying 在最终买下之前 · the dress she had first asked for 她最初要看的那件衣服（定语从句，关系代词被省略）

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
in + 衣物表示穿着：A woman in jeans。
the way sb. is dressed某人穿着的样子：did not like the way she was dressed。
glance at瞥一眼：Glancing at her scornfully。
seek out找出，找到：After seeking out the rude assistant。
be eager to do sth.急于做某事：the assistant was eager to serve her this time。
with great difficulty费了好大劲儿：With great difficulty, he climbed into the shop window。
enjoy oneself玩得开心，自得其乐：She enjoyed herself making the assistant bring…。

三语法核心总结

让步状语从句
Though she hesitated for a moment…
分词短语作状语
Glancing… / Not realizing… / dressed in…
the way + 从句
the way she was dressed
make sb. do sth.
让某人做某事（作宾补的不定式省 to）
  • Though she hesitated for a moment, she finally went in…：though 引导让步状语从句，表示「虽然……但是……」。注意英语中 though 与 but 不能同时使用，两者只能择一。
  • 三个分词短语的分工：Glancing at her scornfully（伴随动作）、Not realizing who she was（原因）、dressed in a fur coat（状态）。现在分词强调主动、进行；过去分词强调被动、完成——「瞥」是售货员发出的动作，故用现在分词；「穿着」是妇女被装扮的状态，故用过去分词。
  • the way she was dressed：the way 后面接定语从句时，习惯上省略关系词 that 或 in which，写成 the way she was dressed，意为「她穿着的样子」。同类还有 the way he speaks、the way it works。
