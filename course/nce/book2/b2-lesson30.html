课文对话 · Football or polo? 足球还是水球？（课本 Lesson 30，印刷页 142-145）
1The Wayle /weɪl/ n. 威尔（河名） is a small river that cuts /kʌts/ v. 穿过 across the park near my home.
威尔河是横穿过我家附近公园的一条小河。
2I like sitting by the Wayle on fine afternoons.
我喜欢在天气晴朗的下午坐在威尔河畔。
3It was warm last Sunday, so I went and sat on the river bank as usual.
上星期日天气很暖和，于是我和往常一样，又去河边坐着。
4Some children were playing games on the bank and there were some people rowing /ˈrəʊɪŋ/ v. 划（船） on the river.
河岸上有些孩子正在玩耍，河面上有些人正在划船。
5Suddenly, one of the children kicked /kɪkt/ v. 踢 a ball very hard and it went towards /təˈwɔːdz/ prep. 朝，向 a passing boat.
突然，一个孩子狠狠地踢了一脚球，球便向一只经过的船飞去。
6Some people on the bank called out to the man in the boat, but he did not hear them.
岸上的人大声向船里的人喊叫，但他没有听见。
7The ball struck him so hard that he nearly /ˈnɪəli/ adv. 几乎 fell into the water.
球重重地打在他身上，使他差点儿落入水中。
8I turned to look at the children, but there weren't any in sight /saɪt/ n. 眼界，视域: they had all run away!
我转过头去看那些孩子，但一个也不见，全都跑了！
9The man laughed when he realized what had happened.
当那个人明白了发生的事情时，笑了起来。
10He called out to the children and threw the ball back to the bank.
他大声叫着那些孩子，把球扔回到岸上。
