🔍 Hot snake 触电的蛇

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 53 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 53（课本印刷页 248-251）· 12 个生词和短语 · 课文 12 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则「蛇引起森林大火」的科普小小说。语言点集中在 被动语态（are caused / was wound）、现在完成进行时 have been trying、以及 in this way、find out、be wound round 等短语；句中还用到 wound 的两种含义。

¶ 1 · 大火之后（句 1-2）

句子语法点
At last firemen have put out a big forest fire in California.at last 最后，终于 · put out 扑灭（火）· fireman 的复数是 firemen（不规则变化）
Since then, they have been trying to find out how the fire began.现在完成进行时 have been trying：从那以后一直试图（并持续到现在）· since then 从那时起，与完成时连用 · 宾语从句 how the fire began，作 find out 的宾语

¶ 2 · 排查原因（句 3-5）

句子语法点
Forest fires are often caused by broken glass or by cigarette ends which people carelessly throw away.被动语态 are caused by：由……引起 · broken glass 碎玻璃 · cigarette ends 烟头 · which people carelessly throw away 是定语从句，修饰 cigarette ends
Yesterday the firemen examined the ground carefully, but were not able to find any broken glass.examine 仔细检查 · be not able to do sth. 未能做某事 · any 用于否定句中
They were also quite sure that a cigarette end did not start the fire.be quite sure that … 十分肯定…… · 宾语从句 that 引导 · start the fire 引起火灾

¶ 3 · 意外发现（句 6-8）

句子语法点
This morning, however, a fireman accidentally discovered the cause.however 作插入语，表转折 · accidentally 偶然地（accident + -al + -ly）· discover the cause 发现原因
He noticed the remains of a snake which was wound round the electric wires of a 16,000-volt power line.被动语态 was wound round：被缠绕在……上 · the remains of a snake 一条蛇的残骸 · a 16,000-volt power line 一条 16,000 伏的高压线
In this way, he was able to solve the mystery.in this way 就这样 · be able to do 能够做（强调成功）· solve the mystery 解开谜团

¶ 4 · 真相大白（句 9-12）

句子语法点
The explanation was simple but very unusual.explanation 解释（explain 的名词形式）· simple but very unusual 简单却很不寻常，为下面的解释作铺垫
A bird had snatched up the snake from the ground and then dropped it on to the wires.过去完成时 had snatched up：先发生的动作 · snatch up 抓起，攫取 · drop sth. on to 把某物丢到……上
The snake then wound itself round the wires.wind oneself round ＝ 把自身缠绕在……上 · 注意 wind（/waɪnd/ 缠绕）与 wound（/wuːnd/ 伤口）拼写相同、读音不同
When it did so, it sent sparks down to the ground and these immediately started a fire.do so 代替前面提到的动作（wound itself round the wires）· send … down to 把……送到 · spark 电火花 · 结尾把整条因果链收齐：蛇缠线 → 短路 → 火花落地 → 起火

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
put out扑灭（火）；也可表示「伸出」：put out a big forest fire。
find out查明，弄清楚：find out how the fire began。
in this way就这样，用这种方法：In this way, he was able to solve the mystery。
be caused by由……引起：Forest fires are often caused by broken glass。
be wound round被缠绕在……上：a snake which was wound round the electric wires。
power line电力线，输电线：the electric wires of a 16,000-volt power line。
hot带电的，通电的（本课标题）：Hot snake ＝ 带电的蛇（触电的蛇）。

三语法核心总结

现在完成进行时
have been trying：一直试图（持续到现在）
被动语态
are caused by / was wound round
宾语从句
how the fire began 作 find out 的宾语
do so
代替前面提到的动作，避免重复
  • 现在完成进行时 have been trying：Since then, they have been trying to find out … 表示从过去某一时刻起一直持续到现在、并且还要继续下去的动作。如果说 have tried，只表示「试过了」；用进行式才写出「一直在试」的持续感。
  • wound 的两副面孔：本课 was wound round 中的 wound 是 wind（/waɪnd/ 缠绕）的过去分词，读 /waʊnd/；而 wound 本身又是一个独立单词「伤口」（读 /wuːnd/）。看到 wound 要先看句子结构，再定读音与词义。
  • do so 的用法：When it did so 中的 did so 代替 wound itself round the wires，避免重复。这是英语中常见的「替代」手段，类似的还有 do that、do it。
