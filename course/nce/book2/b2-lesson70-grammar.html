🔍 Red for danger 危险的红色

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 70 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 70（课本印刷页 316-319）· 16 个生词和短语 · 课文 10 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则「醉汉闯斗牛场」的现场特写。语言点集中在 形容词短语作状语（Apparently sensitive to criticism）、be unaware of / be busy with 等短语，以及 for 引导的并列原因分句和 until 引导的时间状语从句。

¶ 1 · 醉汉闯入（句 1-2）

句子语法点
During a bullfight, a drunk suddenly wandered into the middle of the ring.during 在……期间 · wander into 踉跄走进 · the middle of the ring 斗牛场中央 · suddenly 突然
The crowd began to shout, but the drunk was unaware of the danger.the crowd 人群 · begin to do sth. 开始做某事 · be unaware of 没有意识到 · the danger 危险

¶ 2 · 公牛转向（句 3-4）

句子语法点
The bull was busy with the matador at the time, but it suddenly caught sight of the drunk who was shouting rude remarks and waving a red cap.be busy with 忙于 · at the time 当时 · catch sight of 看见 · 定语从句 who 引导，修饰 the drunk · rude remarks 粗鲁的话 · wave a red cap 挥动红帽子
Apparently sensitive to criticism, the bull forgot all about the matador and charged at the drunk.形容词短语作状语 Apparently sensitive to criticism（表原因）· be sensitive to 对……敏感 · forget all about 把……完全忘掉 · charge at 向……冲去

¶ 3 · 醉汉鞠躬（句 5-8）

句子语法点
The crowd suddenly grew quiet.grow quiet 安静下来（grow 作系动词，后接形容词）· suddenly 突然
The drunk, however, seemed quite sure of himself.be sure of oneself 很自信 · seem 似乎（系动词）· however 然而（作插入语）· quite 相当
When the bull got close to him, he clumsily stepped aside to let it pass.时间状语从句 when 引导 · get close to 靠近 · clumsily 笨拙地 · step aside 闪到一边 · let it pass 让它过去
The crowd broke into cheers and the drunk bowed.break into cheers 爆发出欢呼 · bow 鞠躬 · 与前文「危险」形成喜剧反差

¶ 4 · 拖离现场（句 9-10）

句子语法点
By this time, however, three men had come into the ring and they quickly dragged the drunk to safety.过去完成时 had come（在「欢呼」之前已进入）· by this time 到这时 · drag sb. to safety 把某人拖到安全处
Even the bull seemed to feel sorry for him, for it looked on sympathetically until the drunk was out of the way before once more turning its attention to the matador.并列连词 for 表原因 · look on 旁观 · sympathetically 同情地 · out of the way 不挡道，离开 · 时间状语从句 until 引导 · turn one's attention to 把注意力转向 · once more 再一次

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
be unaware of没有意识到：the drunk was unaware of the danger。
be busy with忙于：The bull was busy with the matador at the time。
catch sight of看见：it suddenly caught sight of the drunk。
be sensitive to对……敏感：Apparently sensitive to criticism。
charge at向……冲去：charged at the drunk。
step aside闪到一边，让开：he clumsily stepped aside to let it pass。
break into cheers爆发出欢呼：The crowd broke into cheers。
drag sb. to safety把某人拖到安全处：dragged the drunk to safety。

三语法核心总结

形容词短语作状语
Apparently sensitive to criticism（表原因）
过去完成时
three men had come into the ring
for 引导原因分句
for it looked on sympathetically
until 引导时间从句
until the drunk was out of the way
  • Apparently sensitive to criticism 是形容词短语作状语：它说明公牛「突然转向醉汉」的原因，相当于 As it was apparently sensitive to criticism。形容词或形容词短语作状语时，其逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致。
  • for 引导的并列原因分句：Even the bull seemed to feel sorry for him, for it looked on sympathetically until … 这里 for 是并列连词，表「因为」，语气比 because 弱，通常用来补充说明理由，不能放在句首。
  • until 与 before 的连用：it looked on sympathetically until the drunk was out of the way before once more turning its attention to the matador. until 说明「一直看到……为止」，before 说明「在这之后才……」；两个连接词前后呼应，把公牛「久久注视」的过程写足。
