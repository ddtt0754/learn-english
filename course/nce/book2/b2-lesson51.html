课文对话 · Reward for virtue 对美德的奖赏（课本 Lesson 51，印刷页 240-243）
1My friend, Hugh, has always been fat, but things got so bad recently that he decided to go on a diet /'daɪət/ n. 节食.
我的朋友休一直很胖，但是近来情况变得越发糟糕，以致他决定节食。
2He began his diet a week ago.
他是一星期前开始节食的。
3First of all, he wrote out a long list of all the foods which were forbidden /fə'bɪdn/ v. 禁止.
首先，他开列了一张长长的单子，上面列了所有禁吃的食物。
4The list included most of the things Hugh loves: butter, potatoes, rice, beer, milk, chocolate, and sweets.
这张单子上的大多数食物都是休喜欢吃的：黄油、土豆、米饭、啤酒、牛奶、巧克力和糖果。
5Yesterday I paid him a visit.
昨天我去拜访他。
6I rang the bell and was not surprised to see that Hugh was still as fat as ever.
我按响了门铃，当看到休仍和往常一样胖时，我并不感到惊奇。
7He led me into his room and hurriedly /'hʌrɪdli/ adv. 匆忙地 hid a large parcel under his desk.
他把我领进屋，慌忙把一个大包藏到了桌子下面。
8It was obvious that he was very embarrassed /ɪm'bærəst/ v. 使尴尬.
显然他感到很尴尬。
9When I asked him what he was doing, he smiled guiltily /'ɡɪltɪli/ adv. 内疚地 and then put the parcel on the desk.
当我问他正干什么时，他内疚地笑了笑，然后把那个大包拿到了桌上。
10He explained that his diet was so strict /strɪkt/ adj. 严格的 that he had to reward /rɪ'wɔːd/ v. 给奖赏 himself occasionally /ə'keɪʒnəli/ adv. 偶尔地.
他解释说，他的饮食控制得太严格了，以致不得不偶尔奖赏自己一下。
11Then he showed me the contents of the parcel.
接着他给我看了包里的东西。
12It contained five large bars of chocolate and three bags of sweets!
里面装了5大块巧克力和3袋糖果！
