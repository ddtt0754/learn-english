新概念英语第二册 Lesson 74 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 74（课本印刷页 346-349）· 6 个生词和短语 · 课文 9 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则「演员野餐被赶」的幽默小品。语言点集中在 过去完成时（had taken / had carried）、so that 引导的目的状语从句、as 引导的非限制性定语从句，以及 in case 引导的条件从句。

¶ 1 · 隐秘的野餐（句 1-3）

句子语法点
An ancient bus stopped by a dry river bed and a party of famous actors and actresses got off.an ancient bus 一辆古旧的公共汽车 · a dry river bed 干涸的河床 · a party of 一群 · get off 下车 · actors and actresses 男女演员
Dressed in dark glasses and old clothes, they had taken special precautions so that no one should recognize them.过去分词短语作状语 Dressed in …（表状态）· 过去完成时 had taken · 目的状语从句 so that 引导 · take precautions 采取预防措施
But as they soon discovered, disguises can sometimes be too perfect.非限制性定语从句 as 引导，指代后面的整个主句（相当于 which），意为「正如他们很快发现的」· disguise 伪装 · too perfect 过分完美

¶ 2 · 野餐开始（句 4-6）

句子语法点
"This is a wonderful place for a picnic," said Gloria Gleam.a wonderful place for a picnic 野餐的好地方 · 直接引语置于句首时，said 可放在主语之前（said Gloria Gleam），起强调作用
"It couldn't be better, Gloria," Brinksley Meers agreed. "No newspaper men, no film fans! Why don't we come more often?"It couldn't be better 再好不过了（比较级表最高级）· agree 同意，附和 · Why don't we …? 我们为什么不……呢？（提出建议）· newspaper men 记者
Meanwhile, two other actors, Rockwall Slinger and Merlin Greeves, had carried two large food baskets to a shady spot under some trees.过去完成时 had carried（在「安顿好」之前已把篮子搬来）· meanwhile 与此同时 · a shady spot 阴凉处 · Rockwall Slinger and Merlin Greeves 是 two other actors 的同位语

¶ 3 · 陌生人出现（句 7）

句子语法点
When they had all made themselves comfortable, a stranger appeared. He looked very angry. "Now you get out of here, all of you!" he shouted. "I'm a sheriff here. Do you see that notice? It says 'No Camping' — in case you can't read!"make oneself comfortable 使自己舒服 · get out of here 从这儿出去 · 宾语从句 Do you see that notice? / It says … · in case 以防，万一（引导条件从句）· 过去完成时 had made（在「陌生人出现」之前已完成）

¶ 4 · 反唇相讥（句 8-9）

句子语法点
"Look, sheriff," said Rockwall, "don't be too hard on us. I'm Rockwall Slinger and this is Merlin Greeves."be hard on sb. 对某人苛刻 · Look 喂（引起注意）· 报出真名，试图自证身份
"Oh, is it?" said the sheriff with a sneer. "Well, I'm Brinksley Meers, and my other name is Gloria Gleam. Now you get out of here fast!"with a sneer 带着讥笑 · Oh, is it? 哦，是吗？（表示怀疑）· my other name is … 我的另一个名字是……（借演员艺名调侃）· get out of here fast 快点滚出去 · 结尾以身份「反串」抖出笑点

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
a party of一群：a party of famous actors and actresses。
take precautions采取预防措施：they had taken special precautions。
so that以便，为了（引导目的状语从句）：so that no one should recognize them。
It couldn't be better再好不过了（比较级表最高级）：It couldn't be better, Gloria。
Why don't we …?我们为什么不……呢？（提出建议）：Why don't we come more often?
make oneself comfortable使自己舒服：they had all made themselves comfortable。
get out of here从这儿出去：Now you get out of here, all of you!
be hard on sb.对某人苛刻：don't be too hard on us。

三语法核心总结

过去完成时
had taken / had carried / had made
so that 目的状语从句
so that no one should recognize them
as 引导非限制性定语从句
as they soon discovered
in case 引导条件从句
in case you can't read
  • as 引导的非限制性定语从句：But as they soon discovered, disguises can sometimes be too perfect. 这里的 as 是关系代词，指代后面的整个主句（＝ which），意为「正如他们很快发现的那样」。这种 as 从句可置于句首、句中或句末，位置比 which 灵活。
  • so that 引导目的状语从句：they had taken special precautions so that no one should recognize them. so that 表示「为了，以便」，从句中常用 should / could / might 等情态动词。若表示目的，so that 前一般不加逗号。
  • in case 的用法：in case you can't read 意为「以防你不识字」，in case 引导条件（或目的）从句，后面用一般现在时表示将来，不用 will。同类还有 in case of fire（万一发生火灾）。
