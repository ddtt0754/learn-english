🔍 What's for supper? 晚餐吃什么？

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 90 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 90（课本印刷页 410-413）· 12 个生词和短语 · 课文 8 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则科普小品，讲北海钻井平台附近鱼类异常长大的原因。语言点集中在 it comes as a surprise to learn that … 这一形式主语句型、have sth. done（have had special cages made）、be frightened out of one's wits 等固定表达，以及 as a result 的用法。

¶ 1 · 鱼越来越贵（句 1）

句子语法点
Fish and chips has always been a favourite dish in Britain, but as the oceans have been overfished, fish has become more and more expensive.fish and chips 炸鱼加炸土豆片（视为一道菜，作主语时谓语用单数 has）· a favourite dish 一道受人喜爱的菜 · 原因状语从句 as 引导 · 被动语态 have been overfished · more and more + 形容词 越来越……

¶ 2 · 巨鱼惊扰潜水员（句 2-4）

句子语法点
So it comes as a surprise to learn that giant fish are terrifying the divers on North Sea oil rigs.it comes as a surprise to learn that … 听说……令人吃惊（it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to learn that …）· 宾语从句 that 引导作 learn 的宾语 · terrify 使害怕 · North Sea oil rigs 北海的石油钻井平台 · the divers on North Sea oil rigs 用介词短语作定语
Oil rigs have to be repaired frequently and divers, who often have to work in darkness a hundred feet under water, have been frightened out of their wits by giant fish bumping into them as they work.被动语态 have to be repaired · frequently 经常地 · 非限制性定语从句 who 引导 · in darkness 在黑暗中 · a hundred feet under water 在水下100英尺 · be frightened out of one's wits 吓得惊慌失措 · 现在分词短语 bumping into them 作定语修饰 giant fish · 时间状语从句 as 引导
Now they have had special cages made to protect them from these monsters.have sth. done 请人做某事，have had special cages made 即「请人做了特别的笼子」· protect sb. from sth. 保护某人免受…… · these monsters 这些庞然大物

¶ 3 · 巨鱼的真面目（句 5-6）

句子语法点
The fish are not sharks or killer whales, but favourite eating varieties like cod and skate which grow to unnatural sizes, sometimes as much as twelve feet in length.not … but … 不是……而是…… · killer whales 虎鲸 · favourite eating varieties 人们爱吃的品种 · 定语从句 which 引导修饰 varieties · grow to unnatural sizes 长到不正常的尺寸 · as much as 多达 · in length 在长度上
Three factors have caused these fish to grow so large: the warm water round the hot oil pipes under the sea; the plentiful supply of food thrown overboard by the crews on the rigs; the total absence of fishing boats around the oil rigs.cause sth. to do sth. 促使某物做某事 · 冒号后 three factors 依次列出，用分号隔开 · round the hot oil pipes 在热油管周围 · thrown overboard by the crews 由船员们扔到海里的过去分词短语作定语 · the total absence of 完全不存在

¶ 4 · 结果与悬念（句 7-8）

句子语法点
As a result, the fish just eat and eat and grow and grow in the lovely warm water.as a result 结果（承接上句的三点原因）· eat and eat / grow and grow 用重复的动词表示「不停地」· in the lovely warm water 在可爱的温暖的水里
Who eats who?全篇以一句设问收尾：潜水员怕鱼，而鱼又是人们的食物，到底「谁吃谁」？这种反问留有余味，是科普小品常用的幽默结尾。

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
a favourite dish一道受人喜爱的菜：a favourite dish in Britain。
more and more expensive越来越贵：fish has become more and more expensive。
it comes as a surprise to learn that …听说……令人吃惊：So it comes as a surprise to learn that …。
work in darkness在黑暗中工作：often have to work in darkness a hundred feet under water。
be frightened out of one's wits吓得惊慌失措：have been frightened out of their wits。
bump into撞上，碰见：giant fish bumping into them as they work。
have sth. done请人做某事：they have had special cages made。
protect sb. from sth.保护某人免受……：to protect them from these monsters。
as much as多达：sometimes as much as twelve feet in length。
as a result结果：As a result, the fish just eat and eat …。

三语法核心总结

it 作形式主语
it comes as a surprise to learn that …
have sth. done
have had special cages made（请人做笼子）
be frightened out of one's wits
吓得惊慌失措
as a result
结果（承接原因）
  • have sth. done 结构：have + 宾语 + 过去分词，表示「请（让）别人做某事」或「遭遇某事」。本课 they have had special cages made 是「他们请人做了特别的笼子」，made 与 cages 之间是被动关系。同类还有 have my hair cut、have the house painted。
  • it comes as a surprise to learn that …：句中的 it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to learn that giant fish are terrifying the divers …。这一句型表示「得知……令人吃惊」，come as a surprise 是固定搭配。
  • fish and chips 作主语用单数：fish and chips 被视为「一道菜」，是一个整体，所以谓语用单数 has。同类还有 bread and butter、a knife and fork 等。
