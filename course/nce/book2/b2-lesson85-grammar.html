🔍 Never too old to learn 活到老学到老

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 85 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 85（课本印刷页 390-393）· 14 个生词和短语 · 课文 9 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一篇记事散文，写一位老校长退休引发的感想。语言点集中在 现在分词作状语（informing me that …）、将来进行时（will be sending / will be attending）、将来完成进行时（will have been teaching）、it is a … that … 这一强调结构，以及 devote oneself to、mark the occasion 等固定表达。

¶ 1 · 校长要退休了（句 1-2）

句子语法点
I have just received a letter from my old school, informing me that my former headmaster, Mr. Stuart Page, will be retiring next week.receive a letter from 收到……的来信 · 现在分词短语作状语 informing me that … （补充说明信的内容，相当于 which informed me that …）· 宾语从句 that 引导 · 将来进行时 will be retiring（表示最近的将来，语气比 will retire 更委婉）· 同位语 Mr. Stuart Page 说明姓名
Pupils of the school, old and new, will be sending him a present to mark the occasion.pupils of the school 学校的学生 · 同位语 old and new 新老（指学生无论新旧）· 将来进行时 will be sending · mark the occasion 纪念这个日子（mark 是「庆祝、纪念」的意思）

¶ 2 · 签名簿与感谢（句 3-4）

句子语法点
All those who have contributed towards the gift will sign their names in a large album which will be sent to the headmaster's home.定语从句 who 引导修饰 all those · contribute towards 为……捐钱 · sign one’s name 签名 · 定语从句 which 引导修饰 a large album · 用两个定语从句把「谁捐钱」「簿子送给谁」交代清楚
We shall all remember Mr. Page for his patience and understanding and for the kindly encouragement he gave us when we went so unwillingly to school.remember sb. for sth. 因某事记得某人 · 两个并列的 for 短语（for his patience … and for the kindly encouragement …）· 定语从句 (that) he gave us 修饰 encouragement · 时间状语从句 when 引导 · go to school unwillingly 很不情愿地去上学

¶ 3 · 告别宴会与巧合（句 5-6）

句子语法点
A great many former pupils will be attending a farewell dinner in his honour next Thursday.a great many 许多（后接可数名词复数）· former pupils 以前的学生 · 将来进行时 will be attending · in one’s honour 为向某人表示敬意（此处指为佩奇先生举行的宴会）· next Thursday 下星期四
It is a curious coincidence that the day before his retirement, Mr. Page will have been teaching for a total of forty years.it is a … that … 强调结构的一种变体：it 是形式主语，that 引导主语从句 · a curious coincidence 奇怪的巧合 · 将来完成进行时 will have been teaching （到将来某时为止一直在做某事，强调持续四十年）· a total of 总数为

¶ 4 · 退休之后（句 7-9）

句子语法点
After he has retired, he will devote himself to gardening.时间状语从句 after 引导，用现在完成时 has retired 表示「将来某时之前已完成」，不可用 will have retired · devote oneself to 致力于……（to 是介词，后接名词或动名词）· gardening 园艺
For him, this will be an entirely new hobby.for him 对他来说 · an entirely new hobby 一种全新的爱好 · entirely 完全地，修饰 new
But this does not matter, for, as he has often remarked, one is never too old to learn.this does not matter 这没有关系 · 并列连词 for 表示「因为」（比 because 正式，且不能置于句首）· 非限制性定语从句 as he has often remarked 正如他常说的那样 · never too old to learn 永远不会因太老而不能学（never too … to … 双重否定表肯定）

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
receive a letter from收到……的来信：I have just received a letter from my old school。
mark the occasion纪念这个日子：will be sending him a present to mark the occasion。
contribute towards为……捐钱：those who have contributed towards the gift。
sign one's name签名：will sign their names in a large album。
remember sb. for sth.因某事记得某人：We shall all remember Mr. Page for his patience。
in one's honour为向某人表示敬意：a farewell dinner in his honour。
a great many许多（接可数名词复数）：A great many former pupils will be attending …。
devote oneself to致力于……：he will devote himself to gardening。
never too old to learn永远不会因太老而不能学：one is never too old to learn。

三语法核心总结

现在分词作状语
informing me that … 补充说明信件内容
将来进行时
will be sending / will be attending / will be retiring
将来完成进行时
will have been teaching（持续四十年）
devote oneself to + 名词/动名词
to 是介词：devote himself to gardening
never too … to …
双重否定表肯定：never too old to learn
  • the day before his retirement 与将来完成进行时：句 6 的 It is a curious coincidence that the day before his retirement, Mr. Page will have been teaching for a total of forty years. 表示「到他退休的前一天，他教书就整整四十年了」。will have been teaching 是将来完成进行时，强调动作从过去一直延续到将来某一时刻。
  • devote oneself to 的 to 是介词：后面要接名词或动名词，不能接动词原形。本课是 devote himself to gardening；同类还有 look forward to seeing you、be used to getting up early。
  • for 表「因为」：句 9 用并列连词 for 引出原因，语气比 because 弱，通常用逗号与主句隔开，且不能回答 why 的提问。同类用法还见于 It must have rained, for the ground is wet.。
