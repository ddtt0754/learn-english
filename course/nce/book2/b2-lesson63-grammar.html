🔍 She was not amused 她并不觉得好笑

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 63 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 63（课本印刷页 288-291）· 6 个生词和短语 · 课文 11 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则「父女之间的幽默落差」的小故事。语言点集中在 a large circle of / admire sb. for sth.、the sort of thing + 定语从句、间接引语中的宾语从句（问珍妮是否喜欢、为何不喜欢），以及 as soon as 引导的时间状语从句。

¶ 1 · 交际家杰里米（句 1-2）

句子语法点
Jeremy Hampden has a large circle of friends and is very popular at parties.a large circle of friends 一大圈朋友 · be popular at parties 在社交场合很受欢迎 · 用一般现在时陈述常态
Everybody admires him for his great sense of humour — everybody, that is, except his six-year-old daughter, Jenny.admire sb. for sth. 因某事而佩服某人 · a sense of humour 幽默感 · that is 也就是说（插入语）· except 除……之外 · 破折号之后的补语是全篇的伏笔

¶ 2 · 婚礼演讲（句 3-6）

句子语法点
Recently, one of Jeremy's closest friends asked him to make a speech at a wedding reception.ask sb. to do sth. 请某人做某事 · make a speech 作演讲 · a wedding reception 婚礼招待会（婚宴）· closest 是 close 的最高级
This is the sort of thing that Jeremy loves.the sort of thing ……类的事情 · 定语从句 that Jeremy loves 修饰 the sort of thing
He prepared the speech carefully and went to the wedding with Jenny.prepare sth. carefully 认真准备 · go to the wedding 参加婚礼 · 交代女儿同行的原因
He had included a large number of funny stories in the speech and, of course, it was a great success.过去完成时 had included（演讲之前就已写入）· include sth. in sth. 把某物包括在某物中 · a large number of 许多（＋可数名词复数）· a great success 一次巨大的成功

¶ 3 · 女儿的反应（句 7-9）

句子语法点
As soon as he had finished, Jenny told him she wanted to go home.时间状语从句 as soon as 引导 · 过去完成时 had finished（先讲完，再说话）· 宾语从句 she wanted to go home
Jeremy was a little disappointed by this but he did as his daughter asked.be disappointed by 因……而失望 · a little 有点儿（修饰形容词）· do as sb. asks 按某人要求的做
On the way home, he asked Jenny if she had enjoyed the speech.宾语从句 if 引导（＝ whether，是否）· on the way home 在回家的路上 · had enjoyed 用过去完成时，指在婚礼当时是否喜欢

¶ 4 · 出人意料的答案（句 10-11）

句子语法点
To his surprise, she said she hadn't.to one's surprise 令某人惊讶的是 · she hadn't ＝ she hadn't enjoyed the speech（省略，避免重复）
Jeremy asked her why this was so and she told him that she did not like to see so many people laughing at him!宾语从句 why this was so（为何如此）· 宾语从句 that 引导 · like to do sth. 喜欢做某事 · see sb. doing sth. 看见某人正在做某事 · laugh at sb. 嘲笑某人 · 结尾以孩子的天真理解制造笑点

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
a large circle of一大圈（朋友）：Jeremy Hampden has a large circle of friends。
admire sb. for sth.因某事而佩服某人：Everybody admires him for his great sense of humour。
a sense of humour幽默感：his great sense of humour。
make a speech作演讲：asked him to make a speech at a wedding reception。
the sort of thing……类的事情：This is the sort of thing that Jeremy loves。
a large number of许多（＋可数名词复数）：a large number of funny stories。
to one's surprise令某人惊讶的是：To his surprise, she said she hadn't。
laugh at sb.嘲笑某人：so many people laughing at him。

三语法核心总结

间接引语的宾语从句
ask sb. if / why …（是否 / 为何）
过去完成时
had included / had finished / had enjoyed
定语从句
the sort of thing that Jeremy loves
see sb. doing sth.
看见某人正在做某事
  • 间接引语中的时态后移：直接引语 "Did you enjoy the speech?" 转为间接引语时变成 he asked Jenny if she had enjoyed the speech，一般过去时后移为过去完成时。本课句 9（if she had enjoyed）与句 11（why this was so、did not like）都是这一规则的体现。
  • the sort of thing that Jeremy loves：the sort of thing 后接定语从句时，关系代词通常用 that（也可省略），不要用 which。同类还有 the kind of book that I like。
  • see sb. doing sth. 与 see sb. do sth.：前者强调「看见某人正在做某事」（动作正在进行），后者强调「看见某人做了某事」（动作已完成）。句中 see so many people laughing at him 用现在分词，写的是珍妮看到的「一群人一直在笑」的画面。
