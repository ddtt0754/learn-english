🔍 Not a gold mine 并非金矿

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 55 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 55（课本印刷页 256-259）· 18 个生词和短语 · 课文 10 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则「寻宝落空」的科普小品。语言点集中在 现在完成时的被动语态（has been invented / has been used / has been buried）、would 表过去习惯、分词短语作状语（Armed with… / hoping to find…），以及 in spite of、be confident that 等短语。

¶ 1 · 探宝器问世（句 1-2）

句子语法点
Dreams of finding lost treasure almost came true recently.dreams of doing sth. 做某事的梦想（of 后接动名词）· come true 变成现实 · recently 最近
A new machine called 'The Revealer' has been invented and it has been used to detect gold which has been buried in the ground.被动语态 has been invented / has been used / has been buried：三者都是现在完成时的被动式（has been + 过去分词）· a machine called … 被叫做……的机器 · use sth. to do sth. 用某物做某事

¶ 2 · 海盗的岩洞（句 3-5）

句子语法点
The machine was used in a cave near the seashore where — it is said — pirates used to hide gold.被动语态 was used · 定语从句 where 引导，修饰 a cave · it is said 据说（插入语）· used to do sth. 过去常常做某事
The pirates would often bury gold in the cave and then fail to collect it.would + 动词原形 表示过去习惯性动作 · fail to do sth. 未能做某事 · collect it 取走它
Armed with the new machine, a search party went into the cave hoping to find buried treasure.armed with 装备着（过去分词短语作状语，表状态）· a search party 探宝队 · hoping to find 希望找到（现在分词短语作状语，表目的）

¶ 3 · 挖出金币（句 6-8）

句子语法点
The leader of the party was examining the soil near the entrance to the cave when the machine showed that there was gold under the ground.the leader of the party 队长 · 时间状语从句 when 引导（正在……这时……）· 宾语从句 that 引导，作 showed 的宾语 · the entrance to 通往……的入口
Very excited, the party dug a hole two feet deep.Very excited 形容词短语作状语，说明队员们的状态 · two feet deep 作后置定语修饰 a hole · dig 的过去式 dug
They finally found a small gold coin which was almost worthless.finally 最后 · 定语从句 which 引导，修饰 a gold coin · worthless 毫无价值的（worth + -less）· 与开篇「梦想找到宝藏」形成强烈落差

¶ 4 · 空铁皮箱（句 9-10）

句子语法点
The party then searched the whole cave thoroughly but did not find anything except an empty tin trunk.search sth. 搜索某地 · thoroughly 彻底地（thorough + -ly）· not … anything except ＝ only，除……之外没有 · an empty tin trunk 一只空铁皮箱
In spite of this, many people are confident that 'The Revealer' may reveal something of value fairly soon.in spite of 尽管（后接名词/代词，不能接句子）· be confident that … 对……有信心 · 宾语从句 that 引导 · of value ＝ valuable，有价值的 · fairly soon 不久

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
come true变成现实：Dreams of finding lost treasure almost came true。
use sth. to do sth.用某物做某事：it has been used to detect gold。
used to do sth.过去常常做某事：pirates used to hide gold。
armed with装备着，带有：Armed with the new machine, a search party went into the cave。
the entrance to……的入口：the entrance to the cave。
in spite of尽管：In spite of this, many people are confident that …。
of value有价值的：reveal something of value。

三语法核心总结

现在完成时的被动语态
has been invented / has been used / has been buried
would + 动词原形
表过去习惯性动作：The pirates would often bury gold…
分词短语作状语
Armed with… / hoping to find…
定语从句
where 修饰 cave；which 修饰 a gold coin
  • 现在完成时的被动语态：has been invented ＝ 已经被发明出来。本课连续出现 has been invented / has been used / has been buried，都是「has been + 过去分词」，既说明动作已完成，又强调主语是被动的承受者。
  • would 表示过去的习惯：The pirates would often bury gold in the cave and then fail to collect it. 这里的 would 不是「将要」，而是「过去常常」，与 used to 意思相近，但 would 只强调反复的动作，不涉及现在是否还有此习惯。
  • armed with / hoping to find 都是状语：armed with the new machine 是过去分词短语作状语（表伴随状态，机器武装的是「探宝队」，故用过去分词）；hoping to find buried treasure 是现在分词短语作状语（表目的，希望是探宝队主动发出的动作，故用现在分词）。
