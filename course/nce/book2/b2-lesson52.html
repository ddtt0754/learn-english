课文对话 · A pretty carpet 漂亮的地毯（课本 Lesson 52，印刷页 244-247）
1We have just moved into a new house and I have been working hard all morning.
我们刚刚搬进一所新房子，我辛辛苦苦地干了整整一个上午。
2I have been trying to get my new room in order.
我试图把我的新房间收拾整齐。
3This has not been easy because I own over a thousand books.
但这并不容易，因为我有1,000多本书。
4To make matters worse, the room is rather small, so I have temporarily /'temprərəli/ adv. 暂时地 put my books on the floor.
更糟糕的是房间还非常小，所以我暂时把书放在了地板上。
5At the moment, they cover every inch /ɪntʃ/ n. 英寸（度量单位） of floor space /speɪs/ n. 空间 and I actually /'æktʃuəli/ adv. 实际上 have to walk on them to get in or out of the room.
这会儿，书把地板的每一点空隙都占据了，我实际上是踩着这些书进出房间的。
6A short while ago, my sister helped me to carry one of my old bookcases up the stairs.
几分钟前，我妹妹帮我把一个旧书橱抬上了楼。
7She went into my room and got a big surprise when she saw all those books on the floor.
她走进我的房间，当她看到地板上的那些书时，大吃一惊。
8'This is the prettiest carpet I have ever seen,' she said.
“这是我见过的最漂亮的地毯，”她说。
9She gazed at it for some time then added, 'You don't need bookcases at all. You can sit here in your spare time and read the carpet!'
她盯着“地毯”看了一会儿，又说：“你根本就用不着书橱，空闲的时候你可以坐在这块地毯上读书的！”
