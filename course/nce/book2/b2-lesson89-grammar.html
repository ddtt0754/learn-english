🔍 A slip of the tongue 口误

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 89 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 89（课本印刷页 406-409）· 6 个生词和短语 · 课文 9 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则幽默小品，讲一场糟糕的演出因报幕员的口误而变得有趣。语言点集中在 need not have done 与 should have done 的对比、get round、burst out laughing 等固定表达，以及 what 引导的名词性从句（what he actually said）。

¶ 1 · 免费演出（句 1）

句子语法点
People will do anything to see a free show — even if it is a bad one.do anything to do sth. 为做某事什么都愿意干 · a free show 免费的演出 · 让步状语从句 even if 引导 · 破折号后的 even if 从句补充说明，让语气更显夸张

¶ 2 · 排队进场（句 2-3）

句子语法点
When the news got round that a comedy show would be presented at our local cinema by the P. and U. Bird Seed Company, we all rushed to see it.时间状语从句 when 引导 · get round 传开（消息）· 同位语从句 that 引导，说明 news 的内容 · 被动语态 would be presented · at our local cinema 在我们当地的电影院 · rush to see it 赶去看
We had to queue for hours to get in and there must have been several hundred people present just before the show began.have to do sth. 不得不做某事 · queue for hours 排队好几个小时 · get in 进去 · there must have been 一定曾有……（对过去情况很有把握的推测）· present 出席的（形容词）

¶ 3 · 演出乏味（句 4-5）

句子语法点
Unfortunately, the show was one of the dullest we have ever seen.one of the dullest (shows) we have ever seen 我们看过的最乏味的演出之一 · 形容词最高级后接定语从句时，从句用现在完成时 · dullest 是 dull 的最高级
Those who failed to get in need not have felt disappointed, as many of the artistes who should have appeared did not come.定语从句 who 引导修饰 those · fail to do sth. 未能做某事 · need not have done 本来不必做（做了却没必要）· should have done 本应该做（却没做）· 原因状语从句 as 引导

¶ 4 · 报幕员的口误（句 6-9）

句子语法点
The only funny things we heard that evening came from the advertiser at the beginning of the programme.定语从句 (that) we heard 修饰 the only funny things · come from 来自 · at the beginning of the programme 在节目开始时 · the only … 后接定语从句时关系代词常用 that
He was obviously very nervous and for some minutes stood awkwardly before the microphone.obviously 显然 · nervous 紧张的 · for some minutes 有好几分钟 · stand awkwardly 局促地站着 · 两个谓语 was 与 stood 并列
As soon as he opened his mouth, everyone burst out laughing.时间状语从句 as soon as 引导（一……就……）· burst out laughing 突然大笑起来（burst out doing sth.）· 本句是全篇的转折点，为下文的口误作铺垫
We all know what the poor man should have said, but what he actually said was: "This is the Poo and Ee Seed Bird Company. Good ladies, evening and gentlemen!"宾语从句 what 引导 · should have said 本应该说（却没说出来）· 主语从句 what he actually said 作主语 · actually 实际上 · 结尾把「好女士们，晚上和先生们」的错位当作笑点，这正呼应标题 a slip of the tongue

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
a free show免费的演出：to see a free show。
get round（消息）传开：When the news got round that …。
queue for hours排队好几个小时：We had to queue for hours to get in。
there must have been一定曾有……（对过去的肯定推测）：there must have been several hundred people present。
need not have done本来不必做（做了却没必要）：need not have felt disappointed。
should have done本应该做（却没做）：many of the artistes who should have appeared did not come。
burst out laughing突然大笑起来：everyone burst out laughing。
a slip of the tongue口误：标题用语，指说错话。

三语法核心总结

need not have done
本来不必做（而实际上做了）
should have done
本应该做（而实际上没做）
what 引导的名词性从句
what he actually said 作主语
同位语从句
the news that a comedy show would be presented …
  • need not have done 与 should have done 的对比：need not have done 表示「本来不必做某事，但事实上却做了」，含惋惜或不解的语气：Those who failed to get in need not have felt disappointed. （没能进去的人本不必感到失望。）should have done 表示「本来应该做某事，但事实上却没做」：many of the artistes who should have appeared did not come.（许多本应出场的艺人却没有来。）
  • the news got round that …：that 从句与 news 是同位关系，说明消息的内容，叫作同位语从句。这类名词还有 fact、idea、hope、belief、doubt 等。注意同位语从句的 that 只起连接作用，不作句子成分，不能省略。
  • there must have been 表推测：must have done 表示对过去事情的肯定推测，意为「一定曾经……」。本课 There must have been several hundred people present. 即「当时一定有好几百人在场」。否定推测则用 can't have done。
