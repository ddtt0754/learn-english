🔍 On strike 罢工

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 84 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 84（课本印刷页 386-389）· 11 个生词和短语 · 课文 11 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则关于罢工的报道。语言点集中在 be due to do sth.、被动语态（is reached / have been seen）、object that …，以及 to some extent、in two days' time 等时间与程度状语。

¶ 1 · 罢工决定（句 1-3）

句子语法点
Busmen have decided to go on strike next week.go on strike 举行罢工 · busmen 公共汽车司机（busman 的复数）· decide to do sth. 决定做某事 · 用现在完成时报道刚作出的决定
The strike is due to begin on Tuesday. No one knows how long it will last.be due to do sth. 定于……，预计…… · on Tuesday 在星期二（具体某天用 on）· 宾语从句 how long it will last 会持续多久
The busmen have stated that the strike will continue until general agreement is reached about pay and working conditions.state 正式提出，宣布 · 宾语从句 that 引导 · 时间状语从句 until 引导 · 被动语态 is reached：达成 · pay and working conditions 工资和工作条件

¶ 2 · 各方应对（句 4-6）

句子语法点
Most people believe that the strike will last for at least a week.most people 大多数人 · at least 至少 · 宾语从句 that 引导
Many owners of private cars are going to offer 'free rides' to people on their way to work.owners of private cars 私人汽车的车主 · offer sth. to sb. 向某人提供某物 · free rides 免费乘车 · on one's way to work 在上班的路上
This will relieve pressure on the trains to some extent.relieve 减轻 · pressure on 对……的压力 · to some extent 在某种程度上 · this 指代上句所说的「免费乘车」

¶ 3 · 学生代驾（句 7-9）

句子语法点
Meanwhile, a number of university students have volunteered to drive buses while the strike lasts.volunteer to do sth. 自愿做某事 · a number of 许多 · meanwhile 与此同时 · 时间状语从句 while 引导
All the students are expert drivers, but before they drive any of the buses, they will have to pass a special test.be expert drivers 是驾车能手 · 时间状语从句 before 引导 · have to 不得不 · pass a test 通过考试 · any of the buses 这些公共汽车中的任何一辆
The students are going to take the test in two days' time. Even so, people are going to find it difficult to get to work.in two days' time 两天后 · even so 即便如此 · find it difficult to do sth. 觉得做某事难（it 是形式宾语，真正宾语是后面的不定式）

¶ 4 · 公众反应（句 10-11）

句子语法点
But so far, the public has expressed its gratitude to the students in letters to the Press.so far 到目前为止 · express one's gratitude to sb. 向某人表达谢意 · in letters to the Press 通过写给报界的信 · the Press 新闻界 · the public 作主语时谓语可用单数（has expressed）
Only one or two people have objected that the students will drive too fast!object that … 反对说…… · one or two 一两个 · 结尾以「反对的理由是学生开得太快」收束，含一丝调侃

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
go on strike举行罢工：Busmen have decided to go on strike next week。
be due to do sth.定于……，预计……：The strike is due to begin on Tuesday。
at least至少：the strike will last for at least a week。
on one's way to work在上班的路上：people on their way to work。
relieve pressure on减轻对……的压力：relieve pressure on the trains。
to some extent在某种程度上：to some extent。
volunteer to do sth.自愿做某事：students have volunteered to drive buses。
object that …反对说……：Only one or two people have objected that the students will drive too fast。

三语法核心总结

be due to do sth.
定于……
被动语态
is reached（达成）
volunteer to do sth.
自愿做某事
find it difficult to do sth.
觉得做某事难（it 为形式宾语）
  • be due to do sth.：The strike is due to begin on Tuesday. be due to 表示「按计划定于……」或「预计将……」，to 后接动词原形。注意与 be due to ＋ 名词（由于）区分：The accident was due to careless driving.（这起事故是由于粗心驾驶造成的。）
  • find it difficult to do sth.：句中 it 是形式宾语，真正的宾语是后面的不定式 to get to work，difficult 是宾语补足语。同类还有 make it clear that …、think it necessary to do sth.。
  • the public 作主语：the public 是集合名词，作主语时谓语既可用单数（has expressed），也可用复数（have expressed），本课用单数，把公众视为一个整体。同类名词还有 the government、the committee。
