🔍 Taxi! 出租汽车！

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 29 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 29（课本印刷页 138-141）· 9 个生词和短语 · 课文 9 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是「会飞的出租汽车」的新闻特写。语言点集中在 现在完成时 has bought / has begun / has flown与一般过去时的对比、定语从句 who flew …、the most surprising thing is that … 这类主语从句，以及 once / on another occasion 这类表示次数的状语。

¶ 1 · 一架不寻常的出租汽车（句 1-4）

句子语法点
Captain Ben Fawcett has bought an unusual taxi and has begun a new service.现在完成时强调「已经买下并开始」· unusual 由 un + usual 构成
The 'taxi' is a small Swiss aeroplane called a 'Pilatus Porter'.called … 是过去分词短语作后置定语 · Swiss 瑞士的
This wonderful plane can carry seven passengers.can + 动词原形表能力 · passenger 乘客
The most surprising thing about it, however, is that it can land anywhere: on snow, water, or even on a ploughed field.主语从句 主语是 the most surprising thing，is that … 引导表语从句 · 冒号后是同位语式的列举

¶ 2 · 送过的乘客（句 5-7）

句子语法点
Captain Fawcett's first passenger was a doctor who flew from Birmingham to a lonely village in the Welsh mountains.who flew … 是定语从句修饰 a doctor · in the Welsh mountains 在威尔士山区
Since then, Captain Fawcett has flown passengers to many unusual places.since then 自从那时起，是现在完成时的标志性时间状语
Once he landed on the roof of a block of flats and on another occasion, he landed in a deserted car park.once ＝ 有一次；on another occasion ＝ 还有一次 · a block of flats 一栋公寓楼

¶ 3 · 拒绝一位商人（句 8-9）

句子语法点
Captain Fawcett has just refused a strange request from a businessman.refuse a request 拒绝一个要求 · a request from sb. 来自某人的要求
… but Captain Fawcett did not take him because the trip was too dangerous.because 引导原因状语从句 · too dangerous 太危险了

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
a block of flats一栋公寓大楼。
on another occasion还有一次；偶尔也说 on one occasion 有一次。
a request from sb.来自某人的请求。
since then从那时起，与现在完成时连用。
the most surprising thing is that …最令人惊奇的是……（表语从句）。

三语法核心总结

现在完成时
has bought／has begun／has flown：强调到现在为止的经历
定语从句
who flew … 修饰 a doctor
表语从句
The most surprising thing is that …
次数状语
once 有一次 · on another occasion 还有一次
  • since then 之后要用现在完成时：Since then, Captain Fawcett has flown …（✓）／flew（✗，那会变成「只讲过去某一时刻」）。
  • the most surprising thing 后面接 that 从句，不能接 what；that 在从句中不作成分。
  • Swiss 是形容词也是单复数同形的名词：a Swiss aeroplane（瑞士飞机）／two Swiss（两个瑞士人），不说 Swisses。
