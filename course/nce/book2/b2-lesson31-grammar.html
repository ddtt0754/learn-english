🔍 Success story 成功者的故事

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 31 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 31（课本印刷页 146-149）· 8 个生词和短语 · 课文 10 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是「从小铺子到大工厂」的成功者故事。语言点集中在 used to do sth. 表示过去常常、过去完成时 had become、定语从句 which employed …，以及 it 作形式主语的句型。

¶ 1 · 小时候的小铺子（句 1-3）

句子语法点
Yesterday afternoon Frank Hawkins was telling me about his experiences as a young man.was telling 用过去进行时描写当时正在做的事 · as a young man 作为年轻人
Before he retired, Frank was the head of a very large business company, but as a boy he used to work in a small shop.used to do sth. 表示「过去常常做（现在已不做）」· the head of 某机构的首脑
It was his job to repair bicycles and at that time he used to work fourteen hours a day.it 作形式主语，真正的主语是 to repair bicycles · fourteen hours a day 每天 14 小时

¶ 2 · 攒钱、开铺子（句 4-7）

句子语法点
He saved money for years and in 1958 he bought a small workshop of his own.save money 攒钱 · of his own 属于他自己的
In his twenties Frank used to make spare parts for aeroplanes.in one's twenties ＝ 在某人二十几岁时 · spare parts 备件
At that time he had two helpers.helper 由 help + -er 构成，指「帮忙的人、助手」
In a few years the small workshop had become a large factory which employed seven hundred and twenty-eight people.过去完成时 had become 表示到那时已经变成 · which employed … 是定语从句修饰 a factory

¶ 3 · 依旧要修车（句 8-10）

句子语法点
Frank smiled when he remembered his hard early years and the long road to success.the long road to success 通往成功的漫长道路 · 用一般过去时叙述
He was still smiling when the door opened and his wife came in.be doing … when … 是常见句型：正……这时……
She wanted him to repair their grandson's bicycle!want sb. to do sth. 想要某人做某事 · 结尾的感叹号点出幽默：厂长还得修孙子的车

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
used to do sth.过去常常做某事（现在不做了）：he used to work in a small shop。
the head of……的首脑／负责人。
of one's own属于某人自己的。
in one's twenties在某人二十几岁的时候。
spare parts备件、零件。

三语法核心总结

used to do
过去常常做（现在不了）／be used to doing 习惯于
过去完成时
had become 表示到过去某一时刻已完成
定语从句
which employed … 修饰 a factory
形式主语
It was his job to repair bicycles.
  • used to do 与 be used to doing 完全不同：used to work 是「过去常常工作」；be used to working 是「习惯于工作」；be used to do 则是「被用来做」。
  • in his twenties 表示年龄段（二十几岁），注意 twenties 要用复数。
  • which employed seven hundred and twenty-eight people：数词 + hundred 时 hundred 不加 s，所以是 seven hundred，不是 seven hundreds。
