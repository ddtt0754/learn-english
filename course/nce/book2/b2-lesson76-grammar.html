🔍 April Fools' Day 愚人节

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 76 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 76（课本印刷页 354-357）· 14 个生词和短语 · 课文 9 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则愚人节玩笑。语言点集中在 现在完成进行时（have been expecting / has been working / has been helping）、现在完成时的被动语态（has been grown / is processed），以及新闻播报常用的 bulletin、go over to 等用语。

¶ 1 · 节目开场（句 1-2）

句子语法点
"To end our special news bulletin," said the voice of the television announcer, "we're going over to the macaroni fields of Calabria."to end our special news bulletin 作为这则特别新闻简报的结尾 · go over to 转到（某地）· the macaroni fields 通心粉田 · Calabria 卡拉布里亚（意大利地名）· 直接引语被插说部分（said the voice of …）分为两半，注意引号配对
"Macaroni has been grown in this area for over six hundred years."现在完成时的被动语态 has been grown：已被种植 · for over six hundred years 六百多年来 · 用被动语态突出「通心粉」这一话题主语

¶ 2 · 丰收在望（句 3-4）

句子语法点
"Two of the leading growers, Giuseppe Moldova and Riccardo Brabante, tell me that they have been expecting a splendid crop this year and harvesting has begun earlier than usual."现在完成进行时 have been expecting：一直在期待 · 宾语从句 that 引导 · a splendid crop 一次大丰收 · earlier than usual 比往常早 · 同位语说明两位种植者的姓名
"Here you can see two workers who, between them, have just finished cutting three cartloads of golden brown macaroni stalks."定语从句 who 引导 · between them 两人协力 · 现在完成时 have just finished cutting · three cartloads of 三车…… · golden brown 金黄色的

¶ 3 · 全村抢收（句 5-6）

句子语法点
"The whole village has been working day and night gathering and threshing this year's crop before the September rains."现在完成进行时 has been working：一直在干 · day and night 日夜不停 · gathering and threshing 收割和脱粒（现在分词短语作状语，表方式）· before the September rains 赶在九月雨季之前
"On the right, you can see Mrs. Brabante herself. She has been helping her husband for thirty years now."on the right 在右边 · herself 亲自（反身代词加强语气）· 现在完成进行时 has been helping（一直帮了三十年）

¶ 4 · 收尾与玩笑（句 7-9）

句子语法点
"Mrs. Brabante is talking to the manager of the local factory where the crop is processed. This last scene shows you what will happen at the end of the harvest: the famous Calabrian macaroni-eating competition!"定语从句 where 引导，修饰 the local factory · 被动语态 is processed：被加工 · 宾语从句 what will happen（会发生的）· the end of the harvest 收获结束之时
"Signor Fratelli, the present champion, has won it every year since 1991."Signor 先生（意大利语称呼语）· the present champion 现任冠军 · has won it every year since 1991 自 1991 年以来年年夺冠（现在完成时 + since）
"And that ends our special bulletin for today, Thursday, April 1st. We're now going back to the studio."that ends … 今天的……到此结束 · April 1st 4 月 1 日（愚人节，全文的「谜底」）· the studio 演播室 · 结尾以日期点破玩笑，回味整篇「通心粉丰收」的荒诞

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
go over to转到（某地）：we're going over to the macaroni fields of Calabria。
day and night日夜不停：The whole village has been working day and night。
between them两人协力：two workers who, between them, have just finished cutting…。
a cartload of一车……：three cartloads of golden brown macaroni stalks。
earlier than usual比往常早：harvesting has begun earlier than usual。
since + 时间点自从……以来（与现在完成时连用）：has won it every year since 1991。
for + 时间段持续……（与完成时连用）：for over six hundred years / for thirty years now。

三语法核心总结

现在完成进行时
have been expecting / has been working / has been helping
现在完成时被动
has been grown
被动语态
is processed
since + 时间点
has won it every year since 1991
  • 现在完成进行时与现在完成时的分工：本课 have been expecting a splendid crop / has been working day and night / has been helping her husband 都用进行式，强调动作从过去持续到现在、并且仍在继续；而 has won it every year since 1991 用现在完成时，只陈述「年年获胜」这一事实。
  • since 与 for 的区别：since 后接时间点（since 1991），for 后接时间段（for over six hundred years / for thirty years now），两者都常与完成时连用。这是完成时最重要的两个时间状语。
  • April 1st 是全文的谜底：整篇新闻一本正经地报道「通心粉丰收」「通心粉收割比赛」，末尾才报出日期 4 月 1 日（愚人节），读者方知是玩笑。理解这类文章，要注意结尾的「点破」往往承载全文的幽默。
