Lesson 65  Jumbo versus the police 小象对警察
去年圣诞节，马戏团老板吉米·盖茨决定送些礼物给儿童医院。

作者1. Last Christmas /'krɪsməs/ n. 圣诞节, the circus /'sɜːkəs/ n. 马戏团 owner, Jimmy Gates, decided to take some presents /'preznts/ n. 礼物 to a children's hospital.

作者2. Dressed up as Father Christmas and accompanied /ə'kʌmpənid/ v. 陪伴，随行 by a 'guard of honour' of six pretty girls, he set off down the main street of the city riding a baby elephant called Jumbo.

作者3. He should have known that the police would never allow this sort of thing.

作者4. A policeman approached /ə'prəʊtʃt/ v. 走近 Jimmy and told him he ought /ɔːt/ modal verb 应该 to have gone along a side street as Jumbo was holding up the traffic.

作者5. Though Jimmy agreed to go at once, Jumbo refused to move.

作者6. Fifteen policemen had to push very hard to get him off the main street.

作者7. The police had a difficult time, but they were most amused.

作者8. 'Jumbo must weigh /weɪ/ v. 重 a few tons,' said a policeman afterwards, 'so it was fortunate /'fɔːtʃənət/ adj. 幸运的 that we didn't have to carry him.'

作者9. 'Of course, we should arrest him, but as he has a good record, we shall let him off this time.'

📄 整篇中文翻译（参考译文）

作者1. 去年圣诞节，马戏团老板吉米·盖茨决定送些礼物给儿童医院。

作者2. 他打扮成圣诞老人，在由6个漂亮姑娘组成的“仪仗队”的陪同下，骑上一头名叫江伯的小象，沿着城里的主要街道出发了。

作者3. 他本应该知道警察绝不会允许这类事情。

作者4. 一个警察走过来告诉吉米，他应该走一条小路，因为江伯阻碍了交通。

作者5. 虽然吉米同意马上就走，但江伯却拒绝移动。

作者6. 15个警察不得不用很大的力气把它推离主要街道。

作者7. 警察虽然吃了苦头，但还是觉得很有趣。

作者8. “江伯一定有好几吨重，”一个警察事后这样说，“值得庆幸的是它没让我们抬它走。”

作者9. “当然，我们应该逮捕它，但由于它一贯表现很好，这次我们饶了它。”

✅ 词汇全覆盖检查：本课生词共 9 个（versus / Christmas / circus / present / accompany / approach / ought / weigh / fortunate）；其中 versus 只出现在标题（Jumbo versus the police）里，已收入固定搭配表，其余 8 个全部嵌入课文句子并加粗释义：Christmas、circus、presents（present）句 1，accompanied（accompany）句 2，approached（approach）、ought 句 4，weigh、fortunate 句 8。
