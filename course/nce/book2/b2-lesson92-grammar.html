🔍 Asking for trouble 自找麻烦

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 92 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 92（课本印刷页 418-421）· 5 个生词和短语 · 课文 10 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则第一人称的幽默小故事。语言点集中在 must have done 表推测、when 引导的并列式时间从句（was almost there when …）、So do I 这类「倒装 + 省略」的应答语，以及 would you mind doing sth.、prefer to do sth. 等固定表达。

¶ 1 · 深夜翻窗（句 1-2）

句子语法点
It must have been about two in the morning when I returned home.must have been 一定曾是……（对过去情况的肯定推测）· about two in the morning 凌晨两点左右 · it … when … 结构：it 指时间，when 引导的从句说明「就在这时」
I tried to wake up my wife by ringing the doorbell, but she was fast asleep, so I got a ladder from the shed in the garden, put it against the wall, and began climbing towards the bedroom window.wake sb. up 叫醒某人 · by ringing the doorbell 靠按门铃（by + 动名词表方式）· be fast asleep 睡得很熟（fast 在此意为「熟」）· 三个并列谓语 got / put / began 连贯地写出一连串动作 · begin doing sth. 开始做某事 · climb towards 朝……爬去

¶ 2 · 警察来了（句 3-5）

句子语法点
I was almost there when a sarcastic voice below said, "I don't think the windows need cleaning at this time of the night."was almost there when … 快到时，就在这时……（when 在此表示「突然、就在这时」）· a sarcastic voice below 下面一个讽刺的声音 · need cleaning 需要擦（need + 动名词，主动形式表被动意义）· at this time of the night 在夜里这个时候
I looked down and nearly fell off the ladder when I saw a policeman.look down 朝下看 · nearly 几乎 · fall off 从……摔下 · 时间状语从句 when 引导，表示「就在此时」
I immediately regretted answering in the way I did, but I said, "I enjoy cleaning windows at night."regret doing sth. 后悔做过某事 · in the way I did 以我那样的方式 · enjoy doing sth. 喜欢做某事 · 嘴上逞强，实则心虚，是这句的幽默所在

¶ 3 · 警察的反唇相讥（句 6）

句子语法点
"So do I," answered the policeman in the same tone. "Excuse my interrupting you. I hate to interrupt a man when he's busy working, but would you mind coming with me to the station?"So do I 我也是（「so + 助动词 + 主语」，表示与前者情况相同）· in the same tone 用同样的腔调 · Excuse my interrupting you 请原谅我打断你（my + 动名词）· hate to do sth. 不愿做某事 · would you mind doing sth. 你介意做某事吗 · when he's busy working 时间状语从句

¶ 4 · 真相大白（句 7-10）

句子语法点
"Well, I'd prefer to stay here," I said. "You see, I've forgotten my key."prefer to do sth. 更愿意做某事 · you see 你知道（插入语）· forget one’s key 忘带钥匙 · 至此才说出实情，扣住标题 asking for trouble
"Your what?" he called.省略的疑问句，表示没听清或不敢相信 · call 在此意为「大声说」
"My key," I shouted.用短短两个词作答，节奏加快，把紧张感推到顶点
Fortunately, the shouting woke up my wife who opened the window just as the policeman had started to climb towards me.定语从句 who 引导修饰 my wife · 时间状语从句 just as 引导（正当……的时候）· had started to do sth. 用过去完成时，表示在妻子开窗之前警察已经动身 · Fortunately 置于句首，为全篇的化险为夷定下基调

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
wake sb. up叫醒某人：I tried to wake up my wife。
by + 动名词靠做某事（方式）：by ringing the doorbell。
be fast asleep睡得很熟：she was fast asleep。
fall off从……摔下：nearly fell off the ladder。
regret doing sth.后悔做过某事：regretted answering in the way I did。
need doing需要被做（主动表被动）：the windows need cleaning。
So do I我也是：So do I, answered the policeman in the same tone。
would you mind doing sth.你介意做某事吗：would you mind coming with me to the station?
prefer to do sth.更愿意做某事：I'd prefer to stay here。

三语法核心总结

must have done
对过去的肯定推测：It must have been about two in the morning。
was doing … when …
正在……就在这时……：I was almost there when …
So do I
「so + 助动词 + 主语」表相同情况
need doing
主动形式表被动意义
  • was/were doing … when …：这一结构表示「正在做某事，就在这时（突然）发生了另一件事」，when 引导的从句常用一般过去时，且是句子的重心。本课 I was almost there when a sarcastic voice below said … 就是「我快爬到那儿了，就在这时下面有个讽刺的声音说……」。
  • need cleaning 的主动表被动：need 后接动名词时，动名词虽为主动形式，却表示被动意义：The windows need cleaning. = The windows need to be cleaned. 同类还有 want、require。
  • So do I 的用法：表示「我也是」时，用「so + 助动词（be/do/have/情态动词）+ 主语」。本课对方说 I enjoy cleaning windows at night.，警察回答 So do I.，用同样的句式回敬，讽刺意味十足。若表示「我也不」，则用 Neither/Nor + 助动词 + 主语。
