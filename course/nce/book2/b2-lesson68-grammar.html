新概念英语第二册 Lesson 68 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 68（课本印刷页 308-311）· 2 个生词和短语 · 课文 12 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则「躲不开的熟人」的幽默小品。语言点集中在 avoid doing sth.、It is no use doing sth.、no matter how 引导的让步状语从句、insist on doing sth.，以及 Would you mind my doing sth.? 这一委婉请求句式。

¶ 1 · 躲不掉的人（句 1-3）

句子语法点
I crossed the street to avoid meeting him, but he saw me and came running towards me.avoid doing sth. 避免做某事（avoid 后接动名词，不能接不定式）· cross the street 穿过马路 · come running 跑过来（现在分词作方式状语）
It was no use pretending that I had not seen him, so I waved to him.It is no use doing sth. 做某事没有用 · 宾语从句 that 引导 · wave to sb. 向某人招手
I never enjoy meeting Nigel Dykes.enjoy doing sth. 喜欢做某事（enjoy 后接动名词）· never 从不，写出不胜其烦

¶ 2 · 无事可做的奈杰尔（句 4-6）

句子语法点
He never has anything to do.have sth. to do 有事可做 · never 与 anything 连用，写出「从来无事可做」
No matter how busy you are, he always insists on coming with you.让步状语从句 no matter how 引导（＝ however）· insist on doing sth. 坚持做某事 · come with sb. 与某人同行
I had to think of a way of preventing him from following me around all morning.think of a way of doing sth. 想到做某事的办法 · prevent sb. from doing sth. 阻止某人做某事 · follow sb. around 跟着某人到处走 · have to 不得不

¶ 3 · 街上相遇（句 7-10）

句子语法点
"Hello, Nigel," I said. "Fancy meeting you here!"Fancy meeting you here! 真想不到在这儿见到你！（fancy 后接动名词，表惊讶）
"Hi, Elizabeth," Nigel answered. "I was just wondering how to spend the morning — until I saw you. You're not busy doing anything, are you?"wonder how to do sth. 想着如何做某事 · spend the morning 消磨上午 · 反意疑问句 are you?（对否定句提问时用肯定形式）
"No, not at all," I answered. "I'm going to ..."not at all 一点也不 · I'm going to … 我打算去……（话被打断，为下文留悬念）
"Would you mind my coming with you?" he asked, before I had finished speaking.Would you mind my doing sth.? 你介意我做某事吗？（my 是动名词的逻辑主语）· before I had finished speaking 在我把话说完之前

¶ 4 · 一起去牙医（句 11-12）

句子语法点
"Not at all," I lied, "but I'm going to the dentist."not at all 没关系 · lie 撒谎 · go to the dentist 去看牙医
"Then I'll come with you," he answered. "There's always plenty to read in the waiting room!"plenty to read 很多可读的东西（不定式作后置定语）· the waiting room 候诊室 · 结尾以「候诊室总有很多东西可读」抖出笑点

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
avoid doing sth.避免做某事：I crossed the street to avoid meeting him。
It is no use doing sth.做某事没有用：It was no use pretending that I had not seen him。
no matter how不管多么（引导让步状语从句）：No matter how busy you are…。
insist on doing sth.坚持做某事：he always insists on coming with you。
prevent sb. from doing sth.阻止某人做某事：preventing him from following me around。
Fancy doing sth.!真想不到……：Fancy meeting you here!
Would you mind my doing sth.?你介意我做某事吗：Would you mind my coming with you?

三语法核心总结

avoid / enjoy / fancy + doing
动词后接动名词，不接不定式
no matter how 让步状语从句
No matter how busy you are…
It is no use doing sth.
做某事没有用
Would you mind my doing sth.?
动名词前用所有格 my 作逻辑主语
  • 后接动名词的动词：本课连续出现 avoid meeting、enjoy meeting、Fancy meeting、insist on coming、prevent … from following，这些动词或短语后面一律接动名词，不能接不定式。同类还有 mind、finish、practise、give up 等。
  • no matter how 引导让步状语从句：No matter how busy you are, he always insists on coming with you. no matter how 等于 however，后面必须紧跟形容词或副词（busy），再接主谓。同类结构还有 no matter what / who / where。
  • Would you mind my coming with you?：mind 后接动名词，动名词的逻辑主语用所有格 my（口语中也用 me）。肯定回答「不介意」用 Not at all. / Certainly not.，「介意」用 I'm sorry, but …。
