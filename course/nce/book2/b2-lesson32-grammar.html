🔍 Shopping made easy 购物变得很方便

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 32 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 32（课本印刷页 150-153）· 6 个生词和短语 · 课文 9 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是「售货员是女儿，母亲每周白拿一件衣服」的讽刺短文。语言点集中在 not so … as … 的否定同级比较、最高级 one of the most expensive …、定语从句 who wrapped it up …，以及 than ever before / as quickly as possible 这类比较表达。

¶ 1 · 今不如昔（句 1-2）

句子语法点
People are not so honest as they once were.not so + 形容词 + as … 否定同级比较，「不如……那样……」· once 曾经
The temptation to steal is greater than ever before — especially in large shops.temptation to do sth. 做某事的诱惑 · greater than ever before 比以往任何时候都更……

¶ 2 · 侦探的监视（句 3-4）

句子语法点
A detective recently watched a well-dressed woman who always went into a large store on Monday mornings.well-dressed 是「副词 + 过去分词」构成的复合形容词 · who always went … 是定语从句
One Monday, there were fewer people in the shop than usual … so it was easier for the detective to watch her.fewer 修饰可数名词复数 · it was easier for sb. to do sth. 中 it 是形式主语

¶ 3 · 偷窃的手法（句 5-9）

句子语法点
The woman first bought a few small articles.a few + 复数名词，表示「几个」· article 指「物品、东西」，也指「文章」
… she chose one of the most expensive dresses in the shop and handed it to an assistant who wrapped it up for her as quickly as possible.one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词 · wrap up 包起来 · as … as possible 尽可能……
Then the woman simply took the parcel and walked out of the shop without paying.simply 意为「就那样、仅仅」· without + 动名词 表示「没有……」
When she was arrested, the detective found out that the shop assistant was her daughter.be arrested 被逮捕（被动语态）· find out 查明
The girl 'gave' her mother a free dress once a week!引号表示「所谓的」· once a week 每周一次

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
not so … as …不如……那样……：People are not so honest as they once were.
than ever before比以往任何时候都……，常与比较级连用。
wrap up包起来、包装好。
as … as possible尽可能……：as quickly as possible。
once a week每周一次；once 表示频度时意为「一次」。

三语法核心总结

否定同级比较
not so + adj. + as … ＝ 不如……
最高级结构
one of the most expensive dresses
复合形容词
well-dressed 穿着讲究的
被动语态
she was arrested 她被逮捕
  • not so … as … 与 not as … as … 意思相同，都表示「不如……那样」；肯定形式则是 as … as …（和……一样）。
  • fewer 与 less：fewer 修饰可数名词复数（fewer people），less 修饰不可数名词（less money）。
  • simply took the parcel 中的 simply 不表示「简单地」，而是「就那样、干脆」，强调她做得毫不掩饰。
