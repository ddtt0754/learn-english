Lesson 1  A private conversation
上星期我去看戏。

作者1. Last week I went to the theatre /ˈθɪətə/ n. 剧场，戏院.

作者2. I had a very good seat /siːt/ n. 座位.

作者3. The play /pleɪ/ n. 戏 was very interesting.

作者4. I did not enjoy it.

作者5. A young man and a young woman were sitting behind me.

作者6. They were talking loudly /ˈlaʊdli/ adv. 大声地.

作者7. I got very angry /ˈæŋɡri/ adj. 生气的.

作者8. I could not hear the actors.

作者9. I turned round.

作者10. I looked at the man and the woman angrily /ˈæŋɡrəli/ adv. 生气地.

作者11. They did not pay any attention /əˈtenʃn/ n. 注意.

作者12. In the end, I could not bear /beə/ v. 容忍 it.

作者13. I turned round again.

作者14. "I can't hear a word!" I said angrily.

男士15. "It's none of your business /ˈbɪznəs/ n. 事," the young man said rudely /ˈruːdli/ adv. 无礼地，粗鲁地.

男士16. "This is a private /ˈpraɪvɪt/ adj. 私人的 conversation /ˌkɒnvəˈseɪʃn/ n. 谈话!"
