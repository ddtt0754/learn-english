课文对话 · Volcanoes 火山（课本 Lesson 67，印刷页 304-307）
1Haroun Tazieff, the Polish scientist, has spent his lifetime studying active /'æktɪv/ adj. 活动的 volcanoes /vɒl'keɪnəʊz/ n. 火山 and deep caves in all parts of the world.
波兰科学家哈罗恩·塔捷耶夫花了毕生的精力来研究世界各地的活火山和深洞。
2In 1948, he went to Lake Kivu /'kiːvuː/ n. 基伍湖 in the Congo /'kɒŋɡəʊ/ n. (the ~) 刚果 to observe a new volcano which he later named Kituro /kɪ'tuərəʊ/ n. 基图罗.
1948年，他去了刚果的基伍湖，去观察一座他后来命名为基图罗的新火山。
3Tazieff was able to set up his camp very close to the volcano while it was erupting /ɪ'rʌptɪŋ/ v. （火山）喷发 violently /'vaɪələntli/ adv. 猛烈地，剧烈地.
当火山正在猛烈喷发时，塔捷耶夫设法把帐篷搭在离它非常近的地方。
4Though he managed /'mænɪdʒd/ v. 设法 to take a number of brilliant /'brɪljənt/ adj. 精彩的 photographs, he could not stay near the volcano for very long.
尽管他设法拍了一些精彩的照片，但他却不能在这座火山附近停留太长的时间。
5He noticed that a river of liquid /'lɪkwɪd/ adj. 液态的 rock was coming towards him.
他发现有一股岩浆正向他流过来。
6It threatened to surround him completely, but Tazieff managed to escape /ɪ'skeɪp/ v. 逃脱 just in time.
眼看就要将他团团围住，但塔捷耶夫还是设法及时逃离了。
7He waited until the volcano became quiet and he was able to return two days later.
他等到火山平静下来，两天以后又返回去了。
8This time, he managed to climb into the mouth of Kituro so that he could take photographs and measure temperatures.
这次他设法爬进了基图罗火山口，以便能拍照，测量温度。
9Tazieff has often risked his life in this way. He has been able to tell us more about active volcanoes than any man alive /ə'laɪv/ adj. 活着的.
塔捷耶夫经常这样冒生命危险去研究火山。他已能够告诉我们关于活火山的很多情况，比任何在世的人都多。
