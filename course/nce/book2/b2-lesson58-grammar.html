🔍 A blessing in disguise? 是因祸得福吗？

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 58 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 58（课本印刷页 268-271）· 14 个生词和短语 · 课文 9 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则「被诅咒的树」的民间传闻。语言点集中在 It is said that … / be said to do sth.、强调结构 it is … that …、现在完成进行时，以及 have sth. done 使役结构。

¶ 1 · 被诅咒的树（句 1-2）

句子语法点
The tiny village of Frinley is said to possess a 'cursed tree'.be said to do sth. 据说……（＝ It is said that …）· possess 拥有 · a cursed tree 一棵被诅咒的树（curse + -ed）
Because the tree was mentioned in a newspaper, the number of visitors to Frinley has now increased.原因状语从句 because 引导 · be mentioned 被提到（被动语态）· the number of ……的数量（谓语用单数）· 现在完成时 has increased

¶ 2 · 恶名远扬（句 3-5）

句子语法点
The tree was planted near the church fifty years ago, but it is only in recent years that it has gained an evil reputation.强调结构 it is … that … 强调时间状语 in recent years · gain a reputation 获得名声 · evil 坏的，邪恶的
It is said that if anyone touches the tree, he will have bad luck; if he picks a leaf, he will die.It is said that … 据说…… · 条件状语从句 if 引导（两个并列）· 用 he 指代 anyone（传统英语用法）
Many villagers believe that the tree has already claimed a number of victims.宾语从句 that 引导 · claim a victim 夺去一条生命（claim 此处意为「以……为其后果」）· a number of 许多（＋可数名词复数）

¶ 3 · 牧师的拒绝（句 6-7）

句子语法点
The vicar has been asked to have the tree cut down, but so far he has refused.被动语态 + 使役结构 has been asked to have sth. done：被要求让人把某物…… · have the tree cut down 让人把树砍倒 · so far 到目前为止（与完成时连用）· refuse 拒绝
He has pointed out that the tree is a useful source of income, as tourists have been coming from all parts of the country to see it.point out 指出 · 宾语从句 that 引导 · a source of income 一项收入来源 · 原因状语从句 as 引导 · 现在完成进行时 have been coming（从全国各地不断涌来）

¶ 4 · 无人遭殃（句 8-9）

句子语法点
In spite of all that has been said, the tourists have been picking leaves and cutting their names on the tree-trunk.in spite of 尽管 · all that has been said 所说的一切（定语从句）· 现在完成进行时 have been picking / cutting（一直不停地在做）
So far, not one of them has been struck down by sudden death!so far 到目前为止 · not one of them 他们中没有一个（谓语用单数）· be struck down by 被……击倒（strike 的过去分词 struck）· sudden death 暴死 · 结尾以「至今无一人遭殃」反讽传闻

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
be said to do sth.据说……：The tiny village of Frinley is said to possess a cursed tree。
It is said that …据说……：It is said that if anyone touches the tree, he will have bad luck。
gain a reputation获得名声：it has gained an evil reputation。
have sth. done让人做某事：have the tree cut down。
point out指出：He has pointed out that the tree is a useful source of income。
a source of income一项收入来源：the tree is a useful source of income。
in spite of尽管：In spite of all that has been said…。
be struck down by被……击倒：not one of them has been struck down by sudden death。

三语法核心总结

It is said that …
据说……（＝ be said to do sth.）
强调结构
it is only in recent years that …
现在完成进行时
have been coming / have been picking
have sth. done
让人把某物……（砍倒、剪短等）
  • be said to do sth. 与 It is said that …：The village is said to possess a cursed tree. 与 It is said that the village possesses a cursed tree. 意思相同。前者更简洁，主语就是被谈论的对象；后者是「据说……」的常规句型。本课两种句型交替出现，可对照体会。
  • it is only in recent years that …：这是强调结构 it is … that …，被强调的部分是时间状语 in recent years。若不用强调结构，原句为 it has gained an evil reputation only in recent years。强调结构的作用是把「只在近几年」突出来。
  • have the tree cut down 的使役结构：have + 宾语 + 过去分词，表示「让某事被做」。句中主语（the vicar）并不亲自砍树，而是让人去砍。同类结构还有 have my hair cut（理发）、have the house painted（请人粉刷房子）。
