新概念英语第二册 Lesson 56 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 56（课本印刷页 260-263）· 10 个生词和短语 · 课文 12 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则「老爷车比赛」的记叙文。语言点集中在 一般现在时的被动语态（is held）、a great deal of / a great many 这组量词、分词短语作状语/定语，以及 no less + 形容词 的用法。

¶ 1 · 赛前（句 1-3）

句子语法点
Once a year, a race is held for old cars.一般现在时的被动语态 is held：每年举行一次（陈述惯例）· once a year 每年一次 · a race for old cars 老爷车比赛
A lot of cars entered for this race last year and there was a great deal of excitement just before it began.enter for 报名参加 · a lot of ＝ lots of · a great deal of 大量的（修饰不可数名词）· just before 就在……之前
One of the most handsome cars was a Rolls-Royce Silver Ghost.one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词 · handsome 漂亮的（可形容事物外观）· Rolls-Royce 罗尔斯-罗伊斯（英国名车）

¶ 2 · 古怪的老爷车（句 4-6）

句子语法点
The most unusual car was a Benz which had only three wheels.定语从句 which 引导，修饰 a Benz · had only three wheels 是当时的事实状态 · unusual 不寻常的
Built in 1885, it was the oldest car taking part.Built in 1885 过去分词短语作状语（等于 As it was built in 1885）· taking part 现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 the oldest car
After a great many loud explosions, the race began.a great many 许多（修饰可数名词复数）· explosion 爆炸声（这里指老爷车发动的轰鸣）

¶ 3 · 途中（句 7-9）

句子语法点
Many of the cars broke down on the course and some drivers spent more time under their cars than in them!break down 出故障，抛锚 · 比较结构 more … than … · 幽默点：司机待在车底下的时间比坐在车里的时间还长
A few cars, however, completed the race.a few 有几个（肯定语气，与 few 含义相反）· however 然而（作插入语）· complete the race 跑完全程
The winning car reached a speed of forty miles an hour — much faster than any of its rivals.the winning car 获胜的车 · reach a speed of 达到……的速度 · much 修饰比较级，表示程度（……得多）· rival 竞争对手

¶ 4 · 冲下坡（句 10-12）

句子语法点
It sped downhill at the end of the race and its driver had a lot of trouble trying to stop it.speed 的过去式 sped（也可用 speeded）· downhill 下坡 · have trouble (in) doing sth. 做某事有困难 · at the end of 在……的末尾
The race gave everyone a great deal of pleasure.give sb. sth. 给某人某物 · a great deal of pleasure 极大的乐趣（修饰不可数名词）
It was very different from modern car races but no less exciting.be different from 与……不同 · no less + 形容词 ＝ 同样……，并不比……逊色 · but 表转折

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
once a year每年一次：Once a year, a race is held for old cars。
enter for报名参加：A lot of cars entered for this race last year。
a great deal of大量的（修饰不可数名词）：there was a great deal of excitement。
a great many许多（修饰可数名词复数）：After a great many loud explosions。
break down出故障，抛锚：Many of the cars broke down on the course。
reach a speed of达到……的速度：reached a speed of forty miles an hour。
have trouble doing sth.做某事有困难：its driver had a lot of trouble trying to stop it。
no less + adj.同样……：very different from modern car races but no less exciting。

三语法核心总结

一般现在时被动
is held（陈述每年举行的惯例）
量词
a great deal of（不可数）/ a great many（可数）
分词短语
Built in 1885… / taking part
no less + adj.
同样……，并不比……逊色
  • a great deal of 与 a great many：前者修饰不可数名词，后者修饰可数名词复数。本课 there was a great deal of excitement（激动，不可数），After a great many loud explosions（爆炸声，可数）。同类量词还有 a lot of（皆可）、a great number of（可数）。
  • no less exciting 的妙处：no less ＋ 形容词 ＝ 「同样……」，It was very different from modern car races but no less exciting. 意为「它和现代汽车比赛很不一样，但同样激动人心」。若说 not less exciting 则偏重「不少于」，语气不同。
  • Built in 1885 是过去分词短语作状语：过去分词短语作状语时，其逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致。Built in 1885, it was the oldest car taking part. 中 Built 的逻辑主语就是 it（the car），相当于 As it was built in 1885。
