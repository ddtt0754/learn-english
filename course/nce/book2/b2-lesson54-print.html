Lesson 54  Sticky fingers 粘糊的手指
早饭后，我送孩子们上学，然后就去了商店。

作者1. After breakfast, I sent the children to school and then I went to the shops.

作者2. It was still early when I returned home.

作者3. The children were at school, my husband was at work and the house was quiet.

作者4. So I decided to make some meat pies /paɪz/ n. 馅饼.

作者5. In a short time I was busy mixing /'mɪksɪŋ/ v. 混合，拌和 butter and flour and my hands were soon covered with sticky /'stɪki/ adj. 黏的 pastry /'peɪstri/ n. 面糊.

作者6. At exactly that moment, the telephone rang.

作者7. Nothing could have been more annoying /ə'nɔɪɪŋ/ adj. 恼人的.

作者8. I picked up the receiver /rɪ'siːvə/ n. 电话的话筒 between two sticky fingers /'fɪŋɡəz/ n. 手指 and was dismayed /dɪs'meɪd/ v. 失望，泄气 when I recognized /'rekəɡnaɪzd/ v. 认出，听出 the voice of Helen Bates.

作者9. It took me ten minutes to persuade /pə'sweɪd/ v. 说服，劝说 her to ring back later.

作者10. At last I hung up the receiver. What a mess /mes/ n. 乱七八糟!

作者11. There was pastry on my fingers, on the telephone, and on the door-knobs /'dɔːnɒbz/ n. 门把手.

作者12. I had no sooner got back to the kitchen than the doorbell rang loud enough to wake the dead.

作者13. This time it was the postman and he wanted me to sign /saɪn/ v. 签字 for a registered /'redʒɪstəd/ v. 挂号邮寄 letter!

📄 整篇中文翻译（参考译文）

作者1. 早饭后，我送孩子们上学，然后就去了商店。

作者2. 我回到家时，时间还早。

作者3. 孩子们在上学，我丈夫在上班，家里清静得很。

作者4. 于是我决定做些肉馅饼。

作者5. 不一会儿我就忙着调拌起黄油和面粉来，很快双手就沾满了黏黏的面糊。

作者6. 恰恰就在这时，电话铃响了。

作者7. 没有比这更烦人的了。

作者8. 我用两个沾满面糊的手指捏起了话筒，当我听出是海伦·贝茨的声音时，非常丧气。

作者9. 我用了10分钟的时间才说服她过会儿再来电话。

作者10. 我终于挂上了电话。真是糟透了！

作者11. 我的手指上、电话机上以及门的把手上，都沾上了面糊。

作者12. 我刚回到厨房，门铃又响了起来，响声足以把死人唤醒。

作者13. 这次是邮递员，他要我签收一封挂号信！

✅ 词汇全覆盖检查：本课生词共 14 个（sticky / finger / pie / mix / pastry / annoying / receiver / dismay / recognize / persuade / mess / doorknob / sign / register）全部嵌入课文句子并加粗释义：pies（pie）句 4，mixing（mix）、sticky、pastry 句 5，annoying 句 7，receiver、fingers（finger）、dismayed（dismay）、recognized（recognize）句 8，persuade 句 9，mess 句 10，door-knobs（doorknob）句 11，sign、registered（register）句 13。
