🔍 In or out? 进来还是出去？

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 59 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 59（课本印刷页 272-275）· 8 个生词和短语 · 课文 11 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则「聪明反被聪明误」的狗故事。语言点集中在 used to do sth.、would do sth.（表过去习惯）、every time / as soon as / until 等时间状语从句，以及 so … (that) … 结果状语从句。

¶ 1 · 门口的狗（句 1-2）

句子语法点
Our dog, Rex, used to sit outside our front gate and bark.used to do sth. 过去常常做某事（现在已不如此）· Rex 是狗的名字（作同位语）· outside our front gate 在我们前门外
Every time he wanted to come into the garden he would bark until someone opened the gate.时间状语从句 every time 引导（＝ whenever）· would + 动词原形（表过去习惯）· until 直到……为止

¶ 2 · 学会开门（句 3-4）

句子语法点
As the neighbours complained of the noise, my husband spent weeks training him to press his paw on the latch to let himself in.原因状语从句 as 引导 · complain of 抱怨 · spend time (in) doing sth. 花时间做某事 · let oneself in 自己进来 · latch 门闩（不是门锁）
Rex soon became an expert at opening the gate.become an expert at doing sth. 成为做某事的专家 · soon 不久 · 这是全文的第一个笑点：狗居然学会了开门

¶ 3 · 新的坏习惯（句 5-7）

句子语法点
However, when I was going out shopping last week, I noticed him in the garden near the gate.however 然而（作插入语）· 时间状语从句 when 引导 · notice sb. + 地点 注意到某人在某处
This time he was barking so that someone would let him out!目的状语从句 so that 引导 · let sb. out 把某人放出去 · this time 这一次（与上次进门的动作相对）
Since then, he has developed another bad habit.现在完成时 has developed · since then 从那时起 · develop a habit 养成一种习惯

¶ 4 · 自作自受（句 8-11）

句子语法点
As soon as he opens the gate from the outside, he comes into the garden and waits until the gate shuts.时间状语从句 as soon as 引导（一……就……）· from the outside 从外面 · wait until 等到……为止
Then he sits and barks until someone lets him out.then 然后 · until 直到……为止 · let sb. out 放某人出去 · 与句 6 的 would let him out 呼应
After this he immediately lets himself in and begins barking again.immediately 立刻 · let oneself in 自己进门 · begin doing sth. 开始做某事 · again 再一次，写出「循环往复」的荒唐
Yesterday my husband removed the gate and Rex got so annoyed we have not seen him since.remove 拆掉，取下 · 结果状语从句 so annoyed (that) …（that 被省略）· have not seen him since 从那以后就再没见过他

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
used to do sth.过去常常做某事：Our dog, Rex, used to sit outside our front gate and bark。
complain of抱怨：the neighbours complained of the noise。
spend time doing sth.花时间做某事：my husband spent weeks training him。
let oneself in自己进来：to press his paw on the latch to let himself in。
become an expert at成为……的专家：Rex soon became an expert at opening the gate。
let sb. out把某人放出去：someone would let him out。
develop a habit养成一种习惯：he has developed another bad habit。
so … (that) …如此……以致于……：Rex got so annoyed we have not seen him since。

三语法核心总结

used to do sth.
过去常常做某事（现在已不如此）
would + 动词原形
表过去习惯性动作
时间状语从句
every time / as / as soon as / until
so … (that) …
结果状语从句，that 可省略
  • used to do sth. 与 would do sth.：两者都可表示过去的习惯，但 used to 强调「现在不再如此」，would 只强调过去反复的动作。本课 Our dog used to sit outside… 暗示狗曾经如此，正好与后文的「新习惯」形成对照。
  • every time 直接引导从句：Every time he wanted to come into the garden… 中的 every time 是连词，等于 whenever，直接引导时间状语从句。同类还有 the moment、the first time 等。
  • so annoyed (that) we have not seen him since：so + 形容词 + (that) 从句，表示「如此……以致于……」。口语中 that 常省略，于是出现 so annoyed we … 连着两个动词短语的情况，别误读为两个并列句。
