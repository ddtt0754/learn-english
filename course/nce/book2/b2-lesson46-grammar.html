🔍 Expensive and uncomfortable 既昂贵又受罪

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 46 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 46（课本印刷页 206-209）· 12 个生词和短语 · 课文 10 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则偷渡被发现的新闻。语言点集中在 被动语态（be astonished at / be discovered / be arrested / be confined to / be ordered to）、It occurred to sb. to do sth. 句型、以及 a number of、account for、on top of、admit doing sth. 等短语。

¶ 1 · 卸货（句 1-2）

句子语法点
When a plane from London arrived at Sydney airport, workers began to unload a number of wooden boxes which contained clothing.时间状语从句 when 引导 · a number of ＋ 复数名词 ＝ 若干 · which contained clothing 是定语从句，修饰 boxes
No one could account for the fact that one of the boxes was extremely heavy.同位语从句 that 引导，说明 the fact 的内容 · account for 说明……的原因；no one could account for 谁也弄不清 · extremely 极其，修饰 heavy

¶ 2 · 开箱（句 3-4）

句子语法点
It suddenly occurred to one of the workers to open up the box.固定句型 It occurred to sb. to do sth. ＝ 某人突然想到做某事；it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to open up the box · open up 打开
He was astonished at what he found.被动语态 be astonished at 对……感到惊讶 · 宾语从句 what 引导，作介词 at 的宾语（what ＝ the thing which）

¶ 3 · 箱中有人（句 5-6）

句子语法点
A man was lying in the box on top of a pile of woollen goods.lie in the box 躺在箱子里 · on top of 在……之上 · a pile of 一堆 · goods 货物，永远用复数
He was so surprised at being discovered that he did not even try to run away.结果状语从句 so … that … · being discovered 是动名词的被动式，作介词 at 的宾语 · not even 甚至都不

¶ 4 · 认罪与代价（句 7-10）

句子语法点
After he was arrested, the man admitted hiding in the box before the plane left London.被动语态 be arrested 被捕 · admit doing sth. 承认做过某事（admit 后接动名词，不接不定式）
He had had a long and uncomfortable trip, for he had been confined to the wooden box for over eighteen hours.had had 是 have 的过去完成时（＝经历过）· for 是并列连词，表原因 · 被动语态 be confined to 被关在……里 · over ＝ more than
The man was ordered to pay £3,500 for the cost of the trip. / The normal price of a ticket is £2,000!be ordered to do sth. 被命令做某事 · the cost of the trip 旅费 · 结尾用「正常票价 2,000 英镑」与他被罚的 3,500 英镑作对比，形成讽刺

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
a number of若干，许多，后接可数名词复数：a number of wooden boxes。
account for说明……的原因；no one could account for the fact 谁也弄不清这个事实。
It occurred to sb. to do sth.某人突然想到做某事：It occurred to one of the workers to open up the box.
be astonished at对……感到惊讶：He was astonished at what he found.
on top of在……之上：on top of a pile of woollen goods。
admit doing sth.承认做过某事：the man admitted hiding in the box。
be confined to被限制、被关在……里：he had been confined to the wooden box。

三语法核心总结

同位语从句
the fact that … ：that 从句说明 fact 的内容
It occurred to sb. to do
某人突然想到做某事（it 为形式主语）
动名词的被动式
being discovered 作介词宾语
admit doing
admit 后接动名词，不接不定式
  • 同位语从句与定语从句：the fact that one of the boxes was extremely heavy 中，that 从句说的是 the fact 本身的内容，叫同位语从句，that 只起连接作用、不作句子成分；而句 1 的 which contained clothing 是定语从句，which 在从句中作主语。
  • admit doing sth.：admit 后面接动名词，不接不定式。the man admitted hiding in the box（✓）；admitted to hide（✗）。同类动词还有 enjoy、finish、avoid、suggest。
  • It occurred to sb. to do sth.：这是英语中很常用的一种句型，it 是形式主语，真正的主语是后面的不定式。同类写法还有 It struck me that …、It happened that …。
