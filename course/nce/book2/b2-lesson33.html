课文对话 · Out of the darkness 冲出黑暗（课本 Lesson 33，印刷页 154-157）
1Nearly a week passed before the girl was able to explain /ɪkˈspleɪn/ v. 解释，叙述 what had happened to her.
几乎过了一个星期，那姑娘才能讲述自己的遭遇。
2One afternoon she set out from the coast /kəʊst/ n. 海岸 in a small boat and was caught in a storm /stɔːm/ n. 暴风雨.
一天下午，她乘小船从海岸出发，遇上了风暴。
3Towards /təˈwɔːdz/ prep. 朝，向 evening, the boat struck a rock /rɒk/ n. 岩石，礁石 and the girl jumped into the sea.
天将黑时，小船撞上了一块岩石，姑娘跳进了大海。
4Then she swam to the shore /ʃɔː/ n. 海岸 after spending the whole night in the water.
她游了整整一夜才游到岸边。
5During that time she covered a distance of eight miles.
在此期间，她游了 8 英里。
6Early next morning, she saw a light /laɪt/ n. 灯光 ahead /əˈhed/ adv. 在前面.
第二天凌晨，她看到前方有灯光。
7She knew she was near the shore because the light was high up on the cliffs /klɪfs/ n. 峭壁.
她知道她已接近海岸，因为灯光高高地悬挂在峭壁上。
8On arriving at the shore, the girl struggled /ˈstrʌɡld/ v. 挣扎 up the cliff towards the light she had seen.
姑娘登岸后，朝着她看到灯光的方向奋力爬上峭壁。
9That was all she remembered.
这就是她所记得的一切。
10When she woke up a day later, she found herself in hospital /ˈhɒspɪtl/ n. 医院.
一天后她醒过来时，发现自己躺在医院里。
