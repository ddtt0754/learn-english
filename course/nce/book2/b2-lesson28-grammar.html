🔍 No parking 禁止停车

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 28 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 28（课本印刷页 134-137）· 7 个生词和短语 · 课文 10 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是「用美杜莎头像吓走乱停车的人」的幽默小品。语言点集中在 现在完成时与现在完成进行（has bought / has had / has not been able to）、最高级 one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词、believe in 与 turn … into/to … 这类固定搭配。

¶ 1 · 一位相信神话的人（句 1-2）

句子语法点
Jasper White is one of those rare people who believe in ancient myths.定语从句 who believe in … 修饰 people；one of + 复数名词，谓语用单数 is · believe in 相信（某事物的存在）
He has just bought a new house in the city, but ever since he moved in, he has had trouble with cars and their owners.现在完成时 has just bought 强调刚发生 · ever since 自从……以来，主句用现在完成时 · have trouble with sb./sth. 与……有麻烦

¶ 2 · 停不进去的车（句 3-5）

句子语法点
When he returns home at night, he always finds that someone has parked a car outside his gate.when 引导时间状语从句用一般现在时表习惯 · has parked 用现在完成时表示「已经停了（并还在）」
Because of this, he has not been able to get his own car into his garage even once.because of + 名词／代词 ＝ 因为（because 后面才接句子）· even once 加强否定「连一次也没有」
Jasper has put up 'No Parking' signs outside his gate, but these have not had any effect.put up 张贴、挂起 · have an effect 起作用，否定形式 not have any effect

¶ 3 · 美杜莎头像（句 6-10）

句子语法点
Now he has put an ugly stone head over the gate.over the gate 在大门上方 · stone head 石雕头像
It is one of the ugliest faces I have ever seen.one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词 · I have ever seen 是定语从句，省略了 that，与现在完成时连用
… he told me that it was Medusa, the Gorgon.the Gorgon 是 Medusa 的同位语 · 间接引语里 is 退成 was
Jasper hopes that she will turn cars and their owners to stone.turn … to stone 把……变成石头 · hope + that 从句用将来时表示愿望
But none of them has been turned to stone yet!none of + 复数名词，谓语可用单数 · yet 用于否定句与疑问句，表示「还（没有）」

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
believe in相信（某人／某物的存在）：believe in ancient myths。
have trouble with与……有麻烦、有纠葛。
because of因为，后接名词或代词，不接句子。
have an effect起作用、有效果：these have not had any effect。
turn … to stone把……变成石头。

三语法核心总结

现在完成时
has just bought／has had／have not had：强调对现在的影响
定语从句
who believe in … 修饰 people；省略 that 的 I have ever seen
最高级结构
one of the + 最高级 + 复数名词
none of 的谓语
none of them has／have 均可，课文用单数 has
  • believe 与 believe in：believe sb. 相信某人的话；believe in sb./sth. 相信其存在或价值。本课是相信古代神话的存在，所以用 believe in。
  • because of 后面不能接句子：Because of this …（✓）／Because of he moved in …（✗，应改用 Because he moved in …）。
  • one of + 复数名词作主语时谓语用单数：one of those rare people is …；但定语从句的谓语要与先行词一致，所以是 who believe …。
