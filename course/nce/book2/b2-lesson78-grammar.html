🔍 The last one? 最后一枝吗？

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 78 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 78（课本印刷页 362-365）· 12 个生词和短语 · 课文 12 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则关于戒烟失败的幽默小品。语言点集中在 be used to doing sth. / be used to do sth. 的区分、keep on doing sth.、make an effort to do sth.，以及 as 引导的非限制性定语从句（as Brian pointed out）。

¶ 1 · 最后一枝烟（句 1-2）

句子语法点
After reading an article entitled 'Cigarette Smoking and Your Health' I lit a cigarette to calm my nerves.entitled 题为……的（过去分词作定语）· light a cigarette 点一枝烟 · calm one's nerves 镇定神经 · to calm … 目的状语
I smoked with concentration and pleasure as I was sure that this would be my last cigarette.原因状语从句 as 引导 · with concentration and pleasure 聚精会神而愉快地 · 宾语从句 that 引导 · this would be my last cigarette 这将是我的最后一枝烟（用过去将来时，因主句是过去时）

¶ 2 · 戒烟症状（句 3-4）

句子语法点
For a whole week I did not smoke at all and during this time, my wife suffered terribly.for a whole week 整整一周 · not … at all 根本不 · suffer terribly 吃尽苦头 · 幽默点：戒烟的不是「我」吃苦，而是妻子
I had all the usual symptoms of someone giving up smoking: a bad temper and an enormous appetite.the usual symptoms of 通常的症状 · someone giving up smoking 戒烟的人（动名词短语作 someone 的后置定语）· give up smoking 戒烟 · a bad temper 脾气暴躁 · an enormous appetite 食欲旺盛

¶ 3 · 朋友们的好意（句 5-6）

句子语法点
My friends kept on offering me cigarettes and cigars.keep on doing sth. 不断地做某事 · offer sb. sth. 向某人提供某物 · cigars 雪茄
They made no effort to hide their amusement whenever I produced a packet of sweets from my pocket.make no effort to do sth. 毫不努力去做某事 · 时间状语从句 whenever 引导 · produce 拿出 · a packet of sweets 一包糖果

¶ 4 · 聚会上的失守（句 7-12）

句子语法点
After seven days of this I went to a party. Everybody around me was smoking and I felt extremely uncomfortable.after seven days of this 这样过了七天 · go to a party 参加聚会 · extremely uncomfortable 极其不自在
When my old friend Brian urged me to accept a cigarette, it was more than I could bear.时间状语从句 when 引导 · urge sb. to do sth. 力劝某人做某事 · more than I could bear 超出我所能忍受的（比较结构）
I took one guiltily, lit it and smoked with satisfaction.guiltily 内疚地 · with satisfaction 心满意足地 · 三个并列谓语 took / lit / smoked 写出「瞬间失守」的三步
My wife was delighted that things had returned to normal once more.be delighted that … 为……而高兴 · return to normal 恢复正常 · 过去完成时 had returned（在妻子高兴之前已恢复）
Anyway, as Brian pointed out, it is the easiest thing in the world to give up smoking. He himself has done it lots of times!非限制性定语从句 as Brian pointed out 正如布赖恩指出的（as 指代整个主句）· the easiest thing in the world 世界上最容易的事情 · He himself has done it lots of times 他本人已戒过很多次 · 结尾以自相矛盾的「成果」收束

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
be entitled题为……的：an article entitled 'Cigarette Smoking and Your Health'。
calm one's nerves镇定神经：to calm my nerves。
give up smoking戒烟：the usual symptoms of someone giving up smoking。
keep on doing sth.不断地做某事：My friends kept on offering me cigarettes and cigars。
make no effort to do sth.毫不努力做某事：They made no effort to hide their amusement。
urge sb. to do sth.力劝某人做某事：my old friend Brian urged me to accept a cigarette。
return to normal恢复正常：things had returned to normal once more。
point out指出：as Brian pointed out。

三语法核心总结

原因状语从句 as
I smoked with concentration … as I was sure that …
keep on doing sth.
不断地做某事
make no effort to do sth.
毫不努力做某事
as 引导非限制性定语从句
as Brian pointed out
  • as 引导原因状语从句 vs as 引导定语从句：I smoked with concentration and pleasure as I was sure that this would be my last cigarette. 中的 as 表原因（因为）；而 as Brian pointed out 中的 as 是关系代词，指代整个主句（正如……）。同一个 as，靠句意和位置判断其功能。
  • give up smoking 的构成：give up 是「放弃」，后接动名词 smoking，表示「戒烟」。注意 give up 后接动名词，不接不定式。同类还有 stop smoking（停止吸烟）。
  • the usual symptoms of someone giving up smoking：句中 someone giving up smoking 是「动名词复合结构」，someone 是 giving up 的逻辑主语，整个短语作 of 的宾语，意为「戒烟者（通常）的症状」。
