🔍 Future champions 未来的冠军

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 94 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 94（课本印刷页 426-429）· 9 个生词和短语 · 课文 11 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则关于儿童游泳训练的报道。语言点集中在 被动语态（be instructed in / are lined up / are hung）、so … that … 结果状语从句（so accustomed to swimming that …）、before / until 引导的时间状语从句，以及 become expert at、be accustomed to、compete against 等固定表达。

¶ 1 · 婴儿学游泳（句 1-2）

句子语法点
Experiments have proved that children can be instructed in swimming at a very early age.宾语从句 that 引导作 proved 的宾语 · 被动语态 can be instructed in swimming · instruct sb. in sth. 在某方面指导某人 · at a very early age 在很小的年纪
At a special swimming pool in Los Angeles, children become expert at holding their breath under water even before they can walk.become expert at 变得精通于……· hold one’s breath 屏住呼吸 · under water 在水下 · 时间状语从句 before 引导 · even 加强语气，强调「甚至还在会走路之前」

¶ 2 · 习惯到能捡重物（句 3-4）

句子语法点
Babies of two months old do not appear to be reluctant to enter the water.babies of two months old 两个月的婴儿（of + 年龄作定语）· appear to be reluctant to do sth. 似乎不愿做某事 · enter the water 下水 · 用否定式「似乎并不怕」描写婴儿的天然水性
It is not long before they are so accustomed to swimming that they can pick up weights from the floor of the pool.it is not long before … 不久就……· be accustomed to (doing) sth. 习惯于……（to 是介词）· so … that … 如此……以致于……· pick up 捡起 · the floor of the pool 池底

¶ 3 · 水下三轮车赛（句 5-9）

句子语法点
A game that is very popular with these young swimmers is the underwater tricycle race.定语从句 that 引导修饰 a game · be popular with sb. 受某人欢迎 · the underwater tricycle race 水下三轮车比赛 · 主句主语被定语从句拉长，谓语 is 仍与 a game 保持一致
Tricycles are lined up on the floor of the pool seven feet under water.被动语态 are lined up 被排成一行 · seven feet under water 在七英尺深的水下（数字作状语）
The children compete against each other to reach the other end of the pool.compete against each other 互相竞争 · to reach the other end of the pool 不定式表目的 · the other end 另一端
Many pedal their tricycles, but most of them prefer to push or drag them.pedal one's tricycle 蹬三轮车 · most of them 他们中的大多数 · prefer to do sth. 宁愿做某事 · push or drag 推或拉 · 用 but 对比两种做法，画面感很强
Some children can cover the whole length of the pool without coming up for breath even once.cover the whole length of the pool 游完全程 · without + 动名词 没有做某事 · come up for breath 浮出水面换气 · even once 即使一次（加强否定）

¶ 4 · 未来的冠军（句 10-11）

句子语法点
Whether they will ever become future Olympic champions, only time will tell.主语从句 whether 引导，置于句首 · future Olympic champions 未来的奥运冠军 · only time will tell 只有时间才能作出回答（tell 作「说明、断定」讲）
Meanwhile, they should encourage those among us who cannot swim five yards before they are gasping for air.meanwhile 与此同时 · encourage sb. 鼓励某人 · those among us 我们当中的人 · 定语从句 who 引导修饰 those · five yards 五码 · 时间状语从句 before 引导 · gasp for air 大口喘气 · 结尾以自嘲收束，与「未来的冠军」形成有趣对照

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
be instructed in在某方面受到指导：children can be instructed in swimming。
at a very early age在很小的年纪：at a very early age。
become expert at变得精通于……：children become expert at holding their breath。
hold one's breath屏住呼吸：holding their breath under water。
be reluctant to do sth.不愿做某事：do not appear to be reluctant to enter the water。
it is not long before …不久就……：It is not long before they are so accustomed …。
be accustomed to (doing) sth.习惯于……：they are so accustomed to swimming that …。
be popular with受……欢迎：A game that is very popular with these young swimmers。
be lined up被排成一行：Tricycles are lined up on the floor of the pool。
compete against与……竞争：The children compete against each other。
come up for breath浮出水面换气：without coming up for breath even once。
gasp for air大口喘气：before they are gasping for air。

三语法核心总结

被动语态
be instructed in swimming / are lined up / be lined up
so … that …
结果状语从句：so accustomed to swimming that they can pick up weights
be accustomed to + 动名词
习惯于……（to 是介词）
主语从句
Whether they will ever become future Olympic champions, only time will tell。
  • be accustomed to 的 to 是介词：后面要接名词或动名词，如 they are so accustomed to swimming that …。同类结构还有 be used to doing sth.（习惯于）、look forward to doing sth.（盼望）。注意不要与 used to do sth.（过去常常）混淆。
  • so … that … 结果状语从句：so + 形容词/副词 + that 从句，表示「如此……以致于……」。本课 They are so accustomed to swimming that they can pick up weights from the floor of the pool. 若 so 修饰的是名词短语，则要改用 such … that …。
  • whether 引导的主语从句：Whether they will ever become future Olympic champions, only time will tell. 中，whether 从句是主语，only time will tell 是主句。这类从句置于句首时，不能用 if 替换 whether。
