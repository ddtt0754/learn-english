🔍 Did you want to tell me something? 你想对我说什么吗？

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 48 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 48（课本印刷页 214-217）· 6 个生词和短语 · 课文 9 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则在牙科诊所的小幽默。语言点主要是 宾语从句（whether、how 引导）、过去完成时（had pulled out / had told / had been）、either … or … 与 be busy doing sth. 等结构，以及 in answer to、search out 等短语。

¶ 1 · 无法作答（句 1-3）

句子语法点
Dentists always ask questions when it is impossible for you to answer.时间状语从句 when 引导 · it is impossible for sb. to do sth. 某人不可能做某事 · 一般现在时表达一贯如此的现象，是全文的笑点铺垫
My dentist had just pulled out one of my teeth and had told me to rest for a while.过去完成时 had just pulled out：刚拔掉 · pull out 拔出 · for a while 一会儿 · had pulled 与 had told 并列，共用主语
I tried to say something, but my mouth was full of cotton wool.be full of 充满 · cotton wool 药棉（不可数）

¶ 2 · 医生发问（句 4-5）

句子语法点
He knew I collected match boxes and asked me whether my collection was growing.宾语从句 whether 引导，作 asked 的宾语 · collect 与 collection 是同根词，前者动词「搜集」，后者名词「收藏品」 · grow 增加，扩大
He then asked me how my brother was and whether I liked my new job in London.宾语从句 两个并列从句：how 引导与 whether 引导 · 注意从句一律用陈述语序

¶ 3 · 只好点头（句 6-7）

句子语法点
In answer to these questions I either nodded or made strange noises.either … or … 要么……要么……，连接两个并列谓语 · in answer to 作为对……的回答 · nod 点头
Meanwhile, my tongue was busy searching out the hole where the tooth had been.be busy doing sth. 忙于做某事 · search out 搜寻，找出 · where the tooth had been 是定语从句，修饰 the hole

¶ 4 · 拔错了牙（句 8-9）

句子语法点
I suddenly felt very worried, but could not say anything.feel worried 感到担心 · but 表转折 · could not say anything 什么也说不出（因为嘴里塞着药棉）
When the dentist at last removed the cotton wool from my mouth, I was able to tell him that he had pulled out the wrong tooth.at last 最后 · remove … from … 从……取出 · be able to do 终于能够做 · the wrong tooth 拔错的那颗牙（wrong 作定语时前置，意为「错误的」）

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
for a while一会儿，片刻：rest for a while。
be full of充满：my mouth was full of cotton wool。
in answer to作为对……的回答：In answer to these questions …
either … or …要么……要么……：I either nodded or made strange noises。
be busy doing sth.忙于做某事：my tongue was busy searching out the hole。
search out搜寻，找出：searching out the hole where the tooth had been。
pull out拔出：had just pulled out one of my teeth。

三语法核心总结

宾语从句
whether / how 引导，用陈述语序
过去完成时
had pulled out / had told：先发生的动作
either … or …
连接两个并列成分
the wrong tooth
拔错的那颗牙（wrong 前置作定语）
  • 宾语从句的语序：无论主句是陈述句还是疑问句，从句一律用陈述语序。He asked me whether my collection was growing.（✓）；He asked me whether was my collection growing.（✗）。这是本课最需要记牢的一点。
  • either … or … 连接两个并列成分，谓语动词通常与最近的主语保持一致（就近原则）：Either you or I am wrong. 本课中是连接两个谓语动词，不存在这个问题。
  • be busy doing sth.：busy 后跟现在分词。my tongue was busy searching out the hole 写得极生动 —— 舌头「忙着」找那个洞，与前面「说不出话」形成反差，正是全文的幽默所在。
