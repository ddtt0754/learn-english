🔍 Reward for virtue 对美德的奖赏

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 51 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 51（课本印刷页 240-243）· 8 个生词和短语 · 课文 12 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一个「节食失败」的幽默小品。语言点集中在 so … that … 结果状语从句、It was obvious that … 主语从句、as fat as ever 这类 as … as 比较结构，以及 go on a diet、pay sb. a visit、first of all 等固定短语。

¶ 1 · 决定节食（句 1-2）

句子语法点
My friend, Hugh, has always been fat, but things got so bad recently that he decided to go on a diet.结果状语从句 so + 形容词 + that 从句 · get 在此作「变得」讲（＝become）· go on a diet 节食 · Hugh 是 My friend 的同位语
He began his diet a week ago.a week ago 一星期前 · 一般过去时，交代节食开始的时间

¶ 2 · 长长的禁食单（句 3-4）

句子语法点
First of all, he wrote out a long list of all the foods which were forbidden.first of all 首先 · write out 开列，写出 · which were forbidden 是定语从句，修饰 the foods · forbid 的过去分词 forbidden 在此作形容词，意为「被禁止的」
The list included most of the things Hugh loves: butter, potatoes, rice, beer, milk, chocolate, and sweets.include 包括 · most of the things 大部分东西 · 冒号后是列举，把「爱吃的全在禁食单上」的荒谬处境摆了出来

¶ 3 · 上门探望（句 5-8）

句子语法点
Yesterday I paid him a visit.pay sb. a visit ＝ visit sb.，去看望某人（pay 后接与动词同源的名词）
I rang the bell and was not surprised to see that Hugh was still as fat as ever.ring the bell 按门铃 · be not surprised to do sth. 对做某事不感惊奇 · as + 形容词 + as ever 和以往一样……（as ever 是 as he ever was 的省略）· 宾语从句 that 引导
He led me into his room and hurriedly hid a large parcel under his desk.lead sb. into 把某人领进 · hurriedly 匆忙地（hurried + -ly）· hide sth. under 把某物藏在……下
It was obvious that he was very embarrassed.主语从句 It was obvious that … ＝ 显然……；it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 that 从句 · be embarrassed 感到尴尬

¶ 4 · 自罚自赏（句 9-12）

句子语法点
When I asked him what he was doing, he smiled guiltily and then put the parcel on the desk.宾语从句 what 引导，作 asked 的宾语 · guiltily 内疚地（guilty + -ily）· 副词修饰 smiled，写出被抓现行的神态
He explained that his diet was so strict that he had to reward himself occasionally.结果状语从句 so + 形容词 + that 从句 · reward oneself 奖赏自己 · occasionally 偶尔地 · 把「偷吃」说成「奖赏」，是本文讥讽的笔法
Then he showed me the contents of the parcel. / It contained five large bars of chocolate and three bags of sweets!the contents of the parcel 包里的东西 · five large bars of chocolate 五大块巧克力 · 结尾用具体数量与前文的「严格节食」形成强烈反差

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
go on a diet节食，实行饮食控制（＝be on a diet）：he decided to go on a diet。
first of all首先：First of all, he wrote out a long list。
write out开列，写出：wrote out a long list of all the foods。
pay sb. a visit看望某人（＝visit sb.）：Yesterday I paid him a visit。
as fat as ever像以往一样胖；as + 形容词 + as ever ＝ 和以往一样……
It was obvious that …显然……（it 作形式主语，that 从句是真正的主语）。
reward oneself奖赏自己：he had to reward himself occasionally。
virtue美德，德行（本课标题）：Reward for virtue ＝ 对美德的奖赏。

三语法核心总结

so … that …
如此……以致于……
It was obvious that …
主语从句，it 作形式主语
as … as ever
和以往一样……
pay sb. a visit
看望某人
  • It was obvious that … 是主语从句：it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 that he was very embarrassed。同类句型还有 It is important that …、It seems that …、It is said that …。
  • as fat as ever：这是 as + 形容词/副词原级 + as + 从句 的比较结构，as ever 是 as he had ever been 的省略，意思是「跟他以前一样胖」，语气上带点无奈与幽默。
  • so … that … 表结果：his diet was so strict that he had to reward himself occasionally （饮食控制得如此严格，以致他不得不偶尔奖赏自己）。注意 so 后面接形容词或副词，如果接名词短语则要用 such。
