🔍 Taken for a ride 乘车兜风

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 50 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 50（课本印刷页 236-239）· 3 个生词和短语 · 课文 12 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是作者乘公交车兜风的小幽默。语言点集中在「公共汽车」相关的固定说法（get on / get off / put sb. off / stay on）、where to get off 这类「疑问词 + 不定式」结构、as far as … 与 in that case，以及 go on an excursion、lose one's way 等短语。

¶ 1 · 上车问路（句 1-4）

句子语法点
I love travelling in the country, but I don't like losing my way.love doing sth. 喜爱做某事 · lose one's way 迷路（＝get lost）· 前后对比，点出作者的两难
I went on an excursion recently, but my trip took me longer than I expected.go on an excursion 作一次短途旅行 · take sb. + 时间 花费某人多少时间 · longer than I expected 比我预料的更长（比较级 + than 从句）
'I'm going to Woodford Green,' I said to the conductor as I got on the bus, 'but I don't know where it is.'时间状语从句 as 引导 · get on the bus 上公共汽车 · 宾语从句 where it is
'I'll tell you where to get off,' answered the conductor.疑问词 + 不定式 where to get off 作 tell 的直接宾语，相当于 tell you where you should get off · get off 下车

¶ 2 · 坐在前头（句 5-6）

句子语法点
I sat in the front of the bus to get a good view of the countryside.in the front of 在……的前部（指车厢内部前端；in front of 则指「在……前面」，车身之外）· to get a good view 是动词不定式作目的状语
After some time, the bus stopped. Looking round, I realized with a shock that I was the only passenger left on the bus.分词短语作状语 Looking round 表伴随 · realize with a shock 吃惊地意识到 · the only passenger left on the bus 车上剩下的唯一乘客（left 是过去分词作后置定语）

¶ 3 · 售票员的疏忽（句 7-10）

句子语法点
'You'll have to get off here,' the conductor said. 'This is as far as we go.'have to 不得不 · as far as ＋ 从句：远到……为止，This is as far as we go. ＝ 我们只开到这儿
'Is this Woodford Green?' I asked.一般疑问句的直接引语，用升调；作者仍在追问目的地在哪儿
'Oh dear,' said the conductor suddenly. 'I forgot to put you off.'Oh dear 哎呀（表示惊讶或抱歉）· forget to do sth. 忘记去做某事（还没做）；与 forget doing sth.（忘记做过）区别开 · put sb. off（在此）让某人下车
'It doesn't matter,' I said. 'I'll get off here.'It doesn't matter. 没关系（回应对方的道歉）· get off 下车

¶ 4 · 干脆不下（句 11-12）

句子语法点
'We're going back now,' said the conductor.be going back 要返回去 · now 在此表「这就」
'Well, in that case, I prefer to stay on the bus,' I answered.in that case 既然如此（承接对方的话）· prefer to do sth. 宁愿做某事 · stay on the bus 留在车上不下车 —— 全文的笑点

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
lose one's way迷路（＝get lost）：I don't like losing my way。
go on an excursion作一次短途旅行：I went on an excursion recently。
get on / get off上（车）/ 下（车）：as I got on the bus / get off here。
put sb. off让某人下车；（英式口语也可表示「使某人分心」）。
where to get off疑问词 + 不定式：作 tell 的宾语，＝ where you should get off。
as far as远到……为止：This is as far as we go。
in that case既然如此：Well, in that case, I prefer to stay on the bus。
take a ride乘车兜风：标题 Taken for a ride 中的 ride 意为「乘车旅行」（本课标题）。

三语法核心总结

疑问词 + 不定式
where to get off / how to do it，作宾语
in the front of
在（车厢）前部（内部）；in front of 在……前面（外部）
as far as
远到……为止
forget to do / doing
忘记要做 / 忘记做过
  • in the front of 与 in front of：in the front of the bus 指「公共汽车车厢内的前部」；in front of the bus 则指「在公共汽车前面（车身之外的马路前方）」。本课作者坐在车厢最前部看风景，故用 in the front of。
  • 疑问词 + 不定式：I'll tell you where to get off. 中 where to get off 是 tell 的直接宾语，等于 where you should get off。这种结构还可以作主语、表语，常见的有 how to do it、what to say、when to start。
  • 标题的幽默：take sb. for a ride 本是习语，意为「欺骗某人」；字面却是「带某人去兜风」。本课讲作者糊里糊涂坐过了站，一路被「兜」着走，标题正用这两层意思双关。
