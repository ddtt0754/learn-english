🔍 The end of a dream 美梦告终

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 49 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 49（课本印刷页 232-235）· 13 个生词和短语 · 课文 10 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则「美梦告终」的幽默小故事。语言点集中在 Tired of … / Glancing at … 等分词短语作状语、not … until … 句型、send sth. doing 与 sweep sth. off 这两个动词搭配，以及 although 引导的让步状语从句。

¶ 1 · 买床（句 1-2）

句子语法点
Tired of sleeping on the floor, a young man in Teheran saved up for years to buy a real bed.分词短语作状语 Tired of … ＝ Being tired of …，表原因，省略了 being · be tired of 对……感到厌倦 · save up 攒钱
For the first time in his life, he became the proud owner of a bed which had springs and a mattress.for the first time in one's life 平生第一次 · the owner of ……的主人 · which had springs and a mattress 是定语从句，修饰 a bed

¶ 2 · 搬上屋顶（句 3-4）

句子语法点
Because the weather was very hot, he carried the bed on to the roof of his house.原因状语从句 because 引导 · carry sth. on to + 地点 把某物搬到……上 · 屋顶用 roof，注意一个房子只有一个 roof
He slept very well for the first two nights, but on the third night, a storm blew up.for the first two nights 头两个晚上 · on the third night 在第三天夜里 · blow up （风暴）刮起，加剧

¶ 3 · 大风吹落（句 5-7）

句子语法点
A gust of wind swept the bed off the roof and sent it crashing into the courtyard below.a gust of wind 一阵风 · sweep sth. off 把某物从……上刮下来 · send sth. doing 使某物……（crashing 是现在分词作宾语补足语）
The young man did not wake up until the bed had struck the ground.not … until … 直到……才…… · until 从句中用过去完成时，表示「床先撞到地上，他才醒」
Although the bed was smashed to pieces, the man was miraculously unhurt.让步状语从句 although 引导 · be smashed to pieces 被摔成碎片 · miraculously 奇迹般地（miracle + -ous + -ly）· unhurt 没有受伤的（un- + hurt）

¶ 4 · 接着睡（句 8-10）

句子语法点
When he woke up, he was still on the mattress.wake up 醒来 · still 仍然 —— 床已摔碎，人却还在床垫上，这是笑点所在
Glancing at the bits of wood and metal that lay around him, the man sadly picked up the mattress and carried it into his house.分词短语作状语 Glancing at … 表伴随动作 · that lay around him 是定语从句，修饰 the bits of wood and metal
After he had put it on the floor, he promptly went to sleep again.promptly 迅速地，立刻 · go to sleep 入睡 · 结尾句写出年轻人的「随遇而安」，收束全文

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
be tired of对……感到厌倦：Tired of sleeping on the floor …
save up攒钱：saved up for years to buy a real bed。
for the first time第一次：For the first time in his life …
blow up（风暴等）刮起，加剧：a storm blew up。
sweep sth. off把某物从……上刮下：swept the bed off the roof。
be smashed to pieces被摔成碎片：the bed was smashed to pieces。
not … until …直到……才……：did not wake up until the bed had struck the ground。

三语法核心总结

分词短语作状语
Tired of … / Glancing at … 表原因、伴随
not … until …
直到……才……
send sth. doing
使某物……（现在分词作宾补）
让步状语从句
although / though 引导
  • 分词短语作状语：句 1 的 Tired of sleeping on the floor 是原因状语（＝Because he was tired of …）；句 9 的 Glancing at the bits of wood and metal 是伴随状语。分词短语的主语必须与主句主语一致，本课两处主语都是 the man。
  • not … until …：The young man did not wake up until the bed had struck the ground. 直译是「直到床撞到地上，他都没醒」，通顺的说法是「他直到床撞到地上才醒」。until 从句用过去完成时，因为「床撞地」发生在「醒来」之前。
  • sent it crashing 的结构：send 后接宾语 it，再接现在分词 crashing 作宾语补足语，表示「使某物一边……一边……」。同类结构还有 send sb. flying（把某人打得飞出去）。
