新概念英语第二册 Lesson 87 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 87（课本印刷页 398-401）· 6 个生词和短语 · 课文 10 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一段侦探对话。语言点集中在 直接引语与叙述句的语序（"…," said the man.）、suggest that … 从句用陈述语序、过去完成时与一般过去时在对话中的分工，以及 on time、break down、be taken off the line 等固定表达。

¶ 1 · 嫌疑人的说辞（句 1-3）

句子语法点
"At the time the murder was committed, I was travelling on the 8 o'clock train to London," said the man.时间状语从句 the murder was committed 用被动语态，可理解为定语从句 that/which the murder was committed 的省略 · 定语从句 定语从句修饰 the time 时 that 常可省略 · the 8 o'clock train to London 开往伦敦的八点钟的火车 · 叙述句用倒装语序 said the man
"Do you always catch such an early train?" asked the inspector.catch a train 赶火车 · such an early train 这么早的火车 · 一般疑问句直接引语 + asked the inspector 的倒装叙述句
"Of course I do," answered the man. "I must be at work at 10 o'clock. My employer will confirm that I was there on time."用 do 代替前面的 catch such an early train，避免重复 · be at work 在上班 · 宾语从句 that 引导作 confirm 的宾语 · on time 准时（注意与 in time 及时区分）

¶ 2 · 探长的追问（句 4-9）

句子语法点
"Would a later train get you to work on time?" asked the inspector.a later train 晚一点的火车 · get sb. to somewhere 使某人到达某处 · 用 would 提问，语气委婉
"I suppose it would, but I never catch a later train."I suppose 我想，我以为 · it would 承前省略 get me to work on time · never 与一般现在时连用，表示一贯如此
"At what time did you arrive at the station?"at what time 在几点钟 · arrive at the station 到达车站（arrive at 用于较小的地方，arrive in 用于城市、国家）
"At ten to eight. I bought a paper and waited for the train."at ten to eight 七点五十分（ten to eight = 差十分八点）· buy a paper 买一份报纸 · wait for the train 等火车 · 回答省略了主语和谓语
"And you didn't notice anything unusual?"notice 注意到 · anything unusual 任何异常的事情（形容词修饰 anything 等不定代词时要后置）· 用否定疑问句追问
"Of course not."of course not 当然没有（简略回答，not 代替 I didn't notice anything unusual）

¶ 3 · 揭穿谎言（句 10）

句子语法点
"I suggest," said the inspector, "that you are not telling the truth. I suggest that you did not catch the 8 o'clock train, but that you caught the 8.25 which would still get you to work on time. You see, on the morning of the murder, the 8 o'clock train did not run at all. It broke down at Ferngreen station and was taken off the line."suggest that … 后接陈述语气的宾语从句（不用 should，也不倒装）· tell the truth 说实话 · 宾语从句 三个并列的 that 从句作 suggest 的宾语 · 定语从句 which 引导修饰 the 8.25 · you see 你知道（插入语，引起对方注意）· break down 出故障，抛锚 · 被动语态 was taken off the line 被取消（不再开行）

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
catch a train赶火车：Do you always catch such an early train?
be at work在上班：I must be at work at 10 o'clock。
on time准时：confirm that I was there on time。
arrive at到达（小地方）：did you arrive at the station。
anything unusual任何异常的事情：you didn't notice anything unusual。
tell the truth说实话：you are not telling the truth。
break down出故障：It broke down at Ferngreen station。
be taken off the line被取消开行：was taken off the line。

三语法核心总结

直接引语 + 倒装叙述句
"…," said the man. / asked the inspector.
suggest that …
后接陈述语气的宾语从句
不定代词 + 形容词后置
anything unusual
被动语态
the murder was committed / was taken off the line
  • suggest that 从句的语气：suggest 表示「建议」时，从句用 (should) + 动词原形；但表示「提出、暗示、认为」时，从句用陈述语气。本课 I suggest that you are not telling the truth. 是「我认为你在撒谎」，所以用陈述语气。
  • at ten to eight：英语用「分钟 + to + 钟点」表示「差几分几点」，如 ten to eight = 7:50；用「分钟 + past + 钟点」表示「几点过几分」，如 ten past eight = 8:10。半点用 half past eight (8:30)。
  • 叙述句的倒装：直接引语后面接「said the man / asked the inspector」时，主语和谓语要倒装；但如果主语是代词，则不用倒装，如 "…," he said.。这是新闻报道和小说对话中很常见的一种句式。
