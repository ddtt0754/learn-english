🔍 Through the forest 穿过森林

新概念英语第二册 Lesson 44 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

NCE Book 2 · Lesson 44（课本印刷页 198-201）· 9 个生词和短语 · 课文 8 句

一全文逐句语法解读

本课是一则主妇勇追劫匪的新闻小故事。语言点主要是 过去完成时（先发生的动作）与 so … that … / such … that … 两个结果状语从句，以及 out of breath、catch up with、go through、be in one's possession 等固定短语。

¶ 1 · 林边遇袭（句 1-2）

句子语法点
Mrs. Anne Sterling did not think of the risk she was taking when she ran through a forest after two men.think of 想到，考虑 · she was taking 是省略了关系代词的定语从句，修饰 the risk · run after 追赶
They had rushed up to her while she was having a picnic at the edge of a forest with her children and tried to steal her handbag.过去完成时 had rushed up 表示先发生的动作 · 时间状语从句 while 引导 · at the edge of 在……边上 · had rushed 与 tried 并列，共用助动词 had（即 had tried），只是后一个 had 被省略了

¶ 2 · 包被抢走（句 3-4）

句子语法点
In the struggle, the strap broke and, with the bag in their possession, both men started running through the trees.in the struggle 在争夺中 · with the bag in their possession 是 with 复合结构，表伴随 · in one's possession 归某人所有 · start doing sth. 开始做某事
Mrs. Sterling got so angry that she ran after them.结果状语从句 so + 形容词 + that 从句：如此……以致于……

¶ 3 · 紧追不舍（句 5-6）

句子语法点
She was soon out of breath, but she continued to run.out of breath 上气不接下气 · continue to do sth. 继续做某事 · but 表转折
When she caught up with them, she saw that they had sat down and were going through the contents of the bag, so she ran straight at them.宾语从句 that 引导，从句里 had sat down 与 were going through 并列 · catch up with 赶上，追上 · go through 翻看，检查 · run straight at 直冲过去

¶ 4 · 劫匪逃跑（句 7-8）

句子语法点
The men got such a fright that they dropped the bag and ran away.结果状语从句 such + a + 名词 + that 从句：如此……以致于…… · get a fright 吓一跳
'The strap needs mending,' said Mrs. Sterling later, 'but they did not steal anything.'need doing ＝ need to be done，主动形式表被动意义 · 直接引语中插入 said Mrs. Sterling later，用倒装语序

二固定搭配与重点词组

短语用法与例句
run after追赶：she ran through a forest after two men。
at the edge of在……的边上：at the edge of a forest。
in one's possession归某人所有，在某人的手里：with the bag in their possession。
out of breath上气不接下气：She was soon out of breath.
catch up with赶上，追上：When she caught up with them …
go through翻看，检查；也可表示「经历」：going through the contents of the bag。
get a fright吓一跳：The men got such a fright that they dropped the bag.
need doing＝ need to be done，需要被做：The strap needs mending.

三语法核心总结

so … that …
so + 形容词/副词 + that 从句
such … that …
such + a + 名词 + that 从句
过去完成时
had rushed up / had sat down：先发生的动作
need doing
主动形式表被动：The strap needs mending.
  • so … that … 与 such … that …：so 后面接形容词或副词（so angry that …）；such 后面接名词短语（such a fright that …）。判断口诀：so 后是「形/副」，such 后是「名」。
  • need doing ＝ need to be done：The strap needs mending. 字面上是主动，意思却是「包带需要被修理」。同类动词还有 want、require：The room wants cleaning. / The car requires repairing.
  • 过去完成时的「先发生」：句 1 用 she was taking（正在冒的风险），句 2 用 had rushed up（先冲上来），句 6 用 had sat down（先坐下了）。凡是在另一个过去动作之前就已完成的动作，都用过去完成时。
