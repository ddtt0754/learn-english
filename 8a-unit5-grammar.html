一全文逐句语法解读

¶ 烹饪方法

句子要点语法点
Cooking is not difficult; it is simple and fun.分号 · simple / fun 形容词
According to my mother, the secret of cooking is love.According to · the secret of
She cuts up onions and peppers, mashes potatoes, and mixes flour with milk.cuts up / mashes / mixes 烹饪动词

¶ 感恩节大餐

句子要点语法点
Last Thanksgiving, we cooked a big dinner together.一般过去时 · Thanksgiving
We made pancakes and a pizza with cheese and cream.一般过去时 · with cheese

¶ 梦想

句子要点语法点
My dream is to study cooking at a university or college.to study 不定式作表语
I want to be a cook. Maybe I will open a small restaurant one day.want to be · 一般将来时 will

¶ 结束语

句子要点语法点
Try to cook for your family. Every meal you make with love is delicious.Try to · 祈使句
