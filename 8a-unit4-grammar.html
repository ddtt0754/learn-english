一全文逐句语法解读

¶ 神奇植物

句子要点语法点
Moss grows on wet stones, and redwoods stand tall in the forest.一般现在时 · on wet stones
Bamboo can grow as tall as a building!as ... as 同级比较 · 情态 can
We can see roses, peonies and lotuses in the garden.列举 3 种花 · in the garden

¶ 春天与昆虫

句子要点语法点
In spring, butterflies appear and fly among the flowers.In spring · fly among
Small bees pass by, collecting honey for their hive.pass by · 现在分词作伴随状语 collecting

¶ 神奇动物

句子要点语法点
A cheetah can run very fast, and a tiny ant can carry food bigger than itself.比较级 bigger than · can 能力
Birds fly high, and some can even cross continents.cross continents · even 递进

¶ 保护自然

句子要点语法点
However, some animals are in danger, and we feel disappointed to see them disappear.However 转折 · in danger
Take a walk in the park, listen to the birds and feel the green world.祈使句 x3 · take a walk
