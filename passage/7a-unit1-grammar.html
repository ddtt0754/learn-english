一语法要点逐段解读

¶ 开场

句子要点语法点
Good morning… Today, my topic is You and Me.问候语 · topic = 主题

¶ 自我介绍

句子要点语法点
Hello, everyone! Hey, let me tell you about myself.祈使句 let me tell · Hey 口语呼语
My full name is Chen Ming.主系表 · full name = 全名
My first name is Ming, and my last name is Chen.and 并列 · first name / last name 姓名表达
I am in Grade 7, and I am a new classmate in Class 1.in Grade 7 年级表达
Our class teacher is Miss Li, and she is very kind.主系表 · class teacher = 班主任
I am from China, a big country with a long history.be from · with a long history 介词短语

¶ 交朋友

句子要点语法点
On my first day, I wanted to make friends with my new classmates.一般过去时 · make friends with
We got to know each other step by step.一般过去时 · get to know · each other
I asked each student about their name and age.一般过去时 · ask about
Both of my new friends and I like the guitar and tennis.both ... and · like + 并列宾语
Sometimes I make a mistake when I speak English.when 时间状语 · make a mistake

¶ 校园生活

句子要点语法点
Our classroom is full of laughter and songs.be full of = 充满
We have a school band, and my friends play the guitar in it.play the + 乐器（定冠词规则）
Even on rainy days, we are happy together.Even on 递进 · together 副词

¶ 结束语

句子要点语法点
A good friend is a treasure.主系表 · treasure 隐喻
Let us say hello with a smile, get to know each other, and enjoy our school life together!祈使句 + 并列 x3 · with a smile

二固定搭配分类

核心短语

短语释义
make friends with与…交朋友
get to know认识
each other互相
full name / last name / first name全名/姓/名
make a mistake犯错误
the same as与…相同
both … and…和…都
play the + 乐器演奏（乐器前加 the）

三语法总结

时态

时态次数典型例句
一般现在时主体I am / we have / I post
一般过去时3 处wanted / got to know / asked

以一般现在时为主（自我介绍和日常），穿插一般过去时回忆开学第一天。

统计

时态
2 种（一般现在/一般过去）
从句
when 时间状语(1处)
情态
无（七年级上基础）

备考要点

  • be 动词三形式：am/is/are——人称一致（I am / he is / they are）
  • play 用法：play + the + 乐器 vs play + 球类（无 the）——中考常考
  • make 短语：make friends / make a mistake——中考短语辨析高频词
