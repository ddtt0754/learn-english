¶ 1 · 开场

句子语法点

¶ 2 · 天空与星球

句子语法点
Look at the sky!祈使句 Look at + 名词（无主语）
In the daytime, we can see clouds and the sun.In the daytime 时间状语 · 情态动词 can + 原形 · and 并列
At night, the moon and stars shine.At night 时间状语 · 主谓宾 · the moon and stars 并列主语（复数）
The earth is our home planet, and Mars is another planet.主系表 × 2 · and 并列句 · another 形容词 另一个
From space, we can see the blue ocean and green land.From space 介词短语 · 情态动词 can + 原形 · blue / green 形容词
The sun rises at sunrise and sets at sunset, and time goes on.三单 rises / sets / goes · at sunrise / at sunset · go on 继续
Are there aliens in space?There be 一般疑问句 Are there + 复数？
That is a big question!主系表 · big question 大问题

¶ 3 · 太空探索

句子语法点
Astronauts fly into outer space by spaceship.主谓宾 · fly into 飞入 · by spaceship 乘宇宙飞船（by + 交通工具）
Chinese astronauts are called taikonauts.被动语态 are called（be + 过去分词）= 被称为 · taikonauts 中国航天员
They live in the space station.主谓宾 · the space station 空间站
A rover studies the soil of Mars, and a satellite goes around the earth.三单 studies / goes · go around 环绕 · and 并列句
With a telescope, we can see far into space.With a telescope 介词短语 · 情态动词 can + 原形 · far into space 副词短语
Astronauts take cloth and toothpaste to the space station.take sth to 把某物带到 · and 并列 两宾语
Even a small marble can show how planets move.情态动词 can + 原形 · how 宾语从句 how planets move · Even 甚至

¶ 4 · 结束语

句子语法点
My friends, space is the final frontier.主系表 · final 形容词 最后的 · frontier 边疆
Every month, scientists discover new things.Every month 时间状语 · 主谓宾 · discover 发现
One day, we may live on Mars or the moon.情态动词 may + 原形（表可能）· One day 有一天 · or 选择并列
Let us learn about space and dream big!祈使句 Let us + 动词原形 × 2 · dream big 大胆梦想

三语法核心总结

📊 时态与句型分布

项目出现情况典型例句
一般现在时全文主体（14+ 句）The sun rises at sunrise.
情态动词 can / may4 处we can see / we may live
被动语态 are called1 处（启蒙）Chinese astronauts are called taikonauts.

演讲稿以一般现在时介绍太空常识，重点为情态动词（can 能力 / may 可能）、被动语态（are called，六年级启蒙）、how 宾语从句（how planets move）与There be 疑问句（Are there aliens?），科普性强。

🎯 中考备考要点（四年级基础）

  • 被动语态启蒙：are called = 被称为，be + 过去分词
  • by + 交通工具：by spaceship，中间不加 the
  • how 宾语从句：can show how planets move，从句用陈述语序
  • There be 疑问句：Are there aliens in space? be 动词提前
  • may 表可能：we may live on Mars，比 will 语气弱
  • at sunrise / at sunset：固定搭配，日出/日落时
