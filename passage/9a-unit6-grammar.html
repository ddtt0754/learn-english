一全文逐句语法解读

¶ 1 · 开场

句子语法点
Good morning, my dear teachers and friends!问候语
Since ancient times, human beings have dreamed of flying beyond the earth.Since + 时间 完成时标志 · 现在完成时 have dreamed · dream of doing
We look at the stars and ask: what is out there?一般现在时 · 直接引语 what is out there

¶ 2 · 太空科技

句子语法点
With a telescope, we can see far into the night sky.With + 工具 = 借助 · 情态 can · far 副词
Our satellites circle the earth, and rockets launch spacecraft into space.一般现在时 · and 并列 · circle = 环绕 · launch … into
Some spaceships are manned, and some are unmanned, but all of them need great craft to build.被动意义 (are manned) · and/but 复合并列 · to build 不定式
A rover can explore the surface of Mars.情态 can · the surface of · explore 及物动词
Astronauts must operate every machine carefully and maintain the station every day.情态 must · and 并列 · every day
When they work on board, their movement is slow and free.when 时间状语从句 · on board = 在飞船上 · slow and free 形容词并列
On the control board, hundreds of buttons light up.On + 地点 · hundreds of · light up = 亮起
They have to tie themselves to their beds, or they may float away in their sleep.have to = 必须 · tie … to · or 否则 · may 可能 · in one's sleep
Astronauts are often asleep at first, but it is hard to fall asleep in a weightless room.主系表 · at first · but 转折 · it is + adj. + to do · fall asleep

¶ 3 · 太空任务

句子语法点
A space mission is never easy.主系表 · never 半否定
It requires years of training, and it can cost billions or even trillions of dollars.and 并列 · 情态 can · billions / trillions of
Scientists often cooperate with each other to finish the work.一般现在时 · cooperate with · to finish 目的
In case of bad weather, they may have to cancel the launch.In case of = 万一 · may have to = 可能不得不
Before landing, they study the geology of Mars.Before + v-ing · 一般现在时
After every trip, they survey the data and publish their findings in reports.After 时间状语 · publish … in = 发表在
Some people say the idea of space travel is crazy, but every great dream starts with a brave idea.省略 that 的宾语从句 · but 转折 · starts with
The universe is full of mysterious things: the solar system, the galaxy, and strange exoplanets far beyond our sun.be full of = 充满 · 冒号 列举 · far beyond = 远超
Even ancient people believed that gods lived in the sky.that 宾语从句 · 一般过去时（间接引语）
…Voyager 1 has travelled far beyond our galaxy, and it is flying towards Proxima Centauri.现在完成时 · and 并列 · 现在进行时 · towards 介词
No one has set foot on another planet yet, but the dream is getting closer.现在完成时（否定） · set foot on · yet 完成时标记 · 现在进行时
Mars is not yet crowded with people, but one day, human beings may live there.主系表 · crowded with · but 转折 · may 可能
Someday, a young astronaut like Ann will take us to a completely new world.一般将来时 · like = 像 / 例如 · take … to · completely 副词

¶ 4 · 结束语

句子语法点
My friends, space is the new frontier.主系表 · frontier = 疆界/前沿
Let us keep dreaming, keep learning, and never stop exploring!祈使句 · keep doing × 2 · and 并列 · stop doing

三语法核心总结

📊 时态分布

时态出现次数典型例句
一般现在时主体satellites circle / astronauts must / scientists cooperate
现在完成时3 处have dreamed / has travelled / has set foot on
一般将来时1 处will take us
现在进行时2 处is flying / is getting closer

一般现在时为主体（陈述事实），穿插现在完成时强调持续性（从古至今的梦想/已完成的旅行），现在进行时描绘动态画面（正在飞行/越来越近）。

📊 语法专项统计

从句类型
2 种（when 时间状语 / that 宾语从句）
语态
2 处被动意义（are manned / are unmanned）
非谓语
dream of doing / Before + v-ing / to do 目的状语 2 处
情态动词
can（3 次）/ may（3 次）/ must（1 次）/ have to（2 次）——情态丰富度最高
特殊结构
it is + adj. + to do / or 否则（表否定条件）/ keep doing vs stop doing
朗读时长
约 2.5 分钟

🎯 中考备考要点

  • 现在完成时 vs 一般过去时：has travelled（至今仍飞） vs. Ancient people believed（过去认为但已改变）——中考必考
  • have to vs must：have to 表客观必须 / must 表主观必须（中考辨析）
  • or 的特殊用法：Tie themselves…, or they may float away → or = 否则（等同于 if not）
  • keep doing vs stop doing：keep dreaming（继续做梦）vs stop dreaming（停止做梦）→ 完形填空经典
  • 数量短语：hundreds / billions / trillions of → 有 s 则加 of（与具体数字用法对比）
