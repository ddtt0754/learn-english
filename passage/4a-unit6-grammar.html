🔍 Changing for the Seasons

一全文逐句语法解读

以下按段落逐句拆解，标注核心语法点与句式结构（本文重点为四季与衣物词汇、名词性物主代词 mine 与 特殊疑问句 Whose / Which）。

¶ 1 · 开场

句子语法点
Good morning, my dear teachers and friends!问候语 · dear 形容词 修饰 teachers / friends
Today, my topic is Changing for the Seasons.主系表 · topic 主语 · 演讲题目（斜体专有标题）

¶ 2 · 春夏

句子语法点
There are four seasons in a year: spring, summer, autumn and winter.There be · there are + 复数 · in a year 时间状语 · 冒号列举四季
In spring, I wear a shirt or a T-shirt.In + 季节作时间状语 · 主谓宾 · or 选择并列 · wear 穿（动词）
In summer, my sister wears a pretty dress.主谓宾 · wears 三单（主语 my sister）· pretty 形容词 漂亮的

¶ 3 · 秋冬与衣物

句子语法点
In autumn, the leaves fall from the trees.In + 季节 · 主谓宾 · leaves 不规则复数（leaf→leaves）· fall from 从……落下
Then winter comes.主谓宾 · comes 三单 · Then 副词 然后
It snows, and it is very cold.主谓宾 · snows 三单（It 指天气）· and 并列 · very cold 主系表
I put on my sweater, my coat and my gloves.put on 穿上（固定短语）· and 并列 三名词 · my 物主代词
My socks keep my feet warm.keep + 宾语 + 形容词 保持……怎么样 · socks 复数（成双成对）
“Whose coat is this?” I ask.特殊疑问句 · Whose 谁的（询问归属）· 直接引语
“It is mine,” my brother says.名词性物主代词 mine = my coat · 直接引语 · says 三单
Which season do I like best?特殊疑问句 · Which 哪一个（选择）· best 最高级 最
I like them all, because my family gets together in every season!because 原因状语从句 · get together 团聚 · them all 全部

¶ 4 · 结束语

句子语法点
My friends, seasons change, but love never changes.转折并列 but · changes 三单 · never 频率副词 从不
Let us enjoy every season!祈使句 Let us + 动词原形 · every season 每个季节
That's all. Thank you for listening!That's = That is · Thank you for + 动名词

项目出现情况典型例句

名词复数
seasons / leaves / trees / gloves / socks（不规则 leaf→leaves）
从句

朗读时长

🎯 中考备考要点（四年级基础）

  • 名词性物主代词：mine = my coat，Whose 提问用 It is mine 回答
  • 名词复数：leaf→leaves 不规则变化，socks / gloves 成对用复数
  • 特殊疑问句：Whose 问归属 / Which 问选择，助动词 do 后用动词原形
  • keep + 宾语 + 形容词：My socks keep my feet warm
  • put on vs wear：put on 强调「穿上」动作，wear 强调「穿着」状态
  • never 频率副词：love never changes，否定用 never 而非 not
