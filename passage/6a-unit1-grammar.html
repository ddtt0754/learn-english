一全文逐句语法解读

以下按段落逐句拆解，标注核心语法点与句式结构（本文为游记叙事，以一般过去时为主体，含大量不规则动词过去式与数量/距离表达）。

¶ 1 · 开场

句子语法点
Good morning, my dear teachers and friends!问候语 · dear 形容词 修饰 teachers / friends
Today, my topic is My Favourite Trip.主系表 · topic 主语 · 演讲题目（斜体专有标题）

¶ 2 · 出发（一般过去时叙事）

句子语法点
Last summer, I went to a small village with my parents.一般过去时 went（go 不规则过去式）· Last summer 时间状语 · with my parents 介词短语
The village was about ten kilometres away from our city, and we climbed a mountain about a thousand metres high.过去式 was / climbed · 距离表达 ten kilometres away from · 高度表达 a thousand metres high · and 并列
We walked through the village and saw beautiful fields and rivers.过去式 walked / saw（see 不规则）· walk through 穿过 · and 并列
The view from the top was really inspiring.主系表 · was 过去式 · really 副词 修饰形容词 · inspiring 鼓舞人心的

¶ 3 · 村庄见闻

句子语法点
We saw bamboo trees and a gingerbread house in the village.过去式 saw · and 并列 两宾语 · in the village 地点状语
We ate pumpkin soup in a nice restaurant, and the food was delicious.过去式 ate（eat 不规则）/ was · and 并列句 · delicious 形容词
We also visited an airport nearby.过去式 visited（规则 -ed）· also 副词 · nearby 副词 在附近
In the old days, people used clay to make houses, and the roads were dry and dusty.过去式 used / were（are 不规则过去式）· use sth to do 用……做 · dry and dusty 形容词并列
I took many photos with my camera, and I sent them to my friends.过去式 took（take 不规则）/ sent（send 不规则）· take photos 拍照 · send sth to sb
After the trip, I felt I had learned a lot about our beautiful country!过去完成时 had learned（过去的过去）· felt（feel 过去式）+ 宾语从句 · a lot about 关于……很多

¶ 4 · 结束语

句子语法点
That's all. Thank you for listening!That's = That is · Thank you for + 动名词

三语法核心总结

📊 时态与句型分布

项目出现情况典型例句
一般过去时全文主体（14+ 处）Last summer, I went to a small village.
过去完成时1 处（had learned）I felt I had learned a lot.
过去式标志Last summer / In the old days / AfterLast summer / In the old days

本文是典型的游记叙事：通篇一般过去时（went / saw / ate / took / was / were），结尾用过去完成时（had learned）表达「过去的过去」，六年级上学期语法进阶重点。

📊 语法专项统计

不规则过去式
go / see / eat / take / send / is / are 共 7 组
过去完成时
had learned（启蒙）1 处
距离/高度表达
ten kilometres away / a thousand metres high
固定搭配
take photos / walk through / use sth to do 等 6 组
并列句
and 多处（was...and climbed / ate...and was）
朗读时长
约 1.5~2 分钟，叙事节奏

🎯 中考备考要点（四年级基础）

  • 不规则过去式：went / saw / ate / took / sent / was / were，六年级必背清单
  • 过去完成时启蒙：had learned（在 felt 之前已经发生），了解即可
  • 距离/高度：ten kilometres away / a thousand metres high，数量 + 单位 + 形容词
  • use sth to do：used clay to make houses，不定式表目的
  • send sth to sb：sent them to my friends，双宾语介词结构
  • take photos：固定搭配，photo 复数 photos
