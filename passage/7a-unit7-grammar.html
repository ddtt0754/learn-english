¶ 开场

句子要点语法点

¶ 生日日期

句子要点语法点
When is your birthday? My birthday is on August 2nd.特殊疑问句 · on + 日期
It is a very special day for me, and my family always celebrates it.主系表 · for me · always 频度副词

¶ 生日准备

句子要点语法点
This year, I wanted to do something special.一般过去时 · something + adj. 后置定语
I wrote a shopping list and went to the store with my mum.一般过去时 · shopping list · went to
The store was having a sale. I bought a kilo of apples and some yogurt.过去进行时 was having · 一般过去时 bought · a kilo of

¶ 庆祝活动

句子要点语法点
On my birthday, my family held a big celebration.一般过去时 held · on my birthday
Everyone sang Happy Birthday in different languages.一般过去时 sang · in different languages
My grandma cooked long noodles, a symbol of long life.一般过去时 · a symbol of = …的象征

¶ 国家节日

句子要点语法点
In China, we also celebrate national festivals.In China · national festivals
National Day marks the founding of our country.一般现在时 · marks = 纪念

¶ 结束语

句子要点语法点
Birthdays are not just about cakes. They are symbols of growing up.not just about · symbols of
Cherish every birthday and enjoy every moment of life!祈使句 and 并列

三语法总结

时态

时态次数典型例句
一般过去时主体wanted / wrote / went / bought / sang / held
一般现在时3 处is / celebrates / marks
过去进行时1 处was having a sale

全册唯一以一般过去时为主体的单元——讲述已发生的生日庆祝。重点：不规则动词过去式、something + adj.。

统计

时态
3 种（一般过去为主 + 一般现在 + 过去进行）
不规则动词
went / bought / sang / held / wrote
重点
something + adj. / a symbol of / 日期表达

备考要点

  • 不规则动词过去式：go→went / buy→bought / sing→sang / hold→held——七年级上必背
  • something + adj.：something special（不定代词 + 形容词后置）——中考固定搭配
  • on + 日期：on August 2nd（具体某天用 on）vs in August（月份用 in）
