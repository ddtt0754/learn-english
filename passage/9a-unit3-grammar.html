¶ 2 · 学习方法与工具

句子语法点
As a learner, I have tried many ways to study well.As + 身份 作状语 · 现在完成时 have tried
I make flashcards to remember new words…一般现在时 · to remember 目的状语
…I do a small project on a website every month.every month 频度状语 · on a website 地点状语
Hands-on experiments make learning fun…make + 宾 + adj. 使役结构 · Hands-on 复合形容词
…I always look up new words in a dictionary.look up 动副短语 = 查阅 · always 频度副词

¶ 3 · 学习策略与心态

句子语法点
But different ways suit different people.but 转折 · 主谓宾
In general, a good learning strategy is the key.In general 连接副词 · 主系表
Do not worry about the speed at first…祈使句（否定） · worry about = 担心 · at first = 起初
…because you will master grammar rules step by step.because 原因状语从句 · 一般将来时 · step by step = 逐步
Listen to podcasts on your way home…祈使句 · on one's way home 固定短语
…try to build up your confidence.try to do · build up = 建立/增强
Do not put off today's schoolwork until tomorrow.祈使句（否定） · put off = 推迟 · until 介词
Actively join in discussions, and you will find learning enjoyable.副词修饰祈使句 · and 表结果 · find + 宾 + adj.
…you may come across hard problems, but never give up.情态动词 may · come across = 偶然遇到 · give up
Ask your teachers for guidance, and accept their feedback with thanks.祈使句 and 并列祈使 · ask … for = 向……请求 · with thanks 方式状语
In addition, summarize what you have learned every week.In addition 递进连接 · what 宾语从句 · 现在完成时
If you have a growth mindset, every mistake is a chance to improve.if 条件状语从句 · a chance to do
I suggest you follow a correct method and stay patient.suggest + 宾语从句（省略 that） · follow a method · stay + adj.
Zero progress? No! Every little step counts.修辞问句 + 强调回答 · counts = 重要/算数

¶ 4 · 学习资源与平台

句子语法点
Learning materials are all around us.主系表 · all around = 到处
The general idea is simple: every learner needs good habits and good resources.冒号 引出解释 · 主系表 · needs 第三人称单数
In part, our textbooks are the best starting point.In part = 在某种程度上 · 主系表 · starting point 起点
In addition, e-books and magazines provide more resources.主谓宾 · provide = 提供 · more 比较级
Print books still have their advantages — they are easy to carry…破折号 引出解释 · be easy to do 结构
Read a newspaper or an e-book every day…祈使句 · or 并列选择
…click on a good website to open up new worlds.click on = 点击 · to open up 目的状语
In recent years, thanks to the internet, even students in a quiet valley can…In recent years 时间状语 · thanks to = 得益于 · 情态动词 can
…take advantage of the best learning resources and study efficiently.take advantage of = 利用 · efficiently 副词

三语法核心总结

📊 时态分布

时态出现次数典型例句
一般现在时主体I make / suits / I suggest / needs
现在完成时2 处have tried / have learned
一般将来时1 处you will master

以一般现在时为主（描述学习习惯和普遍方法），穿插现在完成时（已尝试的经验）和一般将来时（信心展望）。

📊 语法专项统计

从句类型
3 种（because 原因状语 / if 条件状语 / what 宾语从句）
语态
全主动语态，无被动
非谓语
动名词作主语（3 处）+ to do 作目的状语（4 处）
祈使句
7 处（含否定形式）——学习方法类演讲高频句式
特殊结构
make/find + 宾 + adj. / suggest + 从句 / 修辞问句
朗读时长
约 2.5 分钟

🎯 中考备考要点

  • 动名词作主语：Reading aloud improves… → 中考常出"选填动词形式"题（playing / to play）
  • suggest + 从句：I suggest you follow… → 虚拟语气考点，从句用 (should) + 动词原形
  • 祈使句 + and/or + 陈述句：Actively join in…, and you will… → "祈使句+and" 等同于 if 从句
  • 非谓语辨析：try to do（努力做） vs. try doing（尝试做）；a chance to do vs. a chance of doing
  • 频度副词位置：always/never 在实义动词前（always look up），在 be 动词后（learning is always…）
