一全文逐句语法解读

¶ 开场

句子要点语法点
Good morning… Today, my topic is Home Sweet Home.问候语

¶ 家务

句子要点语法点
On weekends, I sort the rubbish, clean the bathroom and tidy my bedroom.一般现在时 · sort / tidy
I hang up my clothes after washing them.hang up = 挂起 · after washing 介词+动名词

¶ 家庭活动

句子要点语法点
I borrow books from my friend and plan a family movie night.borrow ... from · plan a night
I invite my classmates to visit, so we prepare food ahead of time.一般现在时 · so 结果 · ahead of time

¶ 社区互助

句子要点语法点
Our community is like a big family.is like 比喻
When a neighbour needs a lift, my father stops and gives him a lift.when 状语 · give sb a lift

¶ 邻里故事

句子要点语法点
Our neighbour always tells jokes, and we nod and laugh.tell jokes · nod and laugh
Once, my phone was dead, and I almost lost my grandma's note.dead = 没电/死的 · almost 副词

¶ 结束语

句子要点语法点
Love your home and love your family. Home is not a place; it is a feeling.祈使句 · 分号 独立分句
