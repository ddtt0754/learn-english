一全文逐句语法解读

以下按段落逐句拆解，标注核心语法点与句式结构（本文为生活科普演讲，重点为一般疑问句、反意疑问句与情态动词 can，并用祈使句给建议）。

¶ 1 · 开场

句子语法点
Good morning, my dear teachers and friends!问候语 · dear 形容词 修饰 teachers / friends
Today, my topic is Food and Plants Around Us.主系表 · topic 主语 · 演讲题目（斜体专有标题）

¶ 2 · 美味的食物

句子语法点
Are you hungry?一般疑问句 · Are + 主语 + 形容词（be 动词提前）
Let me tell you about my favourite food.祈使句 Let me + 动词原形 · tell sb about sth 告诉某人某事
I like beef noodles and dumplings.主谓宾 · like 喜欢 · and 并列 两食物名词
After dinner, I have a little ice cream and a cup of tea.After dinner 时间状语 · a little + 不可数名词 一点 · a cup of 一杯
Sometimes we eat hamburgers, too.Sometimes 频率副词 · too 副词 也（句末）
Mrs Wang, our cook, makes great food.主谓宾 · makes 三单 · our cook 同位语 说明 Mrs Wang
She once made a coconut soup for us.一般过去时 once（曾经）标志 · made（make 过去式）· for us 介词短语
Food is interesting, is not it?反意疑问句（口语全写形式，= isn't it）· 前肯后否

¶ 3 · 植物小知识

句子语法点
Plants grow in a different way from us.主谓宾 · grow 生长 · in a different way from 以不同于……的方式
A plant starts from a seed.主谓宾 · starts 三单 · start from 从……开始
It has a root in the soil and a stem above the ground.主谓宾 · has 三单 · in the soil / above the ground 介词短语
Do not pull the stem too hard.祈使句否定 Do not + 动词原形 · too hard 副词短语 修饰 pull
We can pull up a carrot and see its round body.情态动词 can + 动词原形 × 2 · pull up 拔出 · its 物主代词 它的
By the lake, we can see lotus flowers in summer.By the lake 地点状语 · in summer 时间状语 · can see 能看见
Plants make our world beautiful.make + 宾语 + 形容词 使……怎么样 · beautiful 形容词宾补

¶ 4 · 结束语

句子语法点
My friends, food gives us energy, and plants give us clean air.主谓宾+双宾 · give sb sth 给某人某物 · and 并列句
Let us love food, protect plants, and enjoy nature every day!祈使句 Let us + 动词原形 × 3 · every day 时间状语
That's all. Thank you for listening!That's = That is · Thank you for + 动名词

三语法核心总结

📊 时态与句型分布

项目出现情况典型例句
一般现在时全文主体（14+ 句）Plants grow in a different way from us.
一般过去时1 处（once）She once made a coconut soup.
情态动词 can3 处We can pull up a carrot.

演讲稿以一般现在时介绍食物与植物常识，穿插一般疑问句（Are you hungry?）、反意疑问句（is not it?）与情态动词 can，科普内容条理清晰，结尾用祈使句升华主题。

🎯 中考备考要点（四年级基础）

  • a little vs a few：a little + 不可数（ice cream）、a few + 可数复数，易错辨析点
  • 反意疑问句：is not it? 前肯后否，口语中可全写
  • make + 宾语 + 形容词：make our world beautiful
  • give sb sth：food gives us energy，双宾语结构
  • 祈使句 Let me / Let us：Let me tell you... / Let us love food...
  • 科普描述：in a different way from / above the ground，写作描述事物常用
