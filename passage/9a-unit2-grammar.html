🌟 What Inspiring People Teach Us

一全文逐句语法解读

¶ 1 · 开场

句子语法点
Good morning, my dear teachers and friends!问候语 · dear 表尊敬；friends = 同学们（演讲语境）

¶ 2 · 科学先驱：居里夫人

句子语法点
Who do you admire most?特殊疑问句 who 作宾语 · admire = 仰慕（及物动词）
Perhaps you admire scientists, doctors, or writers.一般现在时 · or 并列选择
Look at Marie Curie.祈使句 look at = 看（引出例子）
She was a pioneer in science.一般过去时 · pioneer 作表语
She made great medical discoveries…一般过去时 · made (make) 的过去式
…doctors around the world use her findings to treat terrible diseases.to treat 不定式作目的状语 · around the world 介词短语
In their spare time, doctors still visit patients and clean every wound with care.In 介词短语 作时间状语 · still 副词强调延续 · with care 方式状语
…they are always ready to operate at once.主系表 be ready to do · at once = 立刻
Every operation is a battle for life…隐喻 (metaphor) operation = battle · for life 表目的
…it takes great courage and a strong spirit to win it.it 形式主语 · to win it（真正主语） · take = 需要（非"拿"）

¶ 3 · 志愿奉献与优良品质

句子语法点
Many volunteers also devote their spare time to others.一般现在时 · devote … to = 奉献……于
They are truly selfless…主系表 · truly 副词修饰 selfless
…we should respect them from the bottom of our hearts.情态动词 should · from the bottom of one's heart = 从心底
To achieve anything in life, we must overcome all kinds of hardships.To do 不定式作目的状语 · 情态动词 must · all kinds of = 各种各样的
Remember, every achievement comes from sweat and patience…祈使句 作插入语 · comes from = 来自
…so please be patient when you face difficulties.so 结果状语 · when 时间状语从句 · be patient = 保持耐心

¶ 4 · 发明创造与心系祖国

句子语法点
Great inventors like Edison always continue their research even after failure.like 介词 = 像 · always 频度副词 · even after 递进
They work hard to invent new things…一般现在时 · to invent 目的状语 · work hard = 努力
…each invention makes our lives easier.比较级 easier（easy 比较级） · make + 宾 + adj.
Some scientists go abroad to learn more…一般现在时 · go abroad = 出国 · to learn 目的状语
…and then they come back to serve their country.come back = 回来 · to serve 目的状语
During the war, doctors saved many soldiers with medicine.During 介词短语作时间状语 · with + n. 表示方式/工具
After the war, wise leaders rebuilt their homes and shared their knowledge with everyone.After 时间状语 · rebuilt (rebuild) 过去式 · and 并列谓语 · share … with = 与……分享

¶ 5 · 结束呼吁

句子语法点
My friends, let us learn from these great people.祈使句 · learn from = 向……学习
Let us be brave, be patient, and never give up.祈使句 · be + adj. 系列祈使 · never 否定 · give up = 放弃
If we keep trying, we will surely make our dreams come true!if 条件状语从句 · 一般将来时 · make … come true = 实现

短语释义例句出处

三语法核心总结

📊 时态分布

时态出现次数典型例句
一般现在时主体doctors use / volunteers devote / we should respect
一般过去时3 处She was / She made / doctors saved / leaders rebuilt
一般将来时1 处we will surely make our dreams come true

全文以一般现在时陈述事实和普遍真理，穿插一般过去时回忆居里夫人/医生/领袖事迹，结尾用一般将来时表达号召。

📊 语法专项统计

从句类型
3 种（when 时间状语 / so 结果状语 / if 条件状语）
语态
全主动语态，无被动
非谓语
6 处 to do 作目的状语（to treat / to operate / to achieve / to invent / to learn / to serve）
情态动词
should（1 次）/ must（1 次）/ will（1 次）
特殊结构
it 形式主语 / make + 宾 + adj. / 祈使句系列
朗读时长
约 2 分钟

🎯 中考备考要点

  • it 形式主语：it takes … to do 是中考高频考点，常与 spend/take/cost/pay 辨析同出
  • make + 宾 + adj.：使役动词 + 宾语 + 形容词补足语，类似 make sb. happy / make sth. possible
  • 一般过去时：made (make) / saved / rebuilt 不规则动词过去式是听力和完形填空常考点
  • 非谓语 to do 作目的状语：与 in order to / so as to 互换成同义句
  • when 时间状语从句：注意主从句时态一致性（主将从现/主过从过）
