🔍 A Great Day Together

2026秋 · 人教PEP（2026新版）· 25 个课标单词 · 汕头 2027 中考口语演讲

一全文逐句语法解读

以下按段落逐句拆解，标注核心语法点与句式结构（本文为校园活动叙事，以一般过去时为主，含最高级、as...as 比较与will 将来时）。

¶ 1 · 开场

句子语法点
Good morning, my dear teachers and friends!问候语 · dear 形容词 修饰 teachers / friends
Today, my topic is A Great Day Together.主系表 · topic 主语 · 演讲题目（斜体专有标题）

¶ 2 · 校园庆典

句子语法点
Last week, our school had a big gala.一般过去时 had（have 不规则过去式）· Last week 时间状语
It was the most exciting day ever!主系表 · 最高级 the most exciting（多音节形容词）· ever 以往任何时候
We worked as a team.过去式 worked · as a team 作为一个团队
The students wore beautiful clothes and dressed up for the show.过去式 wore（wear 不规则）/ dressed · dress up 打扮 · for the show 介词短语
Some of them sang songs, and some read poems.过去式 sang（sing 不规则）/ read（read 不规则，音 /red/）· Some...some... 一些……另一些……
A writer came to the book fair and made a speech.过去式 came（come 不规则）/ made（make 不规则）· make a speech 发表演讲

¶ 3 · 运动会

句子语法点
In the afternoon, we had a marathon and many races.过去式 had · In the afternoon 时间状语 · and 并列 两宾语
The race began at two o'clock.过去式 began（begin 不规则）· at + 时刻
We counted down: three, two, one, go!过去式 counted · count down 倒计时 · 冒号列举
The runners ran as fast as they could, and everyone cheered.过去式 ran（run 不规则）/ cheered · as...as one could 尽可能……地
The judge watched carefully.过去式 watched · carefully 副词 修饰 watch
At last, my friend Li Ming won the race!过去式 won（win 不规则）· At last 最终 · Li Ming 同位语
The winner got a big prize.过去式 got（get 不规则）· the winner 获胜者

¶ 4 · 海报与书市

句子语法点
We also made posters.过去式 made · posters 海报
We pasted pictures on paper and put them on the wall.过去式 pasted / put（put 原形同过去式）· put sth on 把……贴/放在……上
Yesterday, I saw a notice on the board: “Book Fair this Saturday!”过去式 saw · Yesterday 时间状语 · 直接引语（通知内容）
Later, we will visit the book fair and meet our favourite writers.一般将来时 will + 动词原形 · Later 随后 · and 并列
I woke up early this morning, and I can't wait!过去式 woke（wake 不规则）· wake up 醒来 · can't wait 迫不及待

¶ 5 · 结束语

句子语法点
My friends, getting together is full of fun.动名词作主语 getting together · be full of 充满
Let us cheer for each other and win our dreams!祈使句 Let us + 动词原形 × 2 · cheer for 为……加油
That's all. Thank you for listening!That's = That is · Thank you for + 动名词

三语法核心总结

📊 时态与句型分布

项目出现情况典型例句
一般过去时叙事主体（18+ 处）Our school had a big gala.
最高级1 处（the most exciting）It was the most exciting day ever!
一般将来时 will1 处Later, we will visit the book fair.

本文为校园活动叙事：以一般过去时回顾庆典与运动会（不规则过去式丰富），用最高级（the most exciting）突出情感，结尾will 将来时（will visit）展望书市活动，最后以祈使句点题。

🎯 中考备考要点（四年级基础）

  • 不规则过去式：wore / sang / began / ran / won / woke 等 11 组，六年级核心词汇
  • 最高级 the most：多音节形容词用 the most + 原级，the most exciting
  • as...as one could：as fast as they could，尽可能……地
  • 动名词作主语：getting together is full of fun
  • will + 动词原形：Later, we will visit the book fair
  • can't wait：口语高频，表示迫不及待
