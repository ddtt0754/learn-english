¶ 2 · 现代发明与生活

句子语法点
I suppose that you know these things well: …that 宾语从句 · suppose = 认为 · 冒号 引出列举
A tiny mini- fan can cool you, and a small button can start a machine by itself.情态动词 can · and 并列句 · by itself = 自己/自动
Super ideas come from smart people all over the world.一般现在时 · come from · all over the world
Workers produce plastic files and printer parts in big workshops.一般现在时 · in workshops 地点状语
They complete hundreds of boxes every day.一般现在时 · hundreds of = 成百上千 · every day
A high-speed train has many wheels and runs rapidly.high-speed 复合形容词 · has / runs 并列谓语 · rapidly 副词
Frozen food can melt in a hot summer, so we keep it in cool boxes.情态 can · so 结果状语 · keep + 宾 + 介词短语
People invent new things all the time, and some tools are expensive, so not everyone can afford them.and 并列 · so 结果状语 · afford = 买得起
In reality, we are supposed to never give up; we should learn to put every idea to good use.In reality · be supposed to = 应当 · 分号 · put … to good use

¶ 3 · 造纸术：古代中国的智慧

句子语法点
Papermaking was born in ancient China, and its birth changed history.被动语态 was born · and 并列 · in ancient China
Later on, printing gradually spread widely around the world.Later on = 后来 · gradually / widely 副词 · around the world
Craftsmen mixed plant fibers like jute with liquid to make paper layers.一般过去时 · like 介词 = 例如 · to make 目的
They pressed the wet paper and shaped it into flat sheets.一般过去时 · shape … into = 把……塑造成 · and 并列
Some paper was sticky and rough, but it was cheap and suitable for daily use.主系表 × 2 · but 转折 · suitable for
The rapid growth of production led to many new jobs.一般过去时 · led to = 导致（lead to 过去式）
An officer was responsible for the whole workshop, and he cared about every piece of paper.主系表 · be responsible for · care about
His ideal was to make the best paper in the country.to make 不定式作表语 · in the country
None of the workers were lazy; they were all certain that their work was valuable.none of + 复数名词 · 分号 · that 宾语从句
Bronze and stone tools were used in early times, and many kinds of ware were made by hand.被动语态 × 2 · in early times · by hand
This creation was a real miracle of ideas.主系表 · of + n. 表示属性
Good ideas can result in great changes and speed up the progress of society.情态 can · result in · speed up · and 并列

¶ 4 · 现代生活新图景

句子语法点
Today, we can travel through a long tunnel under the mountain…情态 can · through + 地点 · under + 地点
…a pipe brings fresh water to every home.一般现在时 · bring … to
Tourism has become a big industry, and many companies are looking to do more business.现在完成时 · and 并列 · 现在进行时 looking to do = 力求做
We can fly a glider in the sky, and the dream of flying has become a reality.情态 can · in the sky · 现在完成时
Students of Arabic learn a beautiful language, and they gain a new window onto the world.一般现在时 · gain a window onto = 获得通向……的窗口（隐喻）
We should be responsible for our own future and for the world around us.情态 should · be responsible for · and 并列两个 for 短语

句子语法点

三语法核心总结

📊 时态分布

时态出现次数典型例句
一般现在时主体suppose / afford / brings / are looking
一般过去时8 处（¶3 造纸历史）was born / mixed / pressed / led to / was / were used / was
现在完成时2 处has become (×2)
一般将来时1 处they will grow

¶2 用一般现在时讲当代发明，¶3 切换一般过去时讲古代造纸史（叙事段落典型时态转换），¶4 用完成时讲现代成果。

📊 语法专项统计

从句类型
3 种（that 宾语从句 / so 结果 / that 形容词补足从句）
语态
3 处被动（was born / were used / were made）——Unit 5 被动语态最丰富
非谓语
4 处 to do（目的/表语），无动名词复杂用法
情态动词
can（6 次）/ should（2 次）
修辞
like seeds 比喻 / window onto the world 隐喻
朗读时长
约 2.5 分钟

🎯 中考备考要点

  • 被动语态：was born / were used / were made → 中考时态+语态混合考点（一般过去时被动 = was/were + p.p.）
  • lead to / result in：两个"导致"近义词辨析——lead to 偏中性因果 / result in 偏结果导向
  • 情态动词被动：可转换为主动形式练习——were used → people used them
  • hundreds of vs five hundred：有 of 则加 s，无数词则加 s（中考常考）
  • be supposed to：= should，但语气更强烈（中考同义句转换）
