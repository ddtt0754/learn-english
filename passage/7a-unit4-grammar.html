一语法要点逐段解读

¶ 开场

句子要点语法点
Good morning… Today, my topic is My Favourite Subject.问候语

¶ 各科感受

句子要点语法点
As a student, I have many classes every day.As + 身份 作状语
My favourite subject is maths, because I am good with numbers.because 原因状语 · be good with
I also like history. In the past, people did great things.In the past 时间状语 · 一般过去时 did
Some students think maths is boring, but I find it useful.but 转折 · find + 宾 + adj.

¶ 英语课

句子要点语法点
My English teacher is excellent.主系表 · excellent = 优秀的
She can help me with my English every term.情态 can · help … with · every term
She says, Remember, English is useful, but learning can be exciting!直接引语 · but 转折
She listens to us carefully, and her classes are never boring.listen to · carefully 副词

¶ 晨间 & 结束

句子要点语法点
I wake up early and get up at six o'clock every morning.wake up / get up 晨间动词
I am good at maths, and I do my homework carefully.be good at · carefully 副词
Choose a subject you love and learn it well. Every subject is a window to a new world!祈使句 + and · 定语从句 (that) you love · window 隐喻

三语法总结

时态

时态次数典型例句
一般现在时主体like / is / helps / sounds
一般过去时1 处people did great things

七年级上开始接触一般将来时（will + 动原）。重点：because 原因状语从句（because vs so 不能同时使用）。

统计

时态
2 种
从句
because(1)/定语(1)
重点
find+宾+adj. / because / wake up vs get up

备考要点

  • because vs so：because 和 so 不能同时使用同一个句子——中考必考
  • find + 宾 + adj.：I find it useful = I think it is useful
  • wake up vs get up：wake up（醒来）vs get up（起床）——中考辨析
