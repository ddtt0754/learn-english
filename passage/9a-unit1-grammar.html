一全文逐句语法解读

以下按段落逐句拆解，标注核心语法点与句式结构。

¶ 1 · 开场

句子语法点
Good morning, everyone.问候语 · everyone 作呼语
Our world is changing every day.现在进行时 表持续变化 · every day 时间状语
Let me share a story with you.祈使句 Let + 宾 + 动原 · share … with 搭配

¶ 2 · 家乡环境

句子语法点
…a sandstorm sometimes sweeps through.一般现在时 + 频率副词 · sweeps through 不及物短语
The land is sandy, and the soil is poor.主系表 × 2 · and 并列句
We have a shortage of clean water…have = 存在/有 · a shortage of 固定名词短语
so our farmland is shrinking.so 结果状语从句 · 现在进行时
We never lack hope…lack 作及物动词 · never 半否定副词
…but a bush can hardly grow…but 转折 · 情态动词 can + hardly = 几乎不能
…even a tree's root is hard to find.名词所有格 · be + adj. + to do 结构
The old road is rough and narrow.主系表 + and 并列表语

¶ 3 · 政府帮扶

句子语法点
…government steps in to help.steps in 不及物短语 · to help 不定式作目的状语
They have built greenhouses to grow corn.现在完成时 · to grow 目的状语
They have opened a wide highway…现在完成时（已完成，结果影响现在）
The hills are covered with green trees.被动语态（一般现在时）· covered with = 被……覆盖
These efforts bring about a better life…bring about 及物短语 = 带来
…they ease the burden on farmers.ease + 宾 · burden on sb. 搭配
…a villager lives in a bright flat with safe housing.一般现在时 · with 伴随状语
We are satisfied with our…town.主系表 · be satisfied with 形容词短语

¶ 4 · 科技帮助

句子语法点
Technology also helps us.also 频度副词置实义动词前
We use e-payment and many electronic tools.and 并列宾语
They are convenient and high-tech.主系表 + and 并列表语
We can link to the world through the internet…情态动词 can · through = 通过
…and listen to audio books easily.listen to 固定搭配 · easily 副词
Man-made satellites support our studies…复合形容词 n. + p.p. · 一般现在时
…and they help us greatly.greatly 程度副词修饰 help

¶ 5 · 蒙巴萨故事

句子语法点
Let me tell you a story from Mombasa…祈使句 · from 表来源
…a busy port in Kenya.同位语（解释 Mombasa）
A young Kenyan, a truck manager, runs his business…同位语 插入主谓之间
He sends each product by truck to the capital.by + 交通工具 · to + 目的地
…railway connects the port to the city.connect A to B 固定搭配
This is a real miracle.主系表
The attraction of this new life is strong…of 介词短语作后置定语
…it brings real benefit to people.bring benefit to = 给……带来益处
Our socialist friends lead a happy life.lead a … life = 过……的生活
Even the dust on the old path is gone.on 介词短语作定语 · be gone 系表结构

¶ 6 · 总结升华

句子语法点
The Olympic Games bring nations together.bring … together = 把……凝聚
As junior students, we hold a discussion on…As + 身份作状语 · hold a discussion on 搭配
In summary, our world is changing for the better.现在进行时 · in summary / for the better
Let us welcome the change with a smile.祈使句 · with a smile 方式状语

三语法核心总结

📊 时态分布

时态出现次数典型例句
一般现在时20+The land is sandy. / Technology helps us.
现在进行时3is changing / is shrinking / is changing
现在完成时2have built / have opened

演讲稿以一般现在时为主体（表达事实/状态/习惯），用现在进行时强调持续变化，用现在完成时强调已完成的建设成果。

📊 语法专项统计

从句类型
2 种（so 结果状语 + but 转折）
语态
1 处被动语态（are covered with）
非谓语
2 处 to do（目的状语 + 形容词补足语）
情态动词
can（3 次）/ Let 祈使（3 次）
特殊结构
同位语（2 处）/ 名词所有格（1 处）/ be gone（1 处）
朗读时长
约 2~2.5 分钟，节奏均匀

🎯 中考备考要点

  • 现在完成时 vs 一般过去时：本文 have built / have opened 是中考高频考点，区分"已完成且影响现在"与"单纯过去动作"
  • 被动语态：are covered with → 转换为主动语态：Green trees cover the hills.
  • 非谓语：to help / to grow 作目的状语，与 for / in order to 互换成同义句
  • 固定搭配：bring about / satisfied with / covered with / lead a … life 等是完形填空和写作高频词
  • 同位语：a busy port / a truck manager → 写作中可替换定语从句，使句式更简洁
