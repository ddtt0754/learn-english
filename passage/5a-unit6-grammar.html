一全文逐句语法解读

以下按段落逐句拆解，标注核心语法点与句式结构（本文为自然天气演讲，重点为There be 句型、when / before 时间状语从句与情态动词 must / should）。

¶ 1 · 开场

句子语法点
Good morning, my dear teachers and friends!问候语 · dear 形容词 修饰 teachers / friends
Today, my topic is Nature and Weather.主系表 · topic 主语 · 演讲题目（斜体专有标题）

¶ 2 · 美丽的自然

句子语法点
Nature is beautiful.主系表 · beautiful 形容词 表语
There are rivers, forests and high mountains.There be 存在句 · there are + 复数 · and 并列 三名词
I love to go hiking in the mountains.主谓宾 · love to do · go hiking 去徒步 · in the mountains 地点状语
I can see a waterfall and hear the wind.情态动词 can + 动词原形 × 2 · and 并列 see / hear
Last summer, we went on a trip to Huangshan, a famous mountain.一般过去时 went（go 过去式）· go on a trip 去旅行 · a famous mountain 同位语
We stayed in a hotel and had great fun.一般过去时 stayed / had · have great fun 玩得开心

¶ 3 · 天气与安全

句子语法点
But the weather can be dangerous.情态动词 can + be · dangerous 形容词 危险的 · but 转折
When it rains heavily, we must close the windows and bring an umbrella or a raincoat.when 时间状语从句 · rains heavily 副词 修饰动词 · 情态动词 must + 原形 × 2 · or 选择并列
When there is fog, we cannot see well.when 时间状语从句 + There be · cannot 情态动词否定 · well 副词
A flood or a fire is very serious.主系表 · or 选择并列 两名词 · serious 形容词 严重的
Before we go out, we should know the weather and bring what we need.before 时间状语从句 · 情态动词 should + 原形 · what 引导宾语从句 what we need

¶ 4 · 结束语

句子语法点
On a fine day, do not worry — go out and enjoy the sunshine!祈使句否定 Do not + 原形 · 破折号补充建议 · enjoy the sunshine 动宾短语
My friends, nature gives us so much.give sb sth 双宾语 · so much 这么多
Let us enjoy it, but always be careful with the weather!祈使句 Let us + 原形 · but 转折 · be careful with 小心/注意

三语法核心总结

📊 时态与句型分布

项目出现情况典型例句
一般现在时全文主体（12+ 句）Nature is beautiful.
一般过去时叙事段（went / stayed / had）Last summer, we went on a trip.
情态动词can / must / should / cannot 共 5 处We must close the windows.

演讲稿以一般现在时介绍自然之美与天气安全常识，用There be 句型列举景物、when / before 时间状语从句说明应对措施，情态动词（can / must / should）贯穿全文，是五年级语法综合运用。

📊 语法专项统计

情态动词
can / must / should / cannot 共 5 处
从句
when 2 处 / before 1 处 / what 宾语从句 1 处
There be
2 处（are rivers / is fog）
一般过去时
went / stayed / had（Last summer 叙事）
固定搭配
go on a trip / have great fun / be careful with 等 6 组
朗读时长
约 1.5~2 分钟

🎯 中考备考要点（四年级基础）

  • 情态动词辨析：must（必须）/ should（应该）/ can（能），语气由强到弱
  • when / before 从句：时间状语从句，主句用一般现在时即可（本文无将来时）
  • what 宾语从句：bring what we need，从句作宾语
  • 副词修饰动词：rains heavily，不能用 heavy
  • there be 就近原则：there is fog（单数）/ there are rivers（复数）
  • 同位语：Huangshan, a famous mountain，解释说明
