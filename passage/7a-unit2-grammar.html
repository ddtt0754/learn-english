¶ 开场

句子要点语法点
Good morning… Today, my topic is My Family, My Treasure.问候语

¶ 介绍家人

句子要点语法点
Here is a photo of my family. There are six people in it.Here is 倒装 · a photo of
What does family mean? To me, it means love.特殊疑问句 · mean = 意思是
My grandfather is my grandmother's husband.'s 所有格 · husband / wife 家庭称谓

¶ 祖父母

句子要点语法点
My grandparents are really funny.主系表 · really 程度副词
My grandfather spends a lot of time fishing.spend time doing · a lot of
He is good at playing Chinese chess.be good at doing · Chinese chess
He often says, Playing chess can make you clever.直接引语 · make + 宾 + adj.

¶ 父母

句子要点语法点
My father is a handsome man with big eyes.with + n. 伴随
He plays ping-pong with a ping-pong bat, and he plays football, too.play + 球类（无 the）
My mother is kind and always helps me with my homework.help … with · always 频度副词
My parents are different: my father is quiet, but my mother often laughs.but 转折

¶ 姐姐和我

句子要点语法点
My sister plays the violin very well.play the + 乐器 · well 副词
When I hurt my knee, she helps me.when 时间状语从句
She is only one year older than me, so I am the youngest in my family.比较级 older than · so 结果 · the youngest 最高级

¶ 家庭活动

句子要点语法点
On weekends, we do fun activities together.On weekends · go + v-ing
We spend a lot of time together and have fun every day.spend time · have fun

¶ 结束语

句子要点语法点
A happy family is the greatest treasure.最高级 the greatest
Let us love our families, help each other, and stay happy together!祈使句 + 并列 x3

三语法总结

时态

时态次数典型例句
一般现在时主体spends / is / helps / says
一般过去时1 处hurt my knee

以一般现在时为主（描述家庭），少量一般过去时（叙事）。重点：'s 所有格、频度副词位置、祈使句。

备考要点

  • 's 所有格：grandmother's husband——七年级上必考（'s vs s'）
  • play 用法辨析：play football（无 the） vs play the violin（有 the）——中考常考
  • 祈使句：Let's / Be careful / Look at——七年级上核心句式
