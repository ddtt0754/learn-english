一全文逐句语法解读

以下按段落逐句拆解，标注核心语法点与句式结构（本文为环保能源演讲，重点为情态动词 must / should / may、if 条件句与keep + 宾语 + 形容词）。

¶ 1 · 开场

句子语法点
Good morning, my dear teachers and friends!问候语 · dear 形容词 修饰 teachers / friends
Today, my topic is Energy, Nature and Us.主系表 · topic 主语 · 演讲题目（斜体专有标题）

¶ 2 · 能源种类

句子语法点
Energy is all around us.主系表 · all around us 在我们周围
There are many types of energy: solar energy from the sun, electricity from power stations, and heat from the sun.There be · many types of 许多种类 · 冒号列举三类能源
The sun is a great source of energy.主系表 · a source of ……的来源
It can light our homes and cool our rooms.情态动词 can + 动词原形 × 2 · light 照亮 / cool 使变凉（动词化）
Solar panels use the sun's power to run machines.主谓宾 · 名词所有格 the sun's · use sth to do 用……做

¶ 3 · 节约能源

句子语法点
We must not waste resources.情态动词 must not（禁止）+ 动词原形 · waste 浪费
If we use too much energy, the resources may run out one day.if 条件状语从句 · too much + 不可数名词 · 情态动词 may · run out 用完
We should reduce the use of electricity.情态动词 should（建议）+ 原形 · the use of ……的使用
Here are some quick tips: turn off the lights, unplug the chargers, and take a short shower.Here are 倒装句 · 冒号列举三个祈使句 · turn off 关闭
These small actions can make a big difference.情态动词 can · make a difference 带来改变
A few minutes of care can change our future.A few + 可数名词复数 · 情态动词 can · change 改变

¶ 4 · 结束语

句子语法点
My friends, nature gives us energy, and we must protect nature.give sb sth 双宾语 · and 并列句 · 情态动词 must + 原形
Let us save energy and keep our planet green!祈使句 Let us + 原形 × 2 · keep + 宾语 + 形容词 保持……怎么样
That's all. Thank you for listening!That's = That is · Thank you for + 动名词

三语法核心总结

📊 时态与句型分布

项目出现情况典型例句
一般现在时全文主体（12+ 句）Energy is all around us.
情态动词 must/should/may/can6 处We must not waste resources.
if 条件句1 处If we use too much energy, the resources may run out.

演讲稿以一般现在时介绍能源种类与节约方法，情态动词密集出现（must 必须 / should 应该 / may 可能 / can 能），if 条件句说明后果，结尾用祈使句呼吁「节能护绿」，主题环保。

朗读时长

🎯 中考备考要点（四年级基础）

  • 情态动词否定：must not（禁止）≠ needn't（不必），辨析考点
  • if 条件句：If we use too much energy, the resources may run out
  • too much vs too many：too much + 不可数（energy）
  • make a big difference：带来巨大不同，写作升华常用
  • keep + 宾语 + 形容词：keep our planet green
  • Here are 倒装：Here are some quick tips，地点副词开头倒装
