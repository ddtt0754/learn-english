¶ 开场

¶ 早晨日常

句子要点语法点
I usually get up at a quarter past six.usually 频度副词 · get up · 时间表达
I take a shower, get dressed, and brush my teeth.take a shower / get dressed / brush teeth 晨间三部曲
I make use of my morning time to read.make use of = 利用 · to read 目的状语
I am on duty this week, so it is my duty to arrive early.on duty · so 结果 · it is ... to do 形式主语

¶ 校园生活

句子要点语法点
At school, I have classes in the morning.have classes
As a school reporter, I also write about our school life.As + 身份 · write about
Around noon, I have lunch in the dining hall.around noon · have lunch

¶ 芬兰朋友

句子要点语法点
My Finnish friend Sam lives in the north. In winter, it is dark outside for most of the day.一般现在时 · in the north · for most of
He plays ice hockey and stays active.一般现在时 · stays active = 保持活跃

¶ 结束语

句子要点语法点
A good daily routine makes a good life.make + 宾 · daily routine
Use every minute well, and every day will become beautiful!祈使句 + and 结果 · 一般将来时 will

三语法总结

时态

时态次数典型例句
一般现在时主体get up / have classes / lives
一般将来时1 处will become

全篇一般现在时（日常行为）配合频度副词（usually/never）。重点：时刻表达法、形式主语 it is ... to do。

统计

备考要点

  • 时刻表达：a quarter past six / at about twelve——中考听力常考
  • 频度副词位置：实义动词前（usually get up），be 后（is always）
  • 祈使句 + and：Use every minute well, and... = If 条件句同义
