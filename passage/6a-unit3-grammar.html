🔍 A Healthy Life

六年级上册 Unit 3 · 语法精读与固定搭配分类

2026秋 · 人教PEP（2026新版）· 20 个课标单词 · 汕头 2027 中考口语演讲

一全文逐句语法解读

以下按段落逐句拆解，标注核心语法点与句式结构（本文为生病康复叙事，以一般过去时为主，含情态动词 may / could / will 与直接引语）。

¶ 1 · 开场

句子语法点
Good morning, my dear teachers and friends!问候语 · dear 形容词 修饰 teachers / friends
Today, my topic is A Healthy Life.主系表 · topic 主语 · 演讲题目（斜体专有标题）

¶ 2 · 生病的一天（一般过去时叙事）

句子语法点
Last week, I had a bad cold.一般过去时 had · have a cold 感冒 · Last week 时间状语
My head hurt, and I had a runny nose and a fever.过去式 hurt（hurt 原形同过去式）/ had · have a runny nose 流鼻涕 · and 并列
I coughed all day.过去式 coughed（规则 -ed）· all day 整天
I felt unhappy and ill.过去式 felt（feel 不规则）· unhappy and ill 形容词并列
I cried because I couldn't go out to play.过去式 cried · because 原因状语从句 · couldn't（can 的过去式否定）· go out 外出
My mother called the doctor, and I drank a glass of warm water.过去式 called / drank（drink 不规则）· a glass of 一杯 · and 并列

¶ 3 · 看护与康复

句子语法点
The doctor said, “You may feel better soon. Do not stay up late.”直接引语 · 情态动词 may 也许 · 祈使句否定 Do not... · stay up 熬夜
“Have a healthy diet.” I discussed my health with my mum.祈使句 Have + 名词 · 过去式 discussed · discuss sth with sb 与某人讨论某事
She made another plan for me: eat more fruit, exercise every day, and watch less video.过去式 made · another 形容词 另一个 · 冒号列举建议（祈使句省略主语）· more / less 比较级
She added, “You will get better in a few days.”直接引语 · 一般将来时 will + 动词原形 · in a few days 几天后
My teacher emailed me about the lessons, and my friends called me to cheer me up.过去式 emailed / called · 不定式表目的 to cheer me up · cheer sb up 使某人振作

¶ 4 · 结束语

句子语法点
My friends, health is the most important thing.主系表 · 最高级 the most important · thing 名词
Take care of yourself, and you will feel better every day!祈使句 + and + will · take care of 照顾 · yourself 反身代词
That's all. Thank you for listening!That's = That is · Thank you for + 动名词

短语释义例句出处

三语法核心总结

📊 时态与句型分布

项目出现情况典型例句
一般过去时叙事主体（14+ 处）Last week, I had a bad cold.
情态动词may / couldn't / will 共 3 处You may feel better soon.
一般将来时 will2 处You will get better in a few days.

本文为生病康复叙事：以一般过去时讲述感冒经历（had / hurt / coughed / felt / cried / drank），穿插直接引语转述医嘱，结尾用will 将来时与祈使句表达健康祝福。

🎯 中考备考要点（四年级基础）

  • have + 病症：have a cold / have a fever / have a runny nose，生病表达核心
  • couldn't：can 的过去式否定，I couldn't go out
  • more / less + 名词：eat more fruit / watch less video，比较级用法
  • 祈使句 + and + will：Take care of yourself, and you will feel better
  • cheer sb up：called me to cheer me up，固定短语
  • in a few days：与将来时连用，表「几天后」
